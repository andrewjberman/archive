**(a) The obvious retarget would have destroyed the parity window.**

quarantines the silver *prefix*; it never quarantined the *bucket*. So `ETFS_BUCKET=opal-data-assets` — the
natural way to do this TO — would have carried the `_cloudprobe/` **parity artifacts** into the monolith
mid-window. And it would not have failed loudly: the parity check would simply have stopped finding one

docstring; confirmed on live S3 — one `ETF_Holdings.csv`, not one per ticker). There is no single `{scope}`
for a raw object carrying two scopes — and the tempting fix, splitting the CSV per fund, is **parsing at
ingest** (Rule 17a): the raw feed becomes unrecoverable to satisfy a directory name. **Routed, not solved.**

Also surfaced a **third** bronze writer the TO never named (`msn/`, the fallback tier) — no home in the new
grammar, so it would have landed somewhere undefined *on the first day a primary breaks*.

CTO ruled all three my way (PRD v1.3.3→v1.3.6): bronze scope = **the vendor's delivery scope**; msn homed
now; and all three `{feed}` tokens I had to invent were ratified as chosen.

**I flagged the `{feed}` tokens rather than shipping them** — ruling 15 specified `{feed}` only for msn, so
three were mine. Bronze is never rewritten in place: an invented token becomes a **permanent tree** on first
write, and the "fix" would be the prohibited in-place history rewrite. Asking cost nothing (no bucket
existed yet); not asking could not be undone.

## 4. I reproduced a failure I am on record for finding
