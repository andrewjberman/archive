## Two defects of mine, both caught by AC1

**1. My wrapper's timestamp prefix blinded the close check (row `D1-S15`).**
`ai_leg_verify.read_unresolved` tests `line.startswith("UNRESOLVED ")`. My wrapper prefixed every line
with `[+   154.0s] `, so the first AC1 pass over six logs holding **387 real reason lines** read
`refused_final=0 refused_transport=0` -- a confident, well-formed, wrong verdict, with no error raised
and `rc=0`. Per log, before and after stripping: 0/0, 0→80, 0→98, 0→61, 0→145, 0→3, 0→3. e32's wrapper
did not stamp, so the defect arrived with a convenience I added between the two orders. The tell was
arithmetic: 179 unresolved keys cannot produce 0 reasons if any log parsed at all. Fixed by committing
`.stripped.log` sidecars whose only transformation is removing that prefix, keeping the timestamped
originals beside them.

**2. My exclude list denied three keys the one attempt the rule grants them.**
I built the FINAL-refusal list from "model-refused in run2 AND run3" without checking whether a LATER
run had turned a key transport. LMCSVF, SCGM1 and SWBGX were model-refused twice, then timed out in
run4 -- so their last reason was TRANSPORT, which is not final -- and my exclude then denied them the
single 1-key attempt. Two successive patches (V1, V2) each missed something; the fix was to stop
patching and derive the list by the general rule over ALL logs: exclude every absent planned key whose
LAST reason is model-stated, plus every transport key whose one attempt is spent. That yielded `to_model=3`
exactly. All three then answered model-stated, so none was owed anything further.

**And a third thing I got wrong, corrected before it was published.** Row `D1-S16`'s first draft
asserted SC-e-77's six rows were OUTSIDE the contract's `values_by_class`. That conflated a ROW's state
with a VERDICT's bound and would have put a false BLOCKING claim against another seat's act. Issuer
msg 1176 corrected it, and the contract's own text confirms the issuer: `absent` reads "a row with no
sub_asset_class stays absent", and `bound` scopes the closed list to "a DIRECT or AI VERDICT". The row
was still uncommitted, so it was rewritten rather than published wrong and amended later. What survives
is narrower and about the GRADER: `ai_leg_verify`'s landed predicate is stricter than the contract, so
a sibling act writing legal rows over a graded act's planned keys can hold a grade open, and the act
graded second always loses. Filed `DISMISSED_WITH_REASON` -- and my first attempt used an invented
disposition `RESOLVED_BY_RULING`, which the row gate refused, correctly: Rule 24 says an enum proposal
arrives with its four-postulate record or it is not a proposal, and I had neither.
