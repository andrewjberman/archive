state grounds WHAT (the symptom); only SOURCE + the dependency graph grounds WHY. Every cycle's
mechanism cites `file:line @ commit-ish` or a comp-tool call with its output. **"proof / provably /
root cause / definitively / source-confirmed" are RESERVED WORDS** — legal only on a source-confirmed
claim.

defect — that is a semantics act, not a cleanup, and it does not happen at the seat.
