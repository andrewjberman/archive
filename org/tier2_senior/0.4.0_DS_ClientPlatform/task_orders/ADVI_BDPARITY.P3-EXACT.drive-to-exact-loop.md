## THE LOOP, AND ITS ONE NON-NEGOTIABLE STEP
Each cycle: **largest class -> NAMED MECHANISM -> fix-or-registered-convention -> re-census.**

**THE MECHANISM STEP IS NOT OPTIONAL AND NOT A FORMALITY.** A causal claim is a HYPOTHESIS until
source/graph-confirmed, and **a hypothesis cannot authorize a fix or a convention change.** Live
state grounds WHAT (the symptom); only SOURCE + the dependency graph grounds WHY. Every cycle's
mechanism cites `file:line @ commit-ish` or a comp-tool call with its output. **"proof / provably /
root cause / definitively / source-confirmed" are RESERVED WORDS** — legal only on a source-confirmed
claim.

**A CONVENTION CHANGE IS A CTO GATE.** Registering a convention is how a difference stops being a
defect — that is a semantics act, not a cleanup, and it does not happen at the seat.

carried without its list is an unfalsifiable claim.
