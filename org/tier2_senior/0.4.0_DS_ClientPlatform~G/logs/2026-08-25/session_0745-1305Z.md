   I typed the `Z` myself. The law names that exact trap. UTC-pinned: 12:45:41Z.
5. **Estimated the time to the fire between turns**, twice, drifting ~20 minutes. My own
   memory note says read the clock.
6. **Said "11 commits" in an echo without counting.** It was 25.

**The shape:** four of the six were caught by a control or a second read, none by care.
And the two that mattered most (1 and 2) were both **instrument** defects that would have
