### ONE write, not two -- and the mistake that made it a question

`safe-apply` was invoked TWICE. The first invocation's output was piped into `head -30`, which
closed the pipe early, so whether the apply ran was **not knowable from that output**. On a
WORLD-risk key-policy edit the number of writes that landed has to be measured, so it was, twice
over:

1. a live `get-key-policy` BETWEEN the two invocations read **5** statements with the new Sid
   absent -- the first invocation wrote nothing;
2. CloudTrail `lookup-events` for `PutKeyPolicy` from 16:30:00Z returned a **denominator of 1
   event**, at **16:44:04Z**, carrying 6 statements, with an errorCode census of `{none: 1}`.

So exactly one key-policy write landed. **Lesson taken: do not pipe a `safe-apply` into `head`.**
The log of the second invocation was captured to a file and read from both ends instead. (The 4
`MalformedPolicyDocumentException` retries that preceded the s21 and a18 applies are absent here,
which fits their explanation: no just-created principal for IAM to propagate.)
