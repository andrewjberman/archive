<objective>
The INTAKE side of the living schema: every error surfacing in the fabric -- a telemetry FAIL (with
E4's three meanings separated), a gate denial, a CVO/verification finding, a postmortem entry --
carries a MECHANISM CLASSIFICATION **at occurrence**, cheap, while the context still exists.

**Never archaeology later.** The ~34,000-documents-of-evidence vs 14-of-doctrine ratio is exactly what
classify-at-intake closes: the evidence exists, but the context that would let anyone classify it is
gone by the time anyone looks.

On top of intake, the ARCHETYPE WEIGHT + DYNAMICS REPORT: the standing per-window distribution of
error mass by mechanism class -- which archetypes carry the weight, which grow, which shrink, the
N-SELF rate, the D1-vs-D4 catch share.

First artifact when this starts: ONE intake point classifying at occurrence. Not the dynamics report,
not the schema for all four sources. One source, classified at the moment it happens, cheaply.
</objective>

<constraints>
1. **CLASSIFY AT OCCURRENCE OR NOT AT ALL.** The entire value is the timing. A classification added
   later is archaeology and the PRD forbids it by design, not by preference.
2. **THIS IS A STEERING SIGNAL, NOT A NOTIFICATION SYSTEM.** Standing moratorium binds (Andrew-direct
   2026-07-28): no alarms, no SNS, no SES, no monitors. A growing archetype becomes a ranked demand
   signal into WS-C3's pattern inventory and WS-C4's synthesis queue -- it becomes WORK, routed to an
   owner. It does not become a page. **A class no gate is absorbing is a STANDING FINDING WITH AN
   OWNER**, which is the TRIGGER_LAYER form, not an alert.
3. **DETERMINISTIC-FIRST.** Classification is cheap and mechanical at intake; do not design a system
   that requires judgment at the moment of occurrence, because the moment of occurrence is exactly
   when nobody has time to exercise judgment.
4. Do not start before P5. Its inputs (E4 meanings, E5 boundary reports, E6 conversion measurement,
   E3 gate denials) are the population it classifies -- starting early means classifying a population
   that does not exist yet, and inventing its shape from imagination.
5. No emojis / non-ASCII anywhere.
</constraints>
