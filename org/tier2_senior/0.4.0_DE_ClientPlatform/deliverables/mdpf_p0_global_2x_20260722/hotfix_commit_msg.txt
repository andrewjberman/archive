fix(ModelPortfolios): unique monthly x-axis category key -- fixes the Global "2x" mis-read (P0, live on PROD)

The monthly Period-End x-axis bound to `period`, a toLocaleDateString label that OMITS
the year, while the GLOBAL view alone renders the full since-inception window (21 month-end
bars) and per-firm views keep a rolling 1-year window (~13). Global therefore produced
DUPLICATE recharts category keys -- "Mar 31" and "Apr 30" each appear for 2025 AND 2026 on
the live legacy data. Duplicate categories break the bar<->row binding: recharts resolves
the tooltip to the FIRST row carrying that category, so hovering the 2026 bar reports the
2025 row's date AND values while the bar renders at its true 2026 height.
