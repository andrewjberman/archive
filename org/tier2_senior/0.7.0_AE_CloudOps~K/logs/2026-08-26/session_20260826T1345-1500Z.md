⛔ **THE TYPO TRAP, HANDLED:** `simulate-principal-policy` does not validate action names — a
nonexistent action returns `implicitDeny` byte-identical to a missing grant. Rows 6–8 are therefore
**not** self-sufficient. `s3:PutObject` is proven a real action by rows 1–2 returning `allowed` in
separate calls; and the `s3:GetObject` absence in row 8 rests on the **policy enumeration**
(`get-role-policy` across all three inline policies shows **no `s3:GetObject` statement at all**),
which is instrument-independent of the simulator.

**What worked:** the A.3 probe in its corrected form. The old one-line version tested
`command -v comp-which` — the member most likely to be provisioned — and could not fail in the state
it exists to detect. The EXERCISE half is what makes it a control.

**What failed:** `proof-of-spec-check.py`. Details below.

**Gap found — `proof-of-spec-check.py:271` builds `needle = 'id="' + cid + '"'`, the XML ATTRIBUTE
form, while many TOs declare `<id>...</id>`, the ELEMENT form.** When the needle misses, the tool
prints `alignment: NOT EVALUATED ... nothing is claimed` and **still exits `VALID` rc=0**. A consumer
branching on rc — which is what a gate does and what a hurried certifier does — cannot distinguish
"alignment enforced and passed" from "alignment could not be checked."

⛔ **MY FIRST TWO MEASUREMENTS OF THIS GAP WERE BOTH WRONG, AND BOTH INFLATED IT.** I reported 17.7%,
then 100%. `3.0.0_SA_CVO` measured 58% (92 of 159 instances) and both of their diagnoses hold:
- **GRAIN** — I measured over TO *files*; the checker runs on *instances*. A TO with no instance
  cannot be exposed by this defect. 2112 was a well-formed count of the wrong population.
- **"0 use the attribute form" was FALSE, and the mechanism is mine to name:** my predicate required
  an `<id>` element FIRST, then asked whether that same file also carried the attribute form. Files
  with **no element** — 661 of them — were bucketed "not addressable" and **discarded**. That bucket
  is exactly where the attribute-form TOs live. **I did not measure them; I defined them out of the
  population and then reported their absence as a zero.** A discarded residue reported as a zero.

**The transferable half, and it is not "be careful":** in the same message where I caught and reported
my own wrong-predicate regex (the 17.7%), the very next number carried a *different* wrong-predicate
error. **A predicate audit run on one claim does not immunise the next one.** `3.0.0_SA_CVO` observed
the identical sequence in another seat an hour earlier.

**Workaround, no tool change:** declaring `decomp_ref.to_ref` resolves the governing TO **by path** and
bypasses the needle entirely. Both instances flipped from "announce only" to `alignment: ENFORCE`.
Routed to CVO; tool fix NOT taken by me (off-mandate).
