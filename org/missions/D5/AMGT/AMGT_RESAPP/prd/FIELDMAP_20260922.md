## 3. WHAT BREAKS FOR A GLOBAL (IEFA) UNIVERSE, the first design inputs for his list

1. **No channel carries the composite FIGI.**
   - Every record keys on the ticker-derived `security_key`, or on a search/query hash.
   - The crosswalk has no legal name, CIK, LEI or web address, so a multi-name filings run cannot resolve anyone.
   - No production Bloomberg pull asks for a FIGI, ISIN or SEDOL. The exchange FIGI (`ID_BB_GLOBAL`) is proven to return on
     this Terminal; the composite FIGI is untested. The natural home is `add_security.IDENTITY_FIELDS`.
2. **Fiscal-year pairing.** The Model block joins by CALENDAR year, so P/E, P/B, EV/EBITDA and yield sit about 9
   months off for non-December year-ends (measured on Keyence FY2025). Most of Japan is affected. The fix is written
   (`fye_join.py`) and not wired.
3. **Currency and units.**
   - Nothing converts currency. London names are in pence, and ADR/CHF/AUD mismatches are only flagged in text.
   - 11 fields have no unit map, including every forecast field.
4. **Language.**
   - The detector labels German, French and every Latin-alphabet language as "en", so no translation happens and no
     warning is given.
   - The section cutter knows only English and Japanese annual reports.
   - Publications search by English name only.
   - The translation tool is run by nothing in production, and the page drops its output anyway.
5. **Seeking Alpha covers a foreign name only through its US line (ADR or OTC)**, never the home listing, and 6861-style
   numeric tickers get nothing. An ADR map is needed first, and SA figures would be in USD at ADR scale.
6. **Officers for non-SEC names.** When the issuer-site route wins, its officer section is thrown away, so Keyence's
   officers come from Bloomberg's fallback.
