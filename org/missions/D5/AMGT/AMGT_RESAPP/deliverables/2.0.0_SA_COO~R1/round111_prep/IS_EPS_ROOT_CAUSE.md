# AMGT_RESAPP round-111 PREP -- where the raw `IS_EPS` in the P/E-not-shown footer comes from (read-only, `2.0.0_SA_COO~R1`, 2026-10-17 ~16:3xZ)

**Finding (PROD read, `deliverables/2.0.0_SA_COO~R1/round110/PROD_READ_FWD40D.md`, walker findings `UIW-20261017T145445Z-007` and `-012`):** RIO LN's and ICT PM's Key Figures footer reads `P/E not shown: price / reported EPS (IS_EPS), FY2025, is not shown: the price is in GBp and reported EPS in USD, ...`.

**Cause, read in the source:** the text is SERVED, not built by the portal. `lib/research/api/scoped_amgt.py` lines 2728-2765 (the historical-P/E derivation, at the live pin `3818736e6b` and at `1514402a12`, same blob `a75ab14fd16d`): for a fiscal year whose `PE_RATIO` cannot be computed it appends to `fiscal_unresolved` the entry `{"mnemonic": "PE_RATIO", "fiscal_year": year, "reason": "price / reported EPS (IS_EPS), FY{year}, is not shown: {why}"}` (line 2764), and `why` is one of five strings, three of which carry a mnemonic: `an input is absent (PX_LAST or IS_EPS)` (2754), `reported EPS (IS_EPS) is not positive` (2756), `the fiscal-year price (PX_LAST) is not positive` (2758); the other two (`the currency of the price or of reported EPS is not stated`, 2760; `the price is in {ccy} and reported EPS in {ccy}, and a ratio of different currencies is not shown`, 2762) carry none. The portal (`ModelTab.tsx` ~195-198, the `fiscal_unresolved` `PE_RATIO` entry for the year) prints the served `reason` verbatim after `P/E not shown: `, as round 108's `bo-1` cut it. The same file's line 2742 sets the derived figure's `basis.definition` to `price / reported EPS (IS_EPS), FY{year}` (shown in a tooltip's `definition` row, not in the footer): a second visible place for the mnemonic.

**So the repair is a SERVED-path change** (the words of two reason strings and one definition, no value), not a portal cell: a round-111 code cell on `scoped_amgt.py` (tests in `lib/research/api/tests/` pin these strings and move with it) and then a redeploy of `opal-amgt-scoped-api` under its own never-widen pair (the live package carries `scoped_amgt.py` at `a75ab14fd16d`; a release pin = the then-live pin plus that one file, the `bp-6` pattern). A portal-side strip of `(IS_EPS)` is the alternative the CTO may prefer (display only, ships with the next portal feature) but it would rewrite a served sentence in the page, which the round-108 bound `do not reword any existing sentence` forbids without a ruling; flagged, not decided.
