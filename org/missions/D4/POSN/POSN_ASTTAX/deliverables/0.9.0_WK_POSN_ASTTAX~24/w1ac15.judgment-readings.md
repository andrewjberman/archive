## Q1 -- Is precedence BY TICKER the honest reading for a page reader, where the two sources agree on `aum` and `fee` but only one carries `active` and `credit`?

**What is served, measured (the dry run and the served artifact agree byte-for-byte):**

- 25 distinct tickers. 23 resolve to `positioning_data` (Yang), 1 to `bbg` (GLD), 1 to `none` (CASH).
- On the 5 tickers BOTH sources carry (DIVZ, IDVZ, PFDE, PFOE, VXX): Yang `aum` EQUALS Bloomberg
  `FUND_TOTAL_ASSETS` to the last printed digit, 5 of 5 (e.g. VXX 515.41574645 on both sides).
  `fee` equals likewise (the CTO's 5-of-5 read at v0.9, which this cell did not re-measure for `fee`).
- The one row that actually exercises the rule is GLD: `fee` 0.4 and `aum` 147692.37654511 come from
  the Bloomberg columns; `active` and `credit` publish NULL because the pull has NO column for them;
  `yield`, `duration` and `beta` publish NULL because GLD's Bloomberg row carries NaN in those three.
- So for a page reader, GLD's Characteristics cells read: fee 0.4, aum 147692.4, and FIVE blanks.

**The reading FOR per-ticker precedence, as the artifact implements it:** a row is one source's
whole reading. Nothing in GLD's row mixes two vendors' definitions, and `source: "bbg"` is published
beside the values, so a reader who asks "where did this come from?" gets ONE answer per row rather
than one per cell. The rule is also the one the satellite AGGREGATES already use (`resolve_ticker`,
unchanged since TO-V4-1), so the per-row numbers and the weighted numbers above them cannot disagree
about provenance.

**The reading AGAINST it, stated as plainly:** per-ticker precedence is INVISIBLE where it costs
something. The two sources agree on the fields they share, so the choice only ever shows up as the
fields the winning source LACKS -- `active` and `credit` for a Bloomberg row. A reader looking at
GLD sees a blank `active` cell and cannot tell "no source carries this" from "the source we picked
does not carry it, and the other one was not asked". There is no field-level source marker; `source`
is per ROW. Per-FIELD precedence would have filled nothing extra here (Yang has no GLD row at all),
so on TODAY's data the two rules produce IDENTICAL output -- the question is about the next ticker
that only one source prices, not about this artifact.

**What the artifact does NOT say, named:** it does not record, per NULL cell, WHY the cell is null
(no column vs. a NaN in the column vs. no source at all). The three are distinguishable in the
producer's inputs and are collapsed to one `null` in the served bytes.

## Q2 -- Is serving GLD's returns from the ETF gold rule, beside Yang's windows, a difference a reader must be told on the PAGE, or only in the ARTIFACT?

**What is served, measured:** GLD's 8 windows are all non-null at `bbg_as_of 2026-09-11`
(1D 0.608, WTD -1.9668, MTD -2.3629, 1M -0.5463, QTD 8.2495, 1Q 3.2224, YTD 0.621, 1Y 18.9435),
computed by `etf_positioning_gold.compute_returns` over 47,506 history rows (16 daily partitions +
173 backfill series). The other 23 priced tickers carry Yang's own windows, copied verbatim.

**The difference, as the PRD discloses it (v0.10):** Yang compounds days strictly AFTER the window
start and uses 90 days for 1Q; the estate ETF rule INCLUDES the start day and uses 91. So in one
Holdings Breakdown panel, 23 rows answer "1Q" with one definition and 1 row answers it with another.

**The reading for ARTIFACT-ONLY disclosure:** the artifact already carries the discriminator a
reader can act on -- `source` per ticker and `method.tickers_block.returns_rule` naming the exact
function and file, plus `returns_as_of`. The magnitude is small relative to the cells' own precision
(one extra day at each end of a quarter), and a page footnote per row would be read as a warning
about DATA QUALITY when the data is fine.

**The reading for PAGE disclosure:** the page is where the two numbers sit in ONE COLUMN under ONE
heading, and a column heading is a claim that its cells are comparable. A reader comparing GLD's 1Q
to VXX's 1Q is comparing two definitions and nothing on the page says so; the artifact's `source`
field is not rendered anywhere in the Holdings Breakdown as F4 is cut. The estate's own doctrine
about disclosure ("the null share is DISCLOSED, not hidden" -- this very producer's docstring) is
about exactly this shape: a number whose basis differs from its neighbours' is honest only if the
reader can SEE the basis.

**One measured fact that bears on both readings, offered without a conclusion:** the difference is
currently confined to ONE ticker of 25, and that ticker is the only one in the satellites that no
Yang book prices. If Yang ever prices GLD, the row silently switches rule (precedence is per ticker,
resolved at each run) and the page would change basis with no event anywhere.
