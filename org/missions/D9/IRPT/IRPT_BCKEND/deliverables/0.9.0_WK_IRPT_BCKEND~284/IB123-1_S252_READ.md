# IB123-1 -- row S252 on every trading-day partition of the blackdiamond_z09 holdings source (read only)

Author `0.9.0_WK_IRPT_BCKEND~284`, 2026-10-11. For the CTO. Data: `IB123-1_s252_all_partitions.json`. Instrument: `s252_all_partitions.py`
(copied from `partition_close_anchor_sweep.py` and extended; fetch step `s252_fetch.py`). S3 reads only. No position row, account id or balance is in any file here.

## What was measured

The prefix holds 91 dated partitions, 2026-06-09 to 2026-10-09. 86 are trading days (86 of 86 in the range; the other 5 are non-trading dates).
All 86 have a day row. 85 are GRADED. 2026-06-09 is FIRST_DAY_NO_PRIOR: the source has no partition for the prior trading day, as the order defines it.
No day is UNGRADEABLE: the Schwab anchor holds every date needed (2026-06-04 to 2026-10-09).

Method as the existing sweep: price = Market Value / Units, joined to the anchor by CUSIP, tolerance 1e-4 relative. A security "moved" on D when its
anchor close differs from the prior trading day's. Each moved security is counted at the own close, the prior close, or neither.

One change from the old instrument, and it matters. The old one keeps one price per CUSIP (the last row wins). A CUSIP sits in many accounts and its rows do not
always agree. Here every priced row counts, and a security is "at the prior close" on a day if ANY of its rows is. That is the headline. The strict figure
(EVERY row at the prior close) is given beside it, because the two differ a lot. The old S252 numbers are not comparable to either.

## Stable, or changing?

Two populations.
- A core of 46 securities (24 equity, 18 fund, 4 ETF) lagged on 10 or more days; 31 securities lag on half or more of their moved days. From about 2026-08-13 the core is at the
  prior close on most days.
- 377 securities lagged on fewer than 10 days, mostly on the four large days above.

One class does not explain it. Funds are mostly lagged on EVERY row (347 of 619 fund lagged days; the rest are mixed). Equities are mostly MIXED: the same CUSIP has some rows
at the own close and some at the prior close (only 364 of 1,571 equity lagged days have every row at the prior close). A security-level rule cannot describe a CUSIP whose price
differs by row. The dimension that varies within one CUSIP is the account or portfolio. I did not read it (see below).

The first partition, 2026-06-09, is outside the totals but reads 343 of 345 moved securities at the PRIOR close, none at its own.

## Not measured

- Securities the anchor does not carry, and securities that did not move (they cannot tell an own close from a prior one).
- Why the rows lag (a pull's timing, a vendor feed, a pricing source). No log or vendor call was read.
- The class of any security beyond the derived label; any partition outside 2026-06-09..2026-10-09.
- The old S252 figures, which used a one-price-per-CUSIP rule, were not re-run.
