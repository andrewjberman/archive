### 0.4 The temporary fork

A per-firm pin is a named, temporary fork. The standard is "identical naming structure and operations per firm" (the CTO's round-198 condition 2). The fork closes by the CUSIP port's OWN release. That release brings Cottonwood and Inflection to the port after the read of the 45 absent Cottonwood sub-class values: the read (deliverables/0.9.0_WK_IRPT_BCKEND~392/IB197-4_cusip_sub_class_read.json) traces all 45 to one rule site in holdings_snapshot.py and names three options (keep_prior_sub_class, documented_correction, backfill_canonical_rows). That release's order pair names the reconvergence as its goal, and this packet does not decide among the options.

The 45 values do not ship in this release: pin 2 carries no port, so the release cannot blank them, and the closure-fix pin keeps the port for 49financial only where it is already served. Surprise row IRPT_BCKEND-D2-W392-S2 stays ARMED under its owner until the CTO rules the option. Once the port's own release lands, the per-firm pins collapse to one, and this section is superseded.
