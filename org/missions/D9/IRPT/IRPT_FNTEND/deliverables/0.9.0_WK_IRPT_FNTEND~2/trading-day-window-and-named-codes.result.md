## The rule

A start is normalised to the first trading day on or after it, and an end to the last trading day on or before it.
Two windows are the same when their normalised boundaries are equal. `MethodsAndSources.tsx` (print) and
`PeriodControls.tsx` (screen) both call `compareWindows`. A window whose dates differ only by closed days says so,
naming the calendar and the boundary. The frozen print now reads, on page 3:

> The source answered the requested window. ... The requested and answered dates differ only by days the market was
> closed (NYSE trading calendar (lib/trading_calendar.py)): requested start 2026-01-01, answered start 2026-01-02: the
> first trading day on or after either is 2026-01-02.
