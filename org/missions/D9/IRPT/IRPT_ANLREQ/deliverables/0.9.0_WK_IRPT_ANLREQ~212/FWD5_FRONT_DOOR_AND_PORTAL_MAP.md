## What a seat-driven other-firm sign-in would need on DEV (answer, from the rows above)

- Principal kinds the harness can mint: one, an internal (`opal-capital`) unscoped test-filer row (rows for fwd1_mint.py:25, :100 and :64). The helper takes no firm or email argument; a second-firm principal needs an edit of its FIRM and EMAIL constants and a row whose firmId is that firm (row for db.ts:213).
- A principal for a second firm does not exist in the harness. Whether one exists on DEV is UNREAD.
- Routes the exercise would call, each refused for a client of another firm by a row above: GET /api/scoped/tickets/{id} (row 1), GET /api/scoped/tickets (rows 5-7), GET /api/scoped/tickets/{id}/attachment (row 18), GET /api/scoped/investor-reporting/analysis/requests/{id}/pdf, .../original, .../commentary-pdf and .../analysis/{jobId}/sections (rows for owned_requests.py:176, owned_report.py:397, :349 and :133). The browser GETs are admitted by the driver guard (row for fwd1_driver.mjs:77).
- The driver has no parameter for the signed-in firm, and its package step only reads presence (row for fwd1_driver.mjs:258). An other-firm exercise therefore needs a new step that opens each package file and records the refusal.
