## Z-W13-S3 -- MX-x-2's catalog `s3_key` lines refused by the naming gate

**Ruling: do NOT extend `NAMING_LEGACY_READS.json`. The catalog stops carrying the raw key.**

- The waiver carrier cannot admit these lines by construction. `.tools/naming-gate.py` `legacy_read_ok()` parses the
  post-image as PYTHON (`ast.parse`) and authorizes ONE positional literal at ONE call site of ONE hashed read helper.
  A YAML `s3_key:` line has no call site, so an entry for it could never grade OK. The authority it would cite,
  `LEGACY_READ_AUTHORITY_MX-t-8_20260925.md`, also authorizes "only ... the portal-index integration's own read helper".
  A catalog file is not that helper.
- A catalog line that states a served address is a second hand-kept copy of the key. Rule 30 exists to prevent that
  second carrier.
- **Route:** in `DATA_CATALOG.yaml` (through its emitter) and in the three `catalog_entries/portal_mdpf_*.yaml`, the
  entry names WHERE the data is read -- the portal_index tool and its dataset id -- and carries no `s3_key` literal. The
  one waived literal stays at the one waived call site.
- **If a consumer genuinely needs the raw key** from the catalog, the worker files a row naming that consumer and its
  line. The key then reaches the catalog as `lib/opal_naming.py` output, once MDPF_CANON has moved it, or as an R20
  register entry that Andrew acknowledges. It never goes through this waiver.
