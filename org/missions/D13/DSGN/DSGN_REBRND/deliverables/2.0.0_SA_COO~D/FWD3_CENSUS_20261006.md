## Classes

- **ORDERED ONE-HUE** -- one hue, light to dark (or alpha low to high): what FWD-3 asks. CONFORMS.
- **DIVERGING** -- two hues for the two signs, magnitude by lightness or alpha. Conforms on magnitude; the hue pair encodes sign, not order. FLAGGED for a
  reading from the CTO (FWD-3's text says "one hue" and does not name a diverging scale).
- **ORDERED MULTI-HUE** -- an ordered quantity encoded by several unrelated hues. VIOLATES FWD-3 unless excepted.
- **EXCEPTED** -- by Andrew's word or by RULING R.
- **CATEGORICAL** -- identity, not order. Out of FWD-3's population, listed so the denominator is closed.

## What this says, said plainly

- **FWD-3 is not realized on the product at DEV.** Thirteen ordered quantities (V1-V13) are still encoded by several hues, and the named positive case (V1) is one of them.
- The ramp EXISTS (`--ramp-1..5`, C6) and nothing but the sentiment tokens reads it. The work is the consumers, not the ramp.
- **Two rulings are needed before any conversion, and they are not this seat's:** (1) V2, the U.S. Regime percentile bar, is the same kind of zone-meaning
  indicator as C1 and C2 but is not excepted: is it? (2) C7-C9, the diverging scales: does FWD-3's "one hue" bind a sign-diverging scale, or is the two-hue
  sign encoding allowed? A reading from `1.0.0_SA_CTO`, not an edit.
- **V1 is convertible now:** `PORTFOLIOS` carries the five hues and every consumer reads `PORTFOLIOS[i].color`; the conversion is the ramp in context
  (`getBandInkForHue`'s sibling for text; the toggle fill and the composition tile for the swatch). It is a design act with a real run per consumer.
