## Classes

- **ORDERED ONE-HUE** -- one hue, light to dark (or alpha low to high): what FWD-3 asks. CONFORMS.
- **DIVERGING** -- two hues for the two signs, magnitude by lightness or alpha. Conforms on magnitude; the hue pair encodes sign, not order. FLAGGED for a
  reading from the CTO (FWD-3's text says "one hue" and does not name a diverging scale).
- **ORDERED MULTI-HUE** -- an ordered quantity encoded by several unrelated hues. VIOLATES FWD-3 unless excepted.
- **EXCEPTED** -- by Andrew's word or by RULING R.
- **CATEGORICAL** -- identity, not order. Out of FWD-3's population, listed so the denominator is closed.
