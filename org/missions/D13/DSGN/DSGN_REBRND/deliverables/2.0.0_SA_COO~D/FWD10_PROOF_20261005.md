## Reading

- **The gradient is now the contract's, on every measured route and the sparse control.**
  - Before: inside equalled between (24 = 24), so nothing inverted.
  - After: 30px inside a panel and 20px between sections.
  - The intermediate state (DEV `56b71adb`) left one 24px panel top on /positioning and one 24px gap on /portfolio-management. Commit `3e1aa46f` closed both.
- **The explicit end state.**
  - Before: every route stopped on its last panel's edge.
  - After: every route ends 20px below its last panel on a page end naming the page and its own as-of. The sparse control ends the same way: a short page ends on purpose.
- **The fold.**
  - With populated fixtures, every route runs past 900px both before and after. So the share below the last content is never dead canvas.
  - Before it is `<main>`'s bottom padding (32px, 3.6%). After it is the 20px gap, the one-line page end and that padding (76-77px, 8.4-8.6%), i.e. the designed end.
  - PROOF grades the control on spacing and end state, not on the fold, and so does this record.
- **What this record does NOT show.**
  - An advisor's judgment that the work "fills the screen" (FWD-10 ACCEPT): that is the CVO's grade on these renders.
  - The two parked routes (/portfolios, /security-analysis).
  - Live data: the fixtures are recorded or synthetic.
