Opal portal: before and after round 1 of the rebrand

Three routes, Market Overview, Positioning and Portfolio Management, each rendered at 1440 px wide before and after round 1 of DSGN_REBRND. For putting on screen beside a competitor you choose.

Read this first: what this pack does NOT show

  • The page bodies are still mostly dark-on-dark. On the AFTER side the page background is light (#F4F6FA; the capture script printed rgb(244, 246, 250)). But almost every panel is still a full-width dark navy block with light text, and the empty and error messages sit in white boxes inside those dark panels.
  • No cell of this round inverted the pages. Your Option A (panels go light, with one deliberate dark band per page) is written into the contract, and the rule for inverting colours is committed (design/brand/inversion-rule.json). But no cell of this round applied it to the pages. portal/CLAUDE.md:44 counts 4,887 light-on-dark idioms still in src/pages/. This is recorded in surprise row DSGN_REBRND-S2.
  • So this side-by-side is not the finished rebrand. It shows this round's colour tokens, typography, controls, focus rings, density and empty/error states, on pages that have not been inverted yet.
  • No data on either side. Both sides were rendered with no test data (no fixtures), against local development servers whose data calls fail (401 Unauthorized is visible on Market Overview). So every panel shows its empty or error state, never real figures. A competitor's site will show data, and this pack cannot.
  • No competitor is in this pack. Capturing a competitor's site would be a request off Opal infrastructure, and it needs your word each time. You choose the competitor and put it beside this page yourself.

What the AFTER side carries that you asked for

  • Your five identity accents, in indigo, at the five named places and nowhere else: the page-title rule, the header subtext, the active tab's label and underline, the active sidebar item, and the avatar (portal commit a7c4fa4f, RB-s-5 Amendment 1). The pre-round tree had indigo in these same places. Round 1 retired them and Amendment 1 restored them, so they look alike on both sides of this pair.
  • The new favicon: an indigo circle carrying the white Opal mark (shown at the bottom of this page, beside the old one).
