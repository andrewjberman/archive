## Definitions used (the worker's reading of FWD-6, for the CVO to grade)

- `RECOVERS`: a missed or refused day is refilled with nobody acting once the login is good again. Two mechanisms occur and each row names which: a second attempt hung off the login chain's success (the FWD-1 way), or a catch-up window on the next fire.
- `NOT-EXPOSED because ...`: the pull cannot run on a lapsed login, because it (a) logs in or re-mints inside its own run, (b) is fired only by the mint's own success event, (c) uses no vendor session, (d) is placed after the daily mint that revalidates the login, inside the credential's lifetime, with a freshness check, or (e) its schedule is DISABLED. For (d) the residual (the mint itself failing) is stated, not hidden.
- A pull that is exposed and has neither answer is NOT put in the table with a false answer. It is listed in the section "Exposed, neither answer is true" with what recovery exists today.
