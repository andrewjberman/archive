## THE CELL -- what exists after CG-s-2, and what this cell makes binding

After TO-CG-B-2 the worker publishes an artifact + its `_build_manifest` under `projection/<vintage>/` and advances NO pointer. That is deliberately half a design: the readers (the serving service CG-s-4, the operator status read CG-s-7, the thin client's age line CG-s-6) need ONE object that says "this is the current projection", and they need to trust that whatever it names is fully present. S8 established that the manifest today carries a field (`edge_count`) whose meaning differs by producer; CG-s-2 fixes the producers, and THIS cell writes the contract every future producer and reader is bound by. **The contract binds FORWARD:** TO-CG-B-2's first artifact and manifest are written to B-2's own committed design (the runtime decision @`18503387b5`, re-stated @`799e2dad0a`; the fixes @`117e539280`); where the contract and that first manifest diverge, the contract is the spec and the conforming edit is PHASE B's (B3-AC2, after B-2's flip), or a surprise row if the divergence needs a decision rather than an edit.

Two questions the decomposition and the round-1 slug hand this cell, neither decided by any document yet: **pointer atomicity** on a bucket with versioning OFF (the pointer is the one mutable object; S3 PUT is atomic per object and read-after-write consistent, so an overwrite is a clean flip, but a reader that fetched the pointer and then the artifact can straddle a flip -- the contract says what a reader does then), and **the 2,256 duplicate edge identities in the source** (60,688 MERGE attempts, 58,432 stored; MERGE idempotency explains the DB, not the source). **And one the round added:** row S14@`6c0297289b` measured that no Pipe/SQS shape gives the ECS task SQS-backed redrive -- the Pipe acks on invocation, so a task that dies at minute 12 of 16 is NOT re-driven by the queue. That makes THIS cell's artifact-first, pointer-last rule the design's only retry safety: a task that dies before the pointer PUT leaves the previous pointer intact and the next real stamp (the nightly 02:00Z derive guarantees one) re-publishes. The contract states that recovery property in words, as the property CG-s-4's reader and CG-x-2's limb (iii) rely on.
