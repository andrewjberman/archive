## THE CELL (decomposition v0.11 s.1a, CG-s-37)

`infra/aws/globgrph/derive_stitch.py` archives its own output into the AWS snapshot and reads that snapshot back on the next cycle. **The question "is the prior cycle's output an input or is it noise?" was answered separately, and differently, on each of three carriers** -- nodes (row W2-S61), props (W2-S69), edges (W2-S71) -- and each was found in a different cell, three cells apart, because every check was scoped to one carrier and the shared dependency was the archive-read itself (row S103, and CLAUDE.md Rule 22's shared-dependency limb).

**The EDGES carrier drops. The PROPS carrier drops. The NODES carrier does not.** It de-duplicates instead, and the difference is the whole cell.

**Why the asymmetry is a defect and not a design choice:** the round-6 edge case is the tell. While the producer emitted the same triples nightly, the duplicates collapsed in the dedup and nothing showed. The stale carry-forward became visible only when a run emitted FEWER than the run before -- 137 emitted against 955 still in the artifact. **A producer that reads back its own output is silently idempotent under a FIXED emission and silently WRONG under a SHRINKING one, which is exactly what a nightly with stable counts cannot surface.**
