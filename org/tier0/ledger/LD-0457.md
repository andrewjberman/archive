# LD-0457 -- The tier model/effort default binds TIER 3. Tier 1 was offered and declined.

## The ruling

Andrew's 2026-08-24 word was: *"For future reference, tier2 agents should always be
instantiated with opus 5 model on extra high effort."*

`org/ORG_CHART.md:13-14` makes the grammar explicit:

```
  0.Y.0  where Y > 0     = Tier 2 (Senior)
  0.Y.Z  where Y > 0,Z>0 = Tier 3 (Junior)
```

So Tier 3 sat outside the 08-24 word, and **both implementations excluded it correctly
and on purpose.** `ajb.ps1` said so in terms: *"SCOPE HELD DELIBERATELY NARROW: tiers 1
and 3 are NOT widened into. He named tier 2."*

**As of 2026-08-26 the default binds Tier 2 AND Tier 3.** Tier 1 does not change.

## Why it was put to him rather than inferred

An agent widening a sovereign word by inference is the failure CLAUDE.md forbids in both
directions. The prior authors were right to hold the line at Tier 2, and the correct
remedy for a rule that seems too narrow is to **ask**, not to widen.

## What made the question live -- measured, not hypothetical

`0.6.2_AE_PartnerETL~A` -- a **Tier-3 serial** by the grammar above, and the
highest-value lane of a five-lane wave -- spawned with **no `--model` and no `--effort`**
while its four Tier-2 peers carried `opus-5` / `xhigh`. It was killed and respawned by
hand with explicit flags before any work reached it.

> ### The property, stated without the incident
> **A DEFAULT THAT ANNOUNCES ITSELF ONLY WHEN IT FIRES CANNOT BE AUDITED BY READING ITS
> OUTPUT.** `spawn-agent.ps1` prints a line when the tier default applies and prints
> nothing when it does not. Four announcements and one silence read as five normal
> spawns. **The absence is the signal, and it has no voice.**
>
> The asymmetry was therefore found by reading `--model` / `--effort` off the **live
> command lines** -- process truth -- and not from any tool's report of what it had done.
> That is the general instrument: to audit a silent default, read the ARTIFACT it
> produces, never the log of the act that produced it.

## The change

| file | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| `.tools/spawn-agent.ps1` | `^0\.([0-9]+)\.0_` | `^0\.([0-9]+)\.[0-9]+_` |
| `.tools/ajb.ps1` | `^0\.([1-9][0-9]*)\.0_` | `^0\.([1-9][0-9]*)\.[0-9]+_` |

The `Y > 0` guard is **retained in both**. Widening `Z` alone would have pulled Tier-0
utility serials (`0.0.N`) into the tier branch -- `0.0.3_BG_BBGTerminal` carries an
explicit `$modelBySerial` pin (`opus-5` / `high`) that the tier branch would then have
been racing. The guard is what keeps the explicit table winning.

## What was NOT widened, and it was offered

**TIER 1 IS UNCHANGED.** The card put "Tier 1 too -- all seats" to Andrew as an explicit
third option, noting that CTO/COO/CVO currently run with no explicit model/effort flags
at all. He did not take it.

Recorded because a future reader who finds Tier 3 added will reasonably wonder whether
Tier 1 was an oversight. **It was not. It was declined.** Re-widening needs his fresh word.

## Scope of effect

**FUTURE SPAWNS ONLY.** No running agent is touched, restarted, reconfigured or
interrupted by this change. The 10 live seats at time of commit are unaffected.
