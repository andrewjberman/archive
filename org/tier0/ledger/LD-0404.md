# LD-0404 -- /integrate becomes the MSTR launch step; the pull is ABSORBED, not dropped

## The ruling (verbatim)

> *"Also something I want to bring in to the mstr launch script is not just blindly running git
> pull, its better to run /integrate as a joint fabric parity assessment and this parity can either
> be just an assessment (default), or it can be /integrate merge, which is assess and proceed the
> merge."*

## What changed

| | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| MSTR load step | `/git-pull once` | **`/integrate`** (assess-only) |
| bare `/integrate` | Steps 0-7 **including merge + push** | **assess-only** |
| merge form | (was the default) | **`/integrate merge`**, explicit |

**The default inversion is the load-bearing half.** A launch step that could silently merge two
fabrics is a shared-branch write nobody asked for, executed on a device that may have just woken
with stale context. **Reading is always safe at load; writing is not.**

## The gap closed rather than inherited

`/git-pull` answered **same-branch currency**. `/integrate` answers **cross-branch parity**. These
are different acts, and a straight swap would have silently dropped the first -- the exact defect
`/git-pull once` was added to close.

**Shape adopted from `0.0.0_AB_MSTR~Z` over my own first proposal.** I proposed keeping the ff as a
step *beside* `/integrate`. Site Z countered: make it **Step 1b, a PRECONDITION**, because

> **A PARITY ASSESSMENT COMPUTED FROM A STALE LOCAL REF IS WRONG THE SAME WAY A SHALLOW CLONE IS
> WRONG** -- both produce confident, well-formed divergence numbers off an incomplete graph.

Step 1 closes history **depth**; Step 1b closes branch **currency**. The pull is therefore **not
replaced, it is ABSORBED as the thing that makes the assessment trustworthy** -- and an integrator
who skips it does not miss a nicety, they get **wrong numbers**, which is the only kind of
enforcement that holds.

## Severity asymmetry preserved (Andrew-direct intent, 2026-07-30)

- **NON-PRIMARY (site Z / `~Z`)**: the pull IS the sync guarantee -> **FAIL LOUD**, a skip is a REAL FINDING.
- **PRIMARY (laptop)**: incidental hygiene, "effectively source" -> **WARN**.
- **A laptop pull-deferral is NOT precedent for a site-Z one.**

## The measurement that motivates it (site Z, 2026-08-12 ~1320Z -- theirs)

`/git-pull once` ran and printed `596254785b..91e1854258  CLOUD -> origin/CLOUD` **on screen**. The
seat ff'd its own branch, saw 0 behind, moved on, **never looking at CLOUD**. The other fabric had
advanced ~34h and the startup was **structurally incapable** of noticing -- the act performed was
same-branch currency, the question that mattered was cross-branch parity. Four hours then went to
finding the divergence by hand.

## Scope -- what this does NOT do

- **Does not authorize a merge at launch.** Assess-only is the load form, deliberately.
- **Does not retire `/git-pull`.** Only the MSTR *launch step* changed.
- **Creates no gate.** Nothing is held on an approval.
