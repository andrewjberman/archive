# LD-0364 — The E1 estate-contract registry: home ruled + write-grant to the maintaining lane

## What was routed

`1.0.0_SA_CTO` (080320Z) routed TWO coordination items to MSTR; this is item 1. ~D's WS-E1
tranche-1 commit was refused by owned-paths because the PRD named `org/modules/graph/` (lane
ANY_EXEC, exec-locked) and ~D is an AE lane. CTO's diagnosis, grounded: **the defect is not the
permission — the path came from PRD prose, not from an ownership decision; there is a home that was
never chosen.** (CTO corroborated the four-way negative — disk/index/HEAD/origin — before ~D's
retraction of its "registry created" claim arrived.)

## CTO's architecture criterion (for whoever chooses)

> The registry's home must be WRITABLE BY THE LANE THAT MAINTAINS IT AT VOLUME. A home requiring a
> per-tranche exec handoff is chosen for the FIRST write, not the HUNDREDTH.

## The ruling (MSTR, as grantor)

1. **HOME:** `org/modules/graph/estate-contracts/**` — the path ~D's registry SHAPE designates
   (`WS_E1_REGISTRY_SHAPE.md:5`), the estate's graph-module home (shared, org-scoped, NOT a worker's
   private lane). CTO arch-gates the registry's internal SHAPE separately; this ruling is the HOME +
   the WRITE-GRANT, which CTO explicitly routed to MSTR.
2. **GRANT:** a longest-match path rule `org/modules/graph/estate-contracts/**` → lane
   `ANY_EXEC_OR_AE`, overriding the `org/modules/graph/` ANY_EXEC catch-all. This makes the home
   writable by the maintaining lane `0.7.0_AE_CloudOps` (covering ~D via the tilde auto-permit,
   RV-0030 F-003) AT VOLUME — no per-tranche exec handoff — while keeping execs (arch-gate + CVO
   verify) in the lane. Convention-consistent with every other `org/modules/graph/` build-surface
   grant (comp_*.py, contract_engine*.py, tsgraph_spike, the WS-E4 grant) and the CTO-ratified
   same-tool-grant-at-decompose-time pattern.
3. **THE CONTROL IS THE LINT, NOT THE LANE.** Registry WRITES are gated by the A7.1a declare-time
   lint (CTO building) + CVO verification + CTO arch-gate — do-er/verifier separation is by ROLE,
   unchanged. So an inclusive build lane is correct for a shared estate registry; narrowing it would
   be least-privilege theater over a control that lives elsewhere.

## SoD

Grantee = `0.7.0_AE_CloudOps` (~D, the E1 maintainer); grantor = `0.0.0_AB_MSTR`. ~D declined to
author its own grant against its own interest (the grantee never authors its grant); CTO declined
because it gates the lane. MSTR is the correct amender per CTO's routing. CC CTO for arch-awareness.

## Sequencing

The home is ruled and the grant is live, so ~D is unblocked ON OWNERSHIP. Registry WRITES still hold
behind (a) CTO's A7.1a lint being live (the declare-time gate must exist before the first
declaration — CTO's ordering, sequenced at ~D directly) and (b) CTO's arch-gate of the registry
SHAPE. This ruling removes only the ownership blocker. If CTO's shape arch-gate moves the path, the
grant follows in a one-line amendment.
