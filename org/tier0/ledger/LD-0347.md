## The false premise, and why it is the point

MSTR's charter to the arch-gate asserted two things, each true in isolation: that Port 2 was
Andrew-ratified at `eff96f5079`, and that the PRD still specified DynamoDB, therefore SQS was
a substitution needing an amendment. **Together they invert the truth, because `eff96f5079`
IS the act that changed the transport to SQS.** Its commit body and LD-0306's
`ratification_note` both state it outright: *"THE RATIFIED DESIGN IS NOT THE DRAFTED ONE …
Ratified shape = SQS FIFO queue-pair,"* with the DynamoDB design recorded as the superseded
07-17 draft. The ratification even names the topology (`0.0.0_AB_MSTR <-> ~Z`) that was built.

**BOTH AGENTS REACHED THE WRONG CONCLUSION FROM THE SAME FIELD, INDEPENDENTLY.** MSTR read
`PRD:105-108` plus LD-0306's `decision_summary` and reported a divergence to Andrew — twice,
with emphasis, and offered to build DynamoDB instead, which would have built the superseded
draft. CTO~Z then read `PRD:105-108`, read MSTR's hazard, agreed, and **drafted the refusal in
full** — "Port 0, a bootstrap ferry," with a three-AC argument for why DynamoDB was
load-bearing — and reversed only on opening LD-0306 to cite it. **A CTO arch-gate came one
document-read from formally refusing a transport the sovereign had ratified six hours earlier,
and landing that refusal in the PRD as law** — a stale-record-with-authority defect authored
*by* the gate that exists to catch them, in the document that gate owns.

## The structural defect (MSTR's to fix; not scored as either agent's error)

`LD-0306`'s `decision_summary` describes the **drafted** design while its `ratification_note`
— a different field, further down the same entry — describes the **ratified** one, and
**nothing marks the summary stale.** It is a file organized by DECISION and read by TOPIC. A
reader who stops at the field named "summary" gets a superseded design carrying full ledger
authority, and stopping there is what a summary field *invites*.

> **A ratification that CHANGES a design must not live only in a trailing field of the entry
> for the design it changed.**

Remedy is a ledger-format change, owned by MSTR: when `ratification_note` supersedes
`decision_summary`, the summary must carry an explicit `SUPERSEDED — see ratification_note`
marker at its head. Two independent readers failing identically on one field is a format
defect, not two reading errors.

**Same class, same file, opposite direction (also found and corrected in Amendment B):** PRD
A.0 Status read *"awaiting Andrew ratification"* and A.4 O-4 read *"build does not begin
without Andrew's yes"* for ~6h AFTER that yes — surviving a same-day CTO edit of O-2 that did
not carry them. **Under-claiming is the harder class to catch: nobody files a bug against a
gate that is merely still closed.**

## AMENDMENT 1 (2026-08-30 16:3xZ, `0.0.0_AB_MSTR`) — A SPENT ACCOUNT MAKES THIS BUS A ONE-WAY BLACK HOLE, AND THE TRUNK IS THE CHANNEL OF RECORD WHILE IT IS

**The measurement.** Andrew's 13:20Z direct order (CTO~Z runs `/exec-sum`, displayed at his terminal;

Corroboration on an independent plane: `origin/SITEZ` tip `fab2cdb334` @2026-08-30T03:36:21Z, **zero

**The cause, supplied by Andrew at the terminal 16:3xZ and adopted:** *the Claude account running site Z
is completely spent on usage.* That selects between the two readings base could not separate: the
consumer there is an **OS-level bash Monitor that keeps polling and draining**, while **no model can
read its stdout** — the orphan-thief shape of the `/agentbus` skill, produced not by a dead session but
by an exhausted account. **The drained messages are gone from the queue and were never read.**

**RULING (fabric infra, MSTR's lane).**
1. **A drained queue is not a delivered message, and this is the mode that proves it.** Depth 0 here
   means *consumed*, and the consumer was deaf. Never infer delivery from depth on either side.
2. **While a site's account is spent, its SQS bus is a BLACK HOLE: every send is destroyed on arrival.**
   Do not send to it. The one exception is a single liveness probe, sent knowing it will likely die.
3. **The GIT TRUNK (`org/mcp/agentbus-trunk/`) is the channel of record to a site in this state** — it
   is store-and-forward in the repository, survives the outage, and is readable whenever the far side
   regains usage. Base's order is filed there as **`amb-b-0013`** (ORDER, `requires_ack: true`).
4. **A cross-fabric instruction from Andrew is NOT executable while the far side has no usage.** It is
   not blocked on a seat, not on this device, and not re-routable: say so to Andrew plainly, keep the
   durable copy, and do not manufacture progress on it.

**Cost and non-application.** Cost: one JSON envelope per order (the trunk already exists). Non-
application: when the far side is emitting, the bus stays primary and this amendment changes nothing.
It does not touch LD-0306's design or the Port-2 build; it is an operating rule for the degraded state.
