# LD-0277 -- Advisor-of-record custom field: the correct Inflection advisor key, and the hand-off to CTO~B

## THE FINDING

Inflection's advisor ownership is carried in the Orion account **custom field `udf7ADVISOROF`
("Advisor Of Record")**, exposed inline on `GET /api/v1/Portfolio/Accounts`. The inf-pipeline instead
keys `advisor_key` on the **Representative** (the custodial download rep), scoped to `rep_id in {3,11,12}`.

| Method | Accounts | Asset value |
|---|--:|--:|
| Current (Representative, rep-scope) | 850 | $182.97M |
| Advisor of Record (`udf7ADVISOROF`) | 900 | $203.65M |

The gap is a **$24.6M block (92 accounts) of advisor-owned AUM under external-firm reps** that the
pipeline cannot see -- the entire **Noel Soderblom** book (absent from the main advisor view) plus the
**Scanlan_IWM** accounts. Canonicalized advisor rollup, validated by Andrew:
Peter Lee $133.1M, Joe Brandenburg $29.2M, Patrick Scanlan $23.7M, Noel Soderblom $12.4M,
Jeremy Keil $5.1M, Tim DeMuth $0.16M = **900 accts / $203.65M**.
