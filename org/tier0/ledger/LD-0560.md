# LD-0560 - A `--say` hint names what a seat should READ, never what it should OWN

## Decision

`/mstr-spawn` already says: **do not write a work-state assertion into a `--say` hint you have not
measured.** That guards *"is this work done"* — a fact about the world, which a measurement settles.

It does not guard *"whose work is this"*, and that is a **DECISION somebody else can change after
the hint is already sitting in a live seat's context.** A `--say` does not expire, cannot be edited,
and the seat has no reason to doubt it.

**THE RULE ADDED:** a hint names what the seat should **READ**, never what it should **OWN**.
*"Read your inbox; `1.0.0_SA_CTO` msg-id 96078 explains why you were seated"* survives any
re-routing. *"X is routed to this lane"* is a claim about a decision the hint's author does not
control. **Write the hint AFTER 1d, and write it as a pointer.**

## The two structural aggravators

1. ⛔ **THE AUTHOR OF A STALE HINT CANNOT RETRACT IT ALONE.** `send_message` refused this seat the
   direct route to `0.7.0_AE_CloudOps` under `mstr-no-tier2-writes` (Andrew-direct 2026-09-03
   ~17:0xZ). **The refusal is CORRECT and was not overridden** — MSTR does not write Tier-2, and the
   content being a correction of MSTR's own hint is not an exception. Budget the relay at the moment
   the hint is written, not when it turns out to be wrong.
2. ⚠ **THE RE-ROUTE WAS CAUSED BY THE PRE-FLIGHT ITSELF.** 1d exists to find encumbrance, and finding
   encumbrance is exactly what changes who should hold the work. **So a spawn whose 1d surfaces
   anything is the spawn most likely to invalidate its own hint** — the two are correlated, not
   independent.

## Not implicated

Both seats are correctly live: `0.7.0_AE_CloudOps` pid 28844 and the fresh lane
`0.7.0_AE_CloudOps~C` pid 3744, each pre-flighted on all four limbs plus the wider serial-keyed
obligation sweep (56 hits, one page, all read, zero ownership rows). The seating decision stands.
The tier-2 write gate should not change.

**COST of the rule:** none — it changes the wording of a hint, not the number of checks.
**NON-APPLICATION:** a hint that asserts nothing about ownership, which is now the only kind this
skill sanctions.
