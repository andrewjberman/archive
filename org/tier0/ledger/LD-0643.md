## Decision

crossbox CALL or ORDER. In that case the laptop manager opens the file with `python .tools/crossbox-call.py open`:

- the body is the seat's own committed file;
- the seat is named as from-serial, so acks and replies route to it;

The manager writes no substance and makes no ruling.
