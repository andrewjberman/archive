Every safe-commit attempt has 3-9 contamination files from other agents' concurrent work, requiring `git reset HEAD` + retry. Lane-violation BLOCKs fire repeatedly when scope-detection finds files outside the agent's owned-paths. F-002 made each attempt cheap-er but didn't reduce attempt RATE.
