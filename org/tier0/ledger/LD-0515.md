# LD-0515 — an aesthetic instruction landed on a live collision

## ⛔ THE REQUEST WAS A PREFERENCE; THE DEFECT UNDER IT WAS NOT

`PCOL` had **12 entries** and both templates indexed it `PCOL[n%12]`. The register carries
**D0-D12 — thirteen**. So `D12 % 12 == 0`: **D12 wrapped onto D0.**

Measured on the page published to Andrew minutes before he asked:

```
  Agent Infrastructure = 27   rgb(94,114,228)
  Platform Ops         = 61   rgb(94,114,228)   <- IDENTICAL
```

Two directives, 88 commits between them, **one colour** — indistinguishable in the stacked area
chart and in the legend beneath it.

⭐ **IT COULD ONLY SURFACE TODAY.** The collision needs BOTH D0 and D12 on the same chart, and D12
had never reached a chart before — `LIVE` was a hardcoded `range(12)` that hid it entirely
(LD-0504). **Fixing the bug that hid D12 is what made the collision that hid D12-vs-D0 visible.**
One defect masked another, and the mask was removed hours earlier by an unrelated repair.

## The palette

ROYGBIV in directive order, **13 hues**, `D0` red through `D12` magenta, indexed
`PCOL[n % PCOL.length]` — so adding D13 extends rather than silently re-pairing two rows.

**Work streams left the spectrum.** `PLANNING`/`AUDIT` were violet and grey; violet now belongs to
D11. They are a neutral family (`#5D6675`, `#98A1B0`) with the residual lighter still (`#C9CFDA`),
distinguishable from each other by value. ⭐ **This is the Rule-24 grain distinction made visible:
a stream is not a directive, so it must not borrow a directive's colour** — a violet stream beside
a violet directive asserts a kinship that does not exist.

## Third stale carrier of the day, and only the RENDERED PAGE showed it

The `by_kind` method line printed under the chart read *"OPERATOR is the declared author-grain
exception"* — **OPERATOR was retired three hours earlier** (LD-0507). The enum, the README, the
schema and the colour map were all corrected; **this one was prose inside a data field, and no
check reads prose.**

Found by looking at a screenshot of the chart, not by any instrument. **The carriers that survive
a correction are the ones nothing can fail on** — the same lesson as the README paragraph
(LD-0507) and this module's own docstring (LD-0508), now three times in one day.
