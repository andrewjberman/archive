# LD-0542 - The CTO was never blocked. The doctrine just told it to ask.

## Decision

Remove the route-through-MSTR instruction from the L2-L5 path. Change no permissions,
because none needed changing.

## What was measured

Andrew asked for an audit expecting, as I did, that lanes would need widening. They did not.

| L2-L5 carrier | lane | `1.0.0_SA_CTO` | `0.0.0_AB_MSTR` |
|---|---|---|---|
| `org/missions/*/*/*/project.json` | TREE_PROJECT_ROW | PERMIT | **REFUSE** |
| `org/missions/DIRECTIVES.yaml` | TIER0_DIRECTIVES_REGISTER | PERMIT | PERMIT |
| `org/missions/MACROS.yaml` | TREE_MACROS | PERMIT | PERMIT |
| `org/projects/<CODE>.json` | ANY_EXEC_OR_AE | PERMIT | PERMIT |
| `org/missions/*/*/*/prd/**` | ANY_CTO | PERMIT | **REFUSE** |

**Five of five already PERMIT**, tilde lanes included. The two MSTR REFUSE cells are the
discriminator, not decoration: the first reproduces the exact refusal that blocked this seat's
own tree commit minutes before, so the instrument fires.

Outside the range and correctly unchanged: `brief/**` is TREE_CRO (L1, refuses the CTO),
`decompositions/**` is TREE_COO (L5, refuses the CTO).

## The finding

⛔ **A DOCTRINE LINE THAT ROUTES AN ACT THROUGH A SEAT THE GATE DOES NOT REQUIRE IS WORSE THAN A
PERMISSION BLOCK, BECAUSE NO REFUSAL TEXT EVER PRINTS.** A blocked seat reads a refusal and asks.
An obedient seat sends a message and waits, and that wait is invisible to every instrument here
-- no gate fires, no lane refuses, no census counts it.

Measured the same evening: two IRPT projects sat unregistered across four seats' messages while
the CTO held PERMIT on every path involved.

Four carriers carried the line. Two are in this seat's lane and are fixed here
(`.owned-paths.yaml` TREE_MACROS, the `/canonical-flow` skill). Three are not
(`CLAUDE.md` Rule 27, the spine's L3 and s.9, `PROJECT_TREE.contract.json` `readers`) and are
routed to `1.0.0_SA_CTO`, which is PERMIT on all three.

⭐ **THE LAST THREE LINES TELLING THE CTO TO ROUTE THROUGH MSTR CAN ONLY BE REMOVED BY THE CTO.**
That is not an obstacle; it is the cleanest available proof that the permission plane was never
the problem.

## Rollback

One revert. `permitted_agents` is untouched in both directions, so a revert restores prose only.
