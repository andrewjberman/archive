# LD-0168 — ADHC_GLOBGRPH project genesis: global versioned Opal graph platform (Andrew-direct)

## Decision (recorded, not originated)
Andrew expanded the LD-0167 CMPAWS sovereign goal into a new fabric-significant project,
**ADHC_GLOBGRPH** — a versioned, agent-queryable **global Opal graph platform**:
- **Scope:** UNION of the comp-aws AWS-resource layer (the LD-0167 per-class compilers'
  output) with the portal/pipeline comp-graph, under **Nessie/Iceberg versioning** +
  **Kuzu Cypher** query.
- **5 Andrew-locked strategy decisions:** (1) serverless-light, (2) Nessie-over-Iceberg,
  (3) structural-first, (4) global-scope, (5) dedicated-S3.

## Operational disposition (COO lane — recorded, not re-dispatched)
COO registered (`org/projects/ADHC_GLOBGRPH.json`), decomposed (P1 S3-bucket GO'd to AE;
remainder gated behind a CTO formal PRD), routed to **CTO** (formal PRD + cross-layer
schema + infra-calls), **AE** build-lead, running parallel to CMPAWS Wave-2. MSTR does not
re-dispatch.

## Governance notes (for the record + the CTO PRD)
1. **Cost.** New **always-on Fargate + DynamoDB + dedicated-S3** footprint = recurring
   monthly spend, landing while the cloud bill is under active NFRA reduction. Andrew's
   `serverless-light` lock signals cost-awareness; the CTO PRD must **size it deliberately
   and flag the footprint to NFRA cost-tracking** (cost-footprint / right-sizing section).
   This is the standing cost-governance constraint, not a new rule.
2. **Dependency.** Data-completeness rides on the LD-0167 CMPAWS compiler F1 progress —
   the platform consumes that output.
3. **Closeout bar.** As with LD-0167, MSTR tracks this as a platform-class deliverable;
   any future closeout needs the versioning + query layer demonstrably working on real
   unioned graph data (not just infra stood up) — held at the eventual /verify-channel
   project-level pass.

## Rollback
Andrew-direct genesis; pausable/tear-down-able at the sovereign level (dedicated-S3 +
serverless = clean teardown). No MSTR rollback applies (recording action).
