# LD-0514 — 09-01 republished, and its headline work had been missing from its own chart

## Decision

`2026-09-01` re-rendered and published: `daily-reports/2026-09-01/` plus
`daily-reports/latest/` and the chooser, with 7-day presigned links. Both pages read back
**size + ETag MATCH**, and the chooser was fetched back *through its own presigned URL*.

**This overrules my own recommendation.** `/open-calls` CALL 1 recommended leaving the delivered
version standing, on the reasoning that silently changing what a shared link renders is worse than
a bad night on the record. Andrew ruled the other way and gave the reason: he needs to see it
**at the S3 link, because that is how it is actually viewed.** A verification that never touches
the serving path is not a verification of the thing people read.

## ⛔ WHAT ONLY THE REPUBLISH REVEALED

`by_project` on the original page: `Agent Infrastructure 25 · Advisor Intelligence 100 ·
Positioning 5 · MP Fortification 2 · **unattributed 154**`.

After: `... · **Platform Ops 61** · Planning 9 · Audit 71 · unattributed 26`.

⭐ **D12 PLATFORM OPS — 61 COMMITS — WAS ABSENT FROM THE CHART ENTIRELY, AND IT WAS THAT DAY'S
HEADLINE.** The page led with *"Two-factor sign-in is live on the dashboard"* while its own
activity chart showed none of the work that built it, because `LIVE` was a hardcoded `range(12)`
against a register carrying D0-D12 (LD-0504). **The report contradicted its own headline and
nothing could notice**, because the residual it fell into was labelled honestly as
"unattributed" — a correct label on a wrong number.

## The one judgement inside the instruction, stated rather than assumed

Andrew said *republish*, not *rewrite*. `team_activity` is **machine-derived** — both censuses
recompute it from that day's git population — so regenerating it with corrected instruments is a
repair of a derived value, not a re-authoring of the day. Every authored field (headline, sprint,
projects, process, blocked, source) was carried through **byte-identical**, and the refresh script
**asserts** it: `assert changed == ["team_activity"]`, which fails the run rather than trusting the
intent.

Two consequences named, not buried:
- **`commits` 286 -> 293.** The original was authored mid-evening against an open Eastern window;
  the day is now closed. Same population rule, more of the day.
- **Chart legend labels are short names, not register titles verbatim.** The register titles
  truncate to unreadable stubs at the legend's 28-char budget (`Satellites "Positioning" Var`).
  The PROJECT CARDS keep the titles verbatim per the skill's string-match rule; this is the bar
  legend, where the original was hand-labelled too.

## Rollback

Re-publish the previous bytes, or delete the objects. `latest/` is overwritten by every publish
by design, so the next day's run supersedes this without action.
