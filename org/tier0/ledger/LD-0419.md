# LD-0419 -- whole-fabric reset-and-relaunch for the 49F end-to-end push

Verbatim + plan: CTO's `org/tier1_exec/1.0.0_SA_CTO/deliverables/` record and
`ADVI_49F_END_TO_END_PUSH_PLAN_20260818.md` @a2f27c8d0a.
MSTR handoff: `org/tier0_exec/0.0.0_AB_MSTR/handoffs/2026-08-18_1515Z.md`.

## What he ordered, and the split that follows from his own words

Handoffs first, **confirmed**, then kill, then reinstantiate with the handoffs tagged into each
launch, then work the 49F push from fresh context. **CTO drives the reset**; MSTR is in the reset
set and is killed last; the successor spawns first and re-takes the population role.

**MSTR's spawn hand stays HELD** for the push. Under this order the reinstantiation is CTO's to
drive with `spawn-agent.ps1 --say`; MSTR resumes spawning after being respawned, and then only onto
COO's dated TOs -- never onto a message.

## Why the ordering is the whole control

A reset destroys working context by design. The three things that make that safe are all
**pre-kill**: handoffs written and ACKed, every lane commit pushed by the integrator, and each
relaunch carrying its predecessor's handoff path. Kill-before-ACK would lose exactly the state the
push needs.

**MSTR's integrator act is load-bearing here in a way it usually is not:** lane seats commit but do
not push, so at kill time their work exists only locally. The push must land AFTER the last lane ACK
and BEFORE the kills.

## The sharpest residual, named rather than discovered

The **23:01Z bd platinum acceptance** is a live client-facing P1 (49F advisors on 08-06 undercounted
data; fix deployed, awaiting its scheduled run). Its verification legs are held by seats being
killed and respawned mid-flight. It is flagged **ROW 0 / HARD FIXTURE** in the push plan and in the
MSTR, COO and lane handoffs, and AE's deploy ledger is deliberately left **OPEN** to it -- stated,
not orphaned, successor closes it.

*The push is about 49F end-to-end; the single most 49F-consequential act on the board tonight is
that verification. It is not a competing priority -- it is phase zero of the same product.*
