# LD-0455 — CVO spawned: the backstop found two standing functions dark with live inbound

## The measurement

```
live roster 16:3xZ   MSTR . CTO . CTO~X . COO          (4)   <- no CVO
1pt process truth    4 claude.exe, 0 carrying 3.0.0_SA_CVO
manifest/2026-08-25.json   contract v2   status PENDING   producer 13:23:21Z
CVO inbox            2 unread, newest = CTO's surprise-channel filing 16:09Z
```

## Why this is a gap and not merely an empty chair

**The two dark functions fail in opposite directions and only one of them is visible.**

The estate-report manifest is a **daily** artifact with a human-gated flip. Its lifecycle is
`RUNNING -> PENDING -> {ADJUDICATED | NO-SEMANTIC-CHANGES | FAILED}`. A day that is never adjudicated
does not error — it simply stays `PENDING` and the day passes. **An uncaptured review day is
permanent**, and nothing downstream reports it.

The Rule-27 surprise channel is worse, because it is **structurally single-owner**: COO's 2026-08-24
ruling bars executing seats from writing `org/surprise_channel/**` precisely so items are filed by
the verifier and not by the seat that found them. That is the right design and it means **an item
routed to a dark CVO cannot be filed by anybody else** — CTO did exactly the right thing at 16:09Z
and the item now sits in an inbox with no reader.

**Neither failure raises anything. Both look like quiet.** That is the whole reason this backstop row
exists, and it is the same shape as the orphan watcher: producer-side signals all read healthy.

## What I did NOT do

- **No TO, no dispatch.** Spawn is not dispatch (Art. VI). CVO is constitutionally independent —
  MSTR does not outrank a CVO ratification verdict, and provisioning the seat grants me nothing over
  it. The `--say` is a startup orient hint naming what is waiting, not direction about what to
  conclude.
- **I did not adjudicate the manifest myself.** The backstop verifies the loop RAN; it does not run
  the loop and does not land semantic changes. Human-in-the-loop stays load-bearing: the pipeline
  suggests, Andrew ratifies, CVO lands.
- **I did not file CTO's surprise item into the tree on CVO's behalf.** Same rule that bars executing
  seats binds me; writing it would defeat the single-owner property that makes the channel worth
  having.

## Rollback

One `/mstr-kill 3.0.0_SA_CVO` turn. The audit trail at `org/mcp/agent-spawn-audit.log` preserves both
the spawn and any kill.
