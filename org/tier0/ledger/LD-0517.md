# LD-0517 - shares not counts, a divergent cool palette, and today's report

## The two edits

**Legends carry SHARES.** A raw count makes the reader hold the denominator in their head. Sub-1%
renders `<1%`, never `0%` - a rounded zero beside a series the chart is visibly drawing reads as a
bug. Verified: both legends sum to 101 (rounding), no raw counts remain.

**The cool palette is divergent, not a ramp.** My first version walked indigo -> green
monotonically. It read as one family and made ADJACENT bands nearly identical - and adjacent is
exactly where a stacked area chart needs contrast, because neighbouring series share an edge.
It now alternates VALUE (dark/light) and HUE (blue/violet/teal/green) between consecutive entries.
Still entirely cool; nothing can be confused with the directive chart's ROYGBIV.
