## What was kept, and why each earns its place

- **The standing rule** -- zero crons, `/drive` OFF, COO coordinates and does not re-drive.
- **The ONE live re-arm** (site-Z `COO~Z`, OPAL_ANALYST atom-decomp) with verbatim, record
  @c4972dab41, scope bound, stop-condition, and the ONLY-DURABLE-CARRIER warning. **Dropping this
  was the whole risk of the edit** -- it is the live sovereign instruction a respawned COO~Z must
  re-arm from.
- **The `/drive` MECHANISM** -- its gate treats "all standing by" as a failure, so it is
  structurally unable to conclude "dispatch nothing" and will break a dispatch hold on a timer.
  This is the *reason* the loop is OFF rather than merely unused; a bare prohibition without it
  loses to the next agent's reasoning.

## The one substantive edit, disclosed

Each discharged clause carried its **own copy** of *"a successor must NOT re-arm off this
discharged row."* Restating a rule once per anecdote is precisely the growth mechanism. It is now
stated **once, as standing rule (1)**. That is a promotion from precedent to law, not a
reformatting, so it is named here rather than left to be discovered in a diff.

## Nothing was deleted

`docs/references/COO_DRIVE_REARM_HISTORY.md` holds the pre-trim cell **byte-for-byte**, struck markup
included, so every Andrew verbatim, commit hash, cron id and closing condition remains citable.
