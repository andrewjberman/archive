# LD-0417 -- FE-1 cancelled: do not first-class the reconstructed points

Verbatim record: `org/tier0_exec/0.0.0_AB_MSTR/deliverables/ANDREW_DIRECT_20260815_NO_FIRST_CLASSING_RECONSTRUCTED_POINTS.md`

## The ruling

`MDPF_EVR.FE-1` asked DE to first-class `point_class: "reconstructed"` in the portal FE — extend
the closed enum, add a legend entry, add distinct styling — so the 7 reconstructed inflection
points (against 438 measured) would render as their own class rather than riding the
estimate-class loud-degrade path. Andrew cancelled it.

## Why it matters beyond this TO

**He is not calling the work low-priority. He is calling the deliverable NET-NEGATIVE.** A visual
treatment that singles out 7 reconstructed points among 438 measured ones **seeds doubt
disproportionate to the magnitude** — his estimate, ~10bps, on a day nobody is looking at.

**PROMINENCE IS NOT A FREE GOOD.** The fabric has been implicitly pricing "make the provenance
more visible" at zero cost and treating it as strictly-more-honest. It is not: surfacing a
distinction invites a reader to weigh it, and a distinction that is immaterial in magnitude but
prominent in presentation *misinforms by emphasis* while every individual label stays true. That
generalises past this TO and belongs in how provenance-surfacing work is proposed at all.

## Scope — narrow, deliberately

Front-end **presentation** only. UNTOUCHED: the EVR emitter and its outputs (Andrew's 2026-08-14
"yes to both" scope + serve ruling, `@e4251a4567`); the server-side provenance lattice
(`point_class` remains in the data, correctly labelled); the ratified EVR spec honesty conditions
— `reconstructed-never-measured`, body labels, calibrate/validate split, 136bp
gate-FAIL-not-relax.

## The conflict surfaced and NOT adjudicated

Today the FE already renders `reconstructed`/`carried` as **estimate-class with a console warn**
(closed-enum loud-degrade, `portal/src/lib/pairedProvenanceRead.ts`) — itself a visible
distinction. So *"at all"* admits a second reading: soften that too, so the points render as
ordinary.

**Not implemented on inference**, and for a substantive reason rather than a procedural one: it
would collide head-on with the ratified **`reconstructed-never-measured`** condition. That is a
genuine tension between two of Andrew's own positions, and adjudicating a sovereign's scope
belongs to him. Asked at the terminal the same turn. **Nothing pends on the answer** — the cancel
is complete in itself and no work is held waiting.

## Disposition

- Record + broadcast to COO and CTO, same turn (record+broadcast law). CTO was not live; queued.
- `MDPF_EVR.FE-1` → `cancelled` by COO as ISSUER, citing the record (`cancelled`, not
  `superseded` — the need went away by ruling; nothing met it another way).
- MSTR's carried spawn-or-defer-date item: **DISCHARGED**, not deferred. It does not become an
