# LD-0165 — MSTR pure-Tier-0 posture + standing COO spawn/reap authorization

## Decision
Andrew sovereign ruling (2026-06-08 MSTR-startup) on SS-0007 open-item #2: **MSTR pulls
back to pure Tier-0.**

**MSTR scope (retained):**
- Tier-0 governance: Decision Ledger (LD), governance reviews (RV / /mstr-review, now
  ad-hoc per LD-0164), cycle/retro records (CY/RT).
- Sovereign-decision escalation to Andrew (S3/S4-class).
- Constitutional / charter authorship (/mstr-charter, embedded LD ratification).
- Event-triggered /mstr-spawn + /mstr-kill ONLY for cases COO cannot self-serve:
  Tier-1 bring-up, concurrency-cap bypass, cross-division provisioning.
- Loop-health / mandate-drift meta-supervision (the LD-0056 design role).

**Delegated to COO (standing authorization):**
- Provision + reap workers and tilde lanes per /allocation + /drive, WITHOUT per-action
  MSTR ratification, within the concurrency cap.
- Day-to-day operational round-trips (relays, status-driving, block resolution).
- COO escalates to MSTR only for: cap-bypass, Tier-1 spawns, or governance-class calls.

## Why
- SS-0007 #2: the 2026-06-05 session ran MSTR at a high cadence of COO-operational
  round-trips (spawns/reaps/relays). That drifted from the LD-0056 architectural intent
  — COO as primary operational driver, MSTR as Tier-1 loop-health meta-supervisor.
- Pulling MSTR back to pure Tier-0 restores that separation: COO owns operations end-to-
  end via /drive + /allocation; MSTR governs the org rather than operating through it.
- Lower MSTR operational footprint = fewer ticks, cleaner audit separation (operational
  actions in the COO lane, governance actions in the MSTR lane).

## Operating note (this session)
Andrew is bringing up agents via a forthcoming COO /allocation request (not via direct
MSTR spawns). MSTR holds — no autonomous bring-up. This is the pure-Tier-0 posture in
practice from turn one.

## Rollback
MSTR rescinds the standing COO authorization and resumes direct operational spawn/reap.
Fully reversible.
