## Two things worth carrying forward

**A SUPERPROJECT SUBMODULE "CONFLICT" IS NOT EVIDENCE OF DIVERGENCE.** `git merge-tree` and
`git merge` both reported `CONFLICT (submodule)` with "commits don't follow merge-base" while
one pointer was a strict ancestor of the other. The superproject cannot rank submodule commits,
so it escalates to the integrator by construction. **Rank the pointers inside the submodule
before treating it as a decision** -- otherwise every routine portal bump reads as a dispute
and gets escalated to an owner who is not needed.

**THE CEILING IS NOT THE MANIFEST, MEASURED AGAIN HERE.** The raw glob over `data/*/scripts/**`
returned 2 files; exactly 1 was in real deploy scope, because `data/etfs` is not a declared
`CODE_TREES` tree and `presentation` is not a layer. The gate then deployed exactly that 1.
Predicting 2 would have overstated the deploy by 100 percent. **Re-derive scope from
`safe_deploy_core.py` CODE_TREES, never from the path shape.**
