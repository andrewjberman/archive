# LD-0018 — Charter CVO to verify-channel the fee-billing imputation methodology

## Decision

DS_ClientPlatform surfaced a "fee billing gap" finding in VALFIN.001
Phase-1 (commit `c02c9c1ed`). COO routed as Routing Ask #3; surfaced
to Andrew. Andrew's answer (verbatim above):

> "If i recall correctly, they only recorded billing data back to
> mid-2025 maybe September. We imputed it backward through some
> mechanism, it's worth having CVO review this methodology."

The "gap" DS observed IS the imputation territory. Pre-Sep-2025 fee
billing is reconstructed via some backfill mechanism (likely lives in
`data/orion/scripts/silver/` or `data/*/scripts/gold/` fee-related
scripts). Whether that backfill is methodologically sound is the
load-bearing question for VALFIN Phase B/C closure-eligibility
+ for any downstream fee-dependent gold script.

Charter CVO with a /verify-channel investigation of the imputation
mechanism. MECHANICAL / NOVEL / META framework, same template
established by VALFIN.001 GREEN (CVO `55a6ef4bc`):
- **MECHANICAL:** locate the imputation script(s) + their inputs;
  confirm artifacts exist + are fresh.
- **NOVEL:** independent probe of the imputation logic against a
  small set of post-Sep-2025 accounts (where real billing exists);
  does the imputation match within an acceptable tolerance? Probe
  data DS did not gate on.
- **META:** does the imputation, in its holistic purpose, produce
  numbers that should be trusted as gold-tier inputs? Are there
  failure modes (advisor changes, fee-arrangement changes, account
  closures) that the imputation doesn't model well? Surface as
  finding-list rather than fix.

Outcome: GREEN (imputation is sound; DS can use) / YELLOW
(specific findings to address but usable) / RED (imputation
unsound; charter fix back to DS via COO + halt VALFIN Phase B/C
on it).

## Why now

Andrew has answered the question. DS has been parked on VALFIN
Phase B/C waiting for this resolution path. CVO has the standing
/cvo-loop authority + the template; this is a natural one-cycle
add to their queue.

## Assumptions

- The imputation mechanism is locatable (likely a documented gold
  or silver script; CVO finds it via `comp-pipescript` lineage from
  the fee fields VALFIN uses).
- Post-Sep-2025 real billing is sufficient ground-truth to probe
  the imputation against (sample size for NOVEL layer).
- If imputation is RED, the fix is DS-lane (gold/silver script
  update) per CLAUDE.md Rule 13; CVO routes, doesn't fix.

## Alternatives considered

- **Charter DS to self-audit the imputation** -- rejected: verifier-
  not-fixer discipline (Art. VIII). DS is the producer; CVO is the
  independent verifier.
- **Defer to a /spot-check cycle** -- /spot-check is alignment-
  metadata-only by default; substance verification is /verify-channel
  scope (which CVO's standing /cvo-loop covers).
- **Charter COO to triage first** -- COO already triaged
  (Routing Ask #3); the substance is methodology, which is CVO's
  scope, not COO's.

## Rollback plan

Charter is informational + investigation; if CVO's investigation
finds the imputation sound (GREEN), the charter closes with verdict
recorded; if YELLOW/RED, the fix charter routes back to DS via COO
through the standard verify-channel verdict flow. No destructive
