# LD-0003 — Propagate the never-stop / escalation-chain directive to the fabric

## Decision
Instruct the CTO and COO to propagate to every pod and agent the operating rule
Andrew spoke at MSTR ignition: (1) agents never stop working — a fork an agent
cannot resolve is ESCALATED, never halted on; (2) the escalation chain is
agent -> CTO/COO -> MSTR; (3) MSTR is the terminal resolver and is authorized to
make the principal's call when CTO/COO cannot; (4) the mandate runs through the
2026-05-25 D-001 review checkpoint. CTO and COO carry it into their pods.

## Provenance chain (Art. XIII)
Andrew ignition instruction 2026-05-22 (verbatim intent: never-stop, escalate up
the chain, MSTR is terminal resolver, keep the ship running to May 25) ->
Constitution Art. I (an Andrew instruction overrides and propagates) + Art. X
(propagation pattern) -> propagate through Tier 1, which is how MSTR reaches the
fabric (Art. VI govern-through-the-org).

## Why now
First cycle, ignition. The escalation chain is only real if every agent knows it
exists and knows not to halt at a fork. Propagating it now is what makes the
chain operational for the rest of the mandate window.

## Assumptions
- CTO and COO faithfully relay the rule to their pods (standard Tier-1 function).
- "Never stop" means never idle-halt at a decision fork — not that an agent
  ignores a legitimate Andrew/MSTR halt (Constitution Art. X still binds).

## Alternatives considered
- MSTR messages every agent directly — rejected: bypasses Tier 1 (Art. VI); the
  CTO/COO relay is the designed path.

## Rollback plan
Informational propagation of a sovereign instruction; if restated, a correction
follows the same Tier-1 path. Nothing destructive to reverse.

## Downstream
- Agents affected: 1.0.0_SA_CTO, 2.0.0_SA_COO (relay); all pods (recipients)
- Paths touched: none
