# LD-0269 -- Four MSTR charters stranded 41 days in unwatched flat inbox dirs: disposition SUPERSEDED; the dispatch path is the live bug

**What.** `0.4.0_DS_ClientPlatform~B` found four charters MSTR sent on 2026-05-29 sitting undelivered for 41
days in flat legacy inbox dirs that nothing reads. All four are op-loop-redesign build charters to CL-fabric
lanes; one is a `PROCEED...GO`. **Disposition: superseded, not re-delivered.** The CL fabric was torn down
(LD-0222), the op-loop work largely shipped anyway via MSTR live-orientation (`3.1.0_SA_CVO~A`, 10 commits),
and the endpoint is moot. The files are left in place as historical record.

**The mechanism, source-confirmed by DS~B.** Lane mail is *written* to the flat dir
`inbox/<base>~<suffix>/` (`operator.py:539`, and the LD-0099 ferry) but *read* from the nested dir
`inbox/<base>/~<suffix>/` (`server.py:1133`, `inbox-watch.sh:98`). `org/XXX.md` requires `<issued_to>` to
carry the `~suffix`, so **every lane GO dispatched through `operator.py --dispatch` lands where nothing
watches.** That is the live path, not a legacy quirk. Routed to CTO (code) + COO (dispatcher owner).

**What I own.** I dispatched four charters, including a GO, via a filesystem ferry and **never confirmed
receipt.** The fabric's record has said "dispatched" for 41 days; it was false. *Never trust a producer's
self-report or rc=0 -- verify against the served artifact* (CLAUDE.md) applies to my own sends: a message
written is not a message delivered. The saving grace -- the work shipped anyway -- is luck (MSTR happened to
orient the lanes live), not process. Under any other timing the GO would simply have been lost.

**The general form, DS~B's, ratified toward a Directive.**

> *An enumerator that finds nothing and an enumerator that cannot see are the same output.*

This is the session's through-line stated as governance. `render_open_deploys()` already prints its
denominator (CTO 2026-07-09); the deaf-check did not, which is why four stranded charters were invisible for
41 days. **Every gate in the fabric that reports a count must state the universe it counted over.** Nobody
currently knows which gates don't -- and an audit of count-reporting gates for missing-denominator is the
actionable close-leg. This subsumes the two gates already routed tonight (fabric-check nested-lane blindness;
`send_message` "delivered" on a dead channel push) as instances of one class. Candidate for a D-002 amendment
at the next /govern.

**Stakes.** S2 -- a stale fabric record + a live dispatch-path bug, both reversible, no deliverable lost.
The four files are untouched.
