# LD-0502 — ETFS_GUARDRAIL registered, and the alert moratorium is widened by exactly one send

## Decision

`ETFS_GUARDRAIL` under **D4**, `dep_stage: PRD`. Register now **13 directives / 37 declared projects**.

## ⛔ THE REGISTER STEP WAS SKIPPED, AND THIS ROW SAYS SO ON ITS OWN FACE

Rule 27's order is **register -> PRD -> decomp**. This project reached a stage-2 PRD
(`docs/architecture/ETFS_GUARDRAIL_PRD.md` @`7aa1554881`, now ON ORIGIN) with **no register row behind
it**. ⭐ `1.0.0_SA_CTO` **surfaced the gap rather than silently patching it** — correctly, since the
register is not that seat's surface. The row is cut here after the fact and **records that the order
did not hold**, rather than presenting itself as though it had.

## ⭐ THE MORATORIUM IS WIDENED BY HIS EXPLICIT WORD — AND THE WIDTH IS WRITTEN DOWN

CLAUDE.md bans any alert / alarm / SNS / SES-send / notification / freshness-checker / monitor
*"unless Andrew explicitly asks."* **He asked.**

```
  AUTHORIZED   ONE SES send, TERMINAL FAILURE PATH ONLY -- emitted by the remediation ladder when
               the ladder terminates unresolved, the same act that already writes the refusal.
  STILL BANNED a new monitor, a new alarm, a new SNS topic, a standing freshness service, or ANY
               send on a SCHEDULE rather than on terminal failure of the ladder.
```

>> **AN UNSTATED WIDTH IS INHERITED BY THE NEXT SEAT AS LICENCE.** That is `1.0.0_SA_CTO`'s reason for
   broadcasting rather than filing, and it is why the bound sits on the register row and not only in a
   PRD section. <<

⛔ **His own sentence is the governing constraint:** *"This email must be a last resort, the backfill
strategy must be effective enough that it doesnt happen."* **A seat that builds this as a polling
monitor has EXCEEDED the carve-out, not implemented it.**

## ⚠ PLACEMENT TENSION — FLAGGED, NOT RESOLVED

The row sits in **D4 on SUBJECT grounds**: every `ETFS_*` project lives there, and the guardrail guards
D4's own monthly ETF ownership report. **But D4 is SITEZ-placed** (his ruling 2026-09-01, for the
`ASSETCLASS_TAXONOMY` phase), while the step-1 brief (`4.0.0_SA_CRO`) and the PRD (`1.0.0_SA_CTO`) were
**both authored on the LAPTOP**. Placement attaches to the DIRECTIVE and never to a member, so
membership in D4 hands this to site Z. **That may be exactly right, or the work may want to stay where
it was built. One line moves it.**

## Two things the PRD surfaces that are HIS, carried so they do not die with the message

- **FWD-8 was NOT folded away.** He observed FWD-8 *"can be folded into"* FWD-7. The **report content**
  is genuinely folded; the **authorization** is not, because they sit at different moments:
  **FWD-8 fires BEFORE egress and ASKS PERMISSION; FWD-7 fires AFTER egress has already failed and
  REPORTS.** A message sent after egress failed cannot ask permission for the egress that already
  happened, so folding them would **silently retire ruling card 4's capability.** Whether card 4 stops
  being a named outcome is his call.
- **A stale question outlives its answer in the brief.** Source brief s.6a still reads *"OPEN — ANDREW'S
  CALL: does aj@ replace andrew@ ... Spec assumes REPLACE"* — which his **card-3 ruling already
  answered (JOIN, BOTH addresses)**. The stale line sits LATER in the file than the ruling, so a
  top-down reader meets the answer first and the open question second.

## IDs are FROZEN from `7aa1554881` forward

FWD-7 was inserted and the prior FWD-7 renumbered to FWD-8. ⛔ **A renumber is normally FORBIDDEN** — it
silently re-points every citation ever made and nothing announces it. It was legal **exactly once**
here because at the moment of the change the list had never landed in a PRD and **nothing cited it**:
no remediation row, no `outcome_ref`, no TO. Any row citing an `ETFS_GUARDRAIL` FWD-n written before
2026-09-02 **does not exist**; none were cut.
