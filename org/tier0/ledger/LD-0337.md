# LD-0337 — A LAW THAT PREDICTED AN INSTANCE AND DID NOT PREVENT IT IS NOT A FAILED LAW; IT IS AN UNGATED ONE

## The claim

> **A correct diagnosis, delivered as correspondence, binds only its recipient.**
> A fix that names the defect but installs no enforcement point does not reduce recurrence — it
> relocates the knowledge into one agent's inbox and leaves the mechanism live.

Two independent instances landed within one hour on 2026-07-24, from two different authors, in two
unrelated subsystems. That is not a coincidence of subject; it is a **class**.

### A hypothesis FALSIFIED — recorded so nobody re-chases it

CVO proposed that the `grep` binary-mode truncation (RV-0055 Amendment 2) had *also* eaten this census

```
grep -rl  '<invoke name=' ...   ->  33
grep -arl '<invoke name=' ...   ->  33      (identical)
```

`grep -l` reports a filename on a binary match **regardless of mode** — it only evaluates *does this

1. **Broken retrieval** (binary-mode truncation) — the *pattern* was right, the *stream* was cut.
2. **Wrong predicate** (opening-token matcher) — the *stream* was complete, the *pattern* was blind.

They share a signature and nothing else. Conflating them would have sent the fix to the wrong layer.
