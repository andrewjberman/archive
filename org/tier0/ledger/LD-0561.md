# LD-0561 - infra/logic/** gets its own lane, so a singular escape stops being used as one

## Decision

`infra/logic/**` -> new lane **`PLATFORM_LOGIC_DOCS`**: `4.0.0_SA_CRO`, `~A`, `~B`, `~C`, `~D`.
Longest-match displaces the `infra/**` catch-all for that subtree and nothing else.

## Why, in the order the reasons actually bind

1. **ANDREW NAMED THE PATH.** *"Persist this group of artifacts in a folder in
   ajb_workspace/infra/logic."* A seat re-siting a folder he named in order to dodge a lane would be
   solving the gate's problem by editing his sentence.
2. **THE LANE RULE'S REASON DOES NOT REACH IT.** `infra/**` is CLOUD_OPS_OR_ETL because in this estate
   `infra/` has meant DEPLOYABLE — Terraform, Lambda source, EC2 — and an AE owns what gets applied to
   the account. `infra/logic/` holds a README, an order-of-operations, a census, doctrine and analysis.
   Nothing `safe-apply` will ever touch. **This is not an exception carved into the AE lane; it is the
   lane being told where its own boundary is.** Architectural basis given independently by
   `1.0.0_SA_CTO` (96245), which also recommended a NAMED lane over `ANY_EXEC`.
3. ⭐ **THIS FILE'S OWN DOCTRINE, WITH ANDREW'S RULING BEHIND IT.** The `ANY_EXEC_OR_AE` description
   records: *"A repeat override for a STANDING role-authorized action = a coverage gap to close"* and
   *"Andrew ruled ENUMERATE over bless-the-override."* `YANG_PIPELINE_DEPLOY`, `DATA_REFRESH_DEPLOY`
   and `PUBLIC_DATA_ROUTER_DEPLOY` are the same shape. He authorized the override here for this
   purpose and the folder landed under it at `845abef6c9` — **but a `/loop` committing every 10 minutes
   would fire that singular escape six times an hour.** `4.0.0_SA_CRO~D` said exactly that before
   asking, and declined to self-override. The record now carries one override, not N.

## SoD

**GRANTOR `0.0.0_AB_MSTR`, GRANTEE `4.0.0_SA_CRO`.** The grantee did not commit its own grant — the
`CRO_WORKSPACE` and `CVO_WORKSPACE` precedents exactly. MSTR is permitted on this file via
`ANY_EXEC_OR_AE`, confirmed by resolving the rule rather than assuming it.

⚠ **My first resolution said MSTR was NOT permitted.** That was my own script failing to unwrap a
nested `lane:` key, not the register. It also claimed I could not write `DIRECTIVES.yaml`, which I had
committed to twice today — the contradiction is what exposed it. **Re-derive a gate's rule and you
inherit your own bug; the register and the hook were both right.**

## Scope held narrow, deliberately

- **NOT `1.0.0_SA_CTO`** — it gave the arch input and explicitly did not ask for write access. A grant
  nobody requested is a widening.
- **NOT `~Y`/`~Z`** — Codex and site-Z conventions; D14 is LAPTOP-placed by Andrew's word.
- A CRO lane appearing later is **REFUSED until enumerated**, which is the correct failure direction
  for an allow-list.

## Rollback

Delete the `PLATFORM_LOGIC_DOCS` lane and the `infra/logic/**` rule; the subtree falls back to
`infra/**` / CLOUD_OPS_OR_ETL and the override path still works on Andrew's word.
