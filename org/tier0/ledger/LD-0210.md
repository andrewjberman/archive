# LD-0210 -- Andrew-direct crawl-risk clearance (DATA_PLATFORM reverse-ETL supplant ground-truth)

## Decision
Record the Andrew-direct **clearance of the crawl-risk gate** on the DATA_PLATFORM
reverse-ETL supplant. MSTR had surfaced a held risk-call: the supplant is
SOURCE-CONFIRMED for 49F (Schwab-direct raw held) but **cot is bounded at BD / inf at
Orion** (no raw custodian source), so extending the supplant to those firms required
either (1) crawling the vendor platforms for ground-truth, or (2) projecting the
49F-calibrated model onto unvalidated firms. Andrew cleared **(1) crawling is fine.**

## Rationale (Andrew, verbatim)
"crawling is fine, nobody would be expecting a crawl on an access-gated view + we
literally crawl the pages already via the ec2s for most vendors."
- **Access-gated, not a public scrape** -> low expectation/detection risk.
- **Already-established practice** -> the EC2 exporters already crawl vendor pages for
  most vendors; this is the same risk class, not a new one.

## Effect
- **Un-gates** the held DS-deep work (Eclipse-char on DC2; cot/inf ground-truth).
- The cot/inf supplant can now be **grounded on crawl-acquired custodian data** rather
  than **projected** from the 49F anchor -> the projection-accuracy risk shrinks to only
  wherever authenticated crawl-access genuinely does not exist.
- COO un-holds the held leg + requests the gated DS-lane respawn (it was held "until
  Andrew clears the cot-inf risk-call"); CTO's per-vendor DC live-crawl arch-gates extend
  to the additional vendors (Orion/Eclipse/BD-platform).

## Scope / boundary
Authorized = crawling vendor platforms/views Opal holds **legitimate authenticated
access** to (accounts/credentials we hold). Extends the ratified ROE to cover the
BD-platform / Orion / Eclipse crawls previously flagged separately-Andrew-gated. **NOT**
a license for unauthenticated/public scraping of third parties we lack access to.
CTO arch-gates per-vendor isolation (the DC0 safety-core); CVO + MSTR govern.

## Rollback
Re-narrow the crawl-ceiling if a vendor relationship/ToS posture changes. Reversible;
no state committed by this record beyond the authorization itself.
