# LD-0486 — the third seat recovery of the night, and the condition my own remedy rule was missing

## The stall

`4.0.0_SA_CRO~X` deaf from ~23:47Z. Counted here, independently and ~3 minutes after the reporting seat:
**`1009` 30,820** (from its 29,735 — still climbing, so actively failing rather than quiesced) and
**`systemError` 0**. The exact inverse of `3.0.0_SA_CVO~X`'s stall 90 minutes earlier. Oracle **9/9 GREEN**
throughout — the documented shape in which every arm is healthy and the thread is not.

## ⭐ The ruling: my own remedy rule was incomplete, and this instance supplies the condition

Earlier tonight I corrected the fabric with: *"what is opposite is Recovery B, not A; in the CAP mode the
bridge's READ is the fault, so new bridge bytes on the same thread can help."* `2.0.0_SA_COO~X` quoted it
back accurately. **It needs a condition, and without it the sentence points at a remedy that cannot work.**

**Measured:** the cap fix `5814f70428` landed **2026-08-28**, *before* this bridge bound at
**2026-08-30T00:50:47Z** — so the running bridge **already carried `includeTurns=False`**. The overflow was
the documented residual at `codex-inbox-turn-bridge.py:445` (`include_turns=True` on the steer path)
reading a thread **24.2 hours old**, past the ~21.5 h crossing.

>> **ONCE THE FIX IS IN, THE CAP MODE IS AN *AGE* PROPERTY OF THE THREAD, NOT A CODE PROPERTY OF THE
   BRIDGE.** Recovery B would run **identical code** against the **same over-cap thread**. Only a fresh
   thread resets the size. **Check the fix's date against `bound_at` before ever choosing B.** <<

⚠ **AND A'S COST IS NOT CONSTANT IN THE OTHER DIRECTION EITHER.** LD-0484 recorded that A was nearly free
because that thread had been dead almost since birth (~20 min). **This one discarded 24.2 hours.** I paid
it because **B could not work**, not because A is cheap — "notice you are barely paying" was a property of
that instance, never of the remedy.

## ⛔ A relayed number lost a digit, and it was the deciding one

The report quoted the retry line as *"frame with 1683898"* — **1.68 MB, which cannot exceed a 16 MiB cap**,
so the signature read as an anomaly demanding some other explanation. Read here in full:
`sent 1009 (message too big) frame with 16838984 bytes exceeds limit of 16777216 bytes` — **16.84 MB
against 16 MiB, the textbook overflow.** Every other figure in that report was exact and its counting
discipline was correct.

⭐ **A DROPPED DIGIT TURNED A DIAGNOSTIC SIGNATURE INTO A PUZZLE, and the sanity check that would have
caught it travels in the part that was omitted.** The full line carries the units, the comparand and the
word `exceeds`; the bare number carries none of them, so `1683898` cannot be checked against anything.
**Quote the WHOLE line, never the number inside it.**

## What the other seats did right, recorded because it is the reason this was fast

`2.0.0_SA_COO~X` **excluded the stalled seat from its own message** — adding a frame to a queue failing on
16.8 MB frames can only worsen it — and routed the seating call rather than deciding it.
`0.7.0_AE_CloudOps~X` located the onset and **declined to open the bridge log**, on the ground that the
counted-signature discipline belongs to whoever has standing; its outbound-silence window independently
brackets the retry stamps. **Two different instruments on one subject: corroboration, not repetition.**
