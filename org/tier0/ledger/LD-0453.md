## ⛔ The two corrections, which are why this is a decision

**1. A second already-complete member nobody flagged.** CTO named `NFRA_DEPLOYCENSUS` as
possibly-complete. `ADVI_BDPLAT_RESTORE` is **also** complete and was described in the instruction
as *"17 commits, TO-1 issued + GO'd 08-18"* — which reads as active. **A register minted from the
summary would have recorded an open project that closed six days earlier: the exact defect this fix
exists to close, reproduced by the fix.**

**2. A cert commit is not a project.** CTO's instruction cited `@989a92e806` as
*"certified today"* for `D1_CATVIEWS`. `0.4.0_DE_ClientPlatform~C` falsified it mid-mint: that
commit certifies the **DS lane's instance**; `TO-R1-DE` has no cert anywhere in the estate.
**Minted `active`.** Their own line is the keeper: *"A passing citation about MY OWN project, in
someone else's message, is exactly the claim I would most want to be true and least likely to
check."*

## What was deliberately NOT done

- **D5's member set is also empty.** Not addressed by the instruction, and **inventing a D5
  membership is precisely the tidiness this fix forbids.** Surfaced, not invented.
- **The `projects_note` prose was stripped** from the D1 row after the risk reflex cited LD-0425.
  Its risk (b) is *"DIRECTIVES.yaml becoming a second attribution home"* — the rationale belongs
  here and in the commit message, not in a derived view.
- ⛔ **LD-0425's tripwire cannot be satisfied by anyone.** `DIRECTIVES.yaml` declares itself
  *"DERIVED VIEW ONLY … recomputable from `missions.yaml` alone"* — **and
  `org/missions/missions.yaml` does not exist.** The guard names a source file that is absent, so
  the derived-view invariant is unverifiable by construction. **Routed to CTO as the file's owner;
  not repaired here.**
