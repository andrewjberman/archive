## ⭐ The boundary is adopted AT MINTING, because it is the thing most likely to go wrong

```
  MDPF_HEALGAP    THE INSTANCE.  The Cottonwood runner's missing 2026-08-31 date, that runner's
                  schedule ordering, and its recovery gap. Terminates when that runner is correct
                  and the date is closed.
  AUTH_SELFHEAL   THE PATTERN.   Auth self-healing + consumer retry across EVERY cookie-riding
                  consumer, both firms. Terminates when the class has an inventory, a validated
                  ordering rule, and a stated position on re-mint.
```

**Two projects on one surface collide unless the split is stated**, so it is stated on the register row
itself rather than left in a brief someone may not read. ⭐ **The brief's own tell is what makes the split
right: `MDPF_HEALGAP`'s fix — moving one `schedule_expression` — does not make any OTHER pipeline safer.**
That is the substance of Andrew's *"standalone project"* instruction; a second cell inside the instance
would have inherited the instance's terminus.

⚠ **`macro_code` is `AUTH`, not `MDPF`, and that is deliberate.** D8's siblings are `MDPF_*`, but this
scope is cross-firm and cross-consumer rather than model-portfolio-pipeline. **The `directive` field
carries the D8 membership; the macro carries the subject** — naming it `MDPF_*` would mis-scope it at
every future read, which is precisely the failure the boundary above exists to prevent.
