# LD-0287 -- THE LAW DOC WAS 72% ONE RULE'S DIARY

## The measurement

    CLAUDE.md          15,118 words / 159 lines   -- loaded into EVERY agent's context, EVERY turn
    Rule 22a alone     11,016 words = 72.3%       -- 49 doctrine blocks, nearly all dated 2026-07-13

**The file's own governing rule:** *"A rule = imperative (<=2 lines) + enforcing gate + pointer. The why and
incident history live at the pointer."*

**Rule 22a was a verification treatise living inside a rulebook** — and every agent paid for it, on every
turn, forever.

## The split (Andrew-directed)

| | `docs/doctrine/` | `docs/references/` |
|---|---|---|
| **Answers** | *What must I do?* | *What happened?* |
| **Tense** | Imperative, timeless | Past, dated |
| **Ages** | Only if the practice changes | Never — it is the record |
| **Edited?** | Yes, as practice sharpens | **No** — corrections are APPENDED |

**A principle whose incident is lost is a principle the next person can talk themselves out of.** So the two
link both ways, and both READMEs say plainly that **neither stands alone.**

## What stayed in CLAUDE.md, and why

**The imperative, plus the mechanism an agent CANNOT OBEY THE RULE WITHOUT.** *A bare prohibition loses to
the agent's own instruments* — so these are NOT cuttable:

- the layer test (STATE → comp-tool **and** live primitive; BEHAVIOR → **exercise** it)
- **`comp-iam` models `(action)`, never `(action, resource)` — it FAILS GREEN**
- `simulate-principal-policy`'s **two independent false-deny modes**, and the remedy: **one `--resource-arns`
  per call, positive control as a SEPARATE invocation, `--context-entries` for conditioned statements**

**What left: the incidents that taught them.**

- **NEVER#3 / NEVER#7:** `never-rules-gate.sh` **exists and is wired** in `settings.json`. Their long "here

- **Rule 18's "latch repair pending" could NOT be confirmed as fixed, so it was LEFT ALONE.** *An unverified

## Amendment — three principles added the same afternoon, and they overturned a ruling of my own

The doctrine doc earned three new entries within hours of being created, all from `0.5.0_AE_OpalETL`
grounding a bug that COO and I dispatched him on — **and overturning the fix we both recommended.**

1. **A PRESENT-DAY FIX AND A STALE-ARTIFACT BUG PRINT THE SAME RED ON A SERVED READ.** COO measured a real
   defect on the served artifact, asserted the mechanism (*"the producer stamps the file date — one-line fix,
   0.5.0's lane"*), and **I relayed it without grounding it.** `0.5.0` pulled the **deployed zip**: the
   producer had been fixed for two weeks. **The bug was RESIDUE.** *Neither of us opened the deployed
   producer.* **Only the deployed bytes tell you which one you have.**

2. **A DATE FIELD THAT LIES ABOUT ITSELF DOES NOT STOP LYING BECAUSE YOU READ IT FROM A LOWER LAYER.** I
   cleared him to read the "true" vintage from the source partition's internal `date` column. **It carried the
   same lie** — byte-proven: `holdings_06-13/06-14/06-15` are the **same book** (identical non-date body hash,
   11,487 rows each) with the date column **relabeled**. **Going one layer down is not the same as finding an
   independent source.**

3. **YOU CANNOT STAMP A DATE YOU CANNOT DETERMINE.** The true vintage is unrecoverable, so **the re-stamp I
   recommended to Andrew and to CTO+COO is UNEXECUTABLE. I withdrew it rather than defend it.**
   > **REPLACING AN OBVIOUS LIE WITH A PLAUSIBLE ONE IS STRICTLY WORSE: AN OBVIOUS LIE GETS CAUGHT; A
   > PLAUSIBLE ONE GETS CITED.**

**The disciplinary note that earned all three:** `0.5.0` had a plausible mechanism for the gold-layer lie, knew
it would be believed, and **refused to assert it because he had not read the producer's source.** He handed up
a **fact** and let me route it. *Seven agents spent eleven hours yesterday asserting mechanisms they had not
read.* **He read the bytes and then declined to guess.**

*(This is the doctrine folder working as intended, on day one: a principle earned, distilled, and landed —
while the incident that earned it stays in the record.)*

## Amendment 2 — the loop closed, and four more principles landed

The ETF investigation closed end-to-end in ~40 minutes across three lanes, and every step of it produced
doctrine. **Every finding came from someone opening the thing itself; every wrong turn came from someone
reasoning about a system from its symptoms.**

- **A HASH IS A PROXY; THE COLUMNS ARE THE THING.** DS~A hashed three daily partitions, got three distinct
  digests, and nearly concluded his own script was fabricating — **the hash was dominated by the two date
  columns he had not dropped, the very fields under investigation.** *When you hash to prove sameness, first
  remove the fields whose sameness is the question.*
- **The "one layer down" rule points FURTHER down than we thought.** The lie is born in the **silver pull**,
  which stamps `as_of_date`/`snapshot_date` with **the date it REQUESTED**, not the vintage of what came back.
  Gold reports faithfully; the ETF producer inherited it. **Keep descending until you reach a field the
  producer did not choose.**
- **A SATURDAY BOOK EQUALLING FRIDAY'S IS CORRECT. MONDAY'S IS THE LIE.** *Before you condemn a repeat, ask
  whether the world moved.*
- **A WITHDRAWAL NEEDS NO GROUNDING; A GRANT DOES.** COO voided his own co-sign the moment its premise was
  falsified — **without re-verifying anything** — then **declined to grant the replacement until he could
  ground it himself.** *Refusing to act is always the safe direction.*

**And the leads that died:** my `INFERRED` 07-10-rebuild link, and `0.5.0`'s mixed-regime hypothesis. **Both
were flagged as unasserted, both were measured, both were killed.** *Two unrelated defects, not one root.*
**A lead cleanly killed is worth exactly as much as one that lands — and neither of them was ever allowed to
authorize a fix.**

**My own record from the same window, for the ledger:** I twice stated a critical path from a reading taken
hours earlier and never re-taken (the coverage guard; then DS~A's selector, which had been live for three
hours when I told him it was not). **A Tier-0 summary is a served artifact, and other agents quote it.** Both
corrected by the lanes, not by me.

## Amendment 3 — the lead-kill was right and its PREMISE was false, and the retraction it forced

**DS~A corrected his own lead-kill within two hours, unprompted, before it could be quoted further.** He had
told me *"the ONLY columns that differ are the date stamps."* **His own output showed `asset_class` differed on
2 rows. He read it and glossed it, because it fit no part of his story.**

The verdict survived — **`current_value` moved on 0 of 33,381 rows, and THAT is the finding** — but the claim
carrying it was false. **`0.5.0`'s body-hash flipped on exactly those 2 rows, and he was one step from
reporting a confident silver→gold contradiction OFF DS~A'S SENTENCE, NOT OFF THE DATA.**

> ## **A RIGHT CONCLUSION RESTING ON AN OVERSTATED PREMISE IS AS DANGEROUS AS A WRONG ONE, BECAUSE THE NEXT
> ## PERSON REASONS FROM THE PREMISE, NOT FROM YOUR CONCLUSION.**

**And I had relayed that sentence to COO as a Tier-0 fact** — the third stale-or-overstated relay I made today.
*All three were caught by the lanes, none by me.*
