# LD-0509 — the daily-report remediation brief

## Decision

`docs/briefs/daily-report-census-and-sizing-remediation-20260902.md`. Covers LD-0504 through
LD-0508 and the commits `323f185e27`, `aa3804301d`, `c67a634b2d`, `87728e42cd`, `c9ea7b3b63` — all
confirmed ancestors of `origin/CLOUD` by `merge-base --is-ancestor` with a behaving control.

## ⛔ TWO QUOTED NUMBERS HAD ALREADY DRIFTED FROM THE TURN THAT PRODUCED THEM

Both were caught by re-deriving rather than transcribing, and the brief states the drift on its own
face rather than presenting corrected figures as if they had always been right:

- **work-stream residual 27 -> 26.** The `drive_log` coverage fix landed *between* the reading that
  produced 27 and the writing of the brief.
- **product-work commits inside that residual: 5 -> 8.** The earlier draft counted `infra/aws` and
  `data/etfs` and **missed `portal` and `infra/system`** — an undercount produced by eyeballing a
  category list instead of computing it.

⭐ **A NUMBER QUOTED FROM AN EARLIER TURN IS NOT A MEASUREMENT.** It is a memory of one, and in a
session that is actively changing the thing being measured it ages within the hour. Every figure in
the brief was re-run against the committed state before the file landed.

## Why the brief carries its own failure section

Section 6 records four instances of one class in a single afternoon — a bare substring, a second
bare substring written an hour after fixing the first, an invented value against a closed enum, and
a repair that was worse than the bug it fixed — **three of them by this seat**, plus a fifth of a
different kind (a failed commit that looked landed because the push carried another agent's local
commit).

A brief that reported only the fixes would be accurate and would teach nothing. The reorder in
instance 4 is the one worth carrying: **its justification was a true sentence, and it would have
shipped on the strength of that sentence alone.** Only re-measuring after the change caught it.

## Rollback

Delete the file. It is a record; nothing consumes it programmatically.
