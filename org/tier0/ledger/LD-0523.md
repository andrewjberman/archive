# LD-0523 - /next-calls: calls and steps together, with the sequence between them

**His word, verbatim and entire:** "I want you to make a skill called /next-calls. The idea is that its a
combination of /open-calls and /next-steps . It assesses next steps but it also assesses open calls, and it
looks at them holistically, in terms of where the open calls sit with regard to the next steps with
recommendations for both and their sequencing."

**Decision.** Author the skill as a strict superset of its two siblings: inherit their qualification rules and
card mechanics unchanged; add the edges (which call gates or shapes which option; which options are FREE), the
re-ranking rule (BLOCKING = gates an option), the SEQUENCE block (his answers and the seat's acts, interleaved,
in order, each line pointing at a card) and the ONE-WORD FORM (the exact string that answers every blocking
call and authorizes the default). The skill writes nothing; effects flow from his reply through the normal laws.

are the fabric's operating instruments) -> this skill file -> the acceptance run at this seat.

**Stakes.** S1: a process instrument committed; reversible by deleting the folder; no gate, no monitor, no
message issued by the skill.

**Rollback plan.** Remove `.claude/skills/next-calls/`; the two siblings are untouched.
