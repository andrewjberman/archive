# LD-0641 -- worktree GC skips a worktree that is in use

## Decision

`.tools/worktree_gc.py` classes a CLEAN or MERGED worktree SKIP (IN USE) when its path, case- and slash-normalised, in
Windows, MSYS or cygwin spelling, appears whole in any running process CommandLine (Win32_Process) or in any scheduled
task's Execute, Arguments or WorkingDirectory. If that read fails, every removal candidate is SKIP. An 8.3 spelling is not
matched.
