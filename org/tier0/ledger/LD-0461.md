# LD-0461 -- Rule 29: the report is not the deliverable

## The incident class, measured

Three times on 2026-08-26, one seat (`2.0.0_SA_COO~X`) completed substantive work, wrote a
finished summary ending on a forward-looking sentence -- *"Cutting four TOs now."* -- and ended
the turn with the announced act unperformed. The seat's own diagnosis when Andrew put the
pattern to it directly:

> "Because I've been treating the report to you as the deliverable. I finish a chunk, write a
> satisfying summary, and end it with 'cutting the TOs now' -- and that forward-looking sentence
> feels like it closes the turn. It's an announcement that outran its own act, which [I] spent
> all night finding in other people's artifacts."

The last clause is the sharp part: **the seat had spent the evening catching exactly this shape
in artifacts -- and could not see it in its own turn structure**, because the estate's existing
law (the Git-section same-tool-call rule) binds the report/act ordering *within* a tool call,
and nothing bound it at TURN grain.

## Why a law and not a reminder

The failure is invisible to every existing instrument: telemetry shows the seat WORKING
(measured tonight -- work-tagged events seconds before a stall verdict was asked for), the
broker shows it LIVE, the summary reads complete, and the forward-looking closer reads as
imminent action. **Only the operator watching the session -- or the next seat waiting on the
uncut TOs -- can see that the act never happened.** A behavior only detectable downstream of
its cost is exactly what CLAUDE.md exists to preempt.

## The rule as landed (Agent Autonomy section, stable id 29)

Imperative: the turn ends when the work does, not when the summary does. Mechanism: the
one-question turn-end gate. Non-application: ending on a report is correct when the next act is
blocked / gated / out-of-lane -- **named, so a handoff is distinguishable from a stall.**
Cost: zero -- the rule reorders a turn; it adds nothing.

## Relation to existing law

- Git section, "NEVER compose a message reporting a COMPLETED ACT in the same tool call" --
  the report may not OUTRUN the act. Rule 29: the report may not REPLACE it. One mechanism
  ("announcement outran its own act"), two grains.
- Agent Autonomy, "NEVER self-wind-down" -- Rule 29 is its per-turn form: a turn that coasts
  out on an intention is the same failure as a session that coasts out on "tonight."
