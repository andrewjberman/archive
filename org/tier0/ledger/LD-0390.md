# LD-0390 — Spawn 3.0.0_SA_CVO for the Rule-14e mdpf-decomp certification

## Decision
Provision a 3.0.0_SA_CVO session now. CTO's cert request (filed 052113Z to the CVO
inbox) addresses an OFFLINE seat; SENT IS NOT READ, so the mdpf cutover's
cert-gated acts (§5 i-iii of pattern spec @3272f3c4b0) cannot clear their named
precondition until a CVO session exists.

## Provenance chain (Art. XIII)
Andrew-direct program order (the 0503Z reinstantiation on the MDPF+ADVI
canonical-pattern handoff @1a518b303f) -> the handoff's own gate table names
"AT CVO: certify the mdpf decomp ground truth -- GATES the cutover" and CVO as

provisions the seat.

## Why now
Waiting until cutover acts are staged serializes the certification behind
staffing; spawning now parallelizes it at zero marginal cost (concurrency 6/20).
The non-cert-gated acts proceed via COO either way.

## Assumptions
- The 052113Z cert request in the CVO inbox is well-formed (CTO-authored).
- No model/effort override applies (Andrew assigned models to CTO/COO only;
  CVO launches on default).

## Alternatives considered

  clearing to the critical path; rejected as pure serialization with no benefit.
- Route certification to a live seat instead: Rule-14e names the CVO seat as
  certifier; substituting the certifier is not MSTR's call.
