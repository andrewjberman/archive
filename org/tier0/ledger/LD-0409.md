# LD-0409 -- site Z freeze #11: bounded answer, and why the fix is an INSTRUMENT rather than a repair

Full record: `org/tier0_exec/0.0.0_AB_MSTR/deliverables/SITEZ_FREEZE_11_INVESTIGATION_AND_FIX_DESIGN_20260813.md`

## The determination this ledgers

**The recurring failure class on that box is structurally undiagnosable after the fact.** Freeze #11
is a *both-zero* Event-41 (no bugcheck, no held power button, no dump) -- the 5th such event in 24.7
days. The four freezes that *were* attributed carry `MANUALLY_INITIATED_CRASH`, which records **a
human forcing a dump**, not what wedged the box. **They were attributable only because someone was
standing there.** AutoElevate is now verified gone and the box froze anyway.

**Hang vs power-loss remains genuinely open**, and both instruments are spent: `PowerButtonTimestamp`
was never conclusive alone, and the downtime axis is confounded because the candidate "unattended"
recoveries proved attended. **A hung box and a powered-off box are indistinguishable if nobody
comes.**

**That bound is the decision-relevant output**, not a failure. It is what converts the ask from
"find the driver" -- which cannot succeed for this class -- into "be able to observe the next one."

## What is recommended (NOT implemented)

Two instruments, each resolving a different limb, plus the decision tree they make possible:

| observation at freeze #12 | conclusion |
|---|---|
| UPS logs **input** failure | building/circuit power -- cause found **and prevented** |
| clean input, **output load ~0 W** | box powered itself off -> internal PSU/VRM |
| clean input, load normal, unreachable | **total hang** |
| clean input, load normal, reachable but wedged | **partial hang** -- force the dump remotely |

⛔ **SPEC IS LOAD-BEARING: a MONITORED UPS (network card, or USB + monitoring software), NOT a bare
battery backup.** A dumb UPS prevents the power limb and **diagnoses nothing** -- it would have left
this investigation byte-for-byte identical. The output-load reading is the only thing that separates
*powered-off* from *hung* remotely, and without it two of four outcomes remain "we cannot tell."

## Standing risks recorded, true regardless of how #11 resolves

1. No UPS/battery on the **sole Bloomberg Terminal and sole producer of a client-facing dashboard**.
2. **`AutoAdminLogon` NOT SET while the pull requires an interactive session** -- every reboot
   silently disables the Bloomberg pipeline until a human logs in. Green task, no error.
3. **RDP-then-disconnect leaves the console locked** -- a second path into that identical state, and
   the reason remote-in must first be exercised *after* a 07:00 pull.
4. **`/kill apps` targets `tailscale` by name** -- remote access needs an exclusion or a routine
   cleanup severs it.

## Method, recorded because it is the reusable part

Four hypotheses were falsified, **three of them ours**. Every falsification came from **a control or
the other seat -- none from care**, and twice a seat ran the control that killed its own position
(site Z on the 7001 human-presence oracle; me on CPU-thermal). The two errors that survived longest
were the same shape in both directions: **two states that look identical because the observer cannot
get to the box** -- which is also, exactly, the defect the fix is designed to remove.

**And the cheapest instrument was never used:** *who was at the box on 08-13, and did they find it
OFF or FROZEN with a lit power LED?* That question has been available since 17:37 and settles
outright what three hours of log work could not. Both seats went to the logs because the logs are
what we could reach.
