# LD-0206 — CLAUDE.md Rule 22: mechanism-grounding governance gate (Andrew-direct, ledgered)

## Decision
Record, as Tier-0 governance, **CLAUDE.md Rule 22** — the *mechanism-grounding gate*.
The rule is Andrew-direct and was already LAW on commit `53919cb32`; this entry puts
the fabric-wide evidentiary/decision change in the **ledger trail** (it had been
anchored only by the doctrine doc — appropriate for a tooling rule, insufficient
discoverability for a governance gate that alters how every Tier-1 owner authorizes
decisions). Andrew confirmed the ledgering ("Yes ledger it 0206").

## The gate
A **causal / mechanism claim** ("root cause is X", "it's because Y", "the seal is
stuck", "won't self-heal") is a **HYPOTHESIS until SOURCE/GRAPH-confirmed**, and a
hypothesis **cannot authorize a fix, ruling, dispatch, or cert.**

## Two grounding modes (the key distinction)
- **STATE / delivery claim** ("served AUA is 06-09", "the deploy landed", "artifact
  is fresh") → ground vs **DEPLOYED / LIVE / SERVED**, never source (committed !=
  deployed). **Unchanged** — this is the existing verification rule.
- **MECHANISM / why claim** → ground vs **SOURCE + the dependency GRAPH**
  (`comp-pipescript io` / `comp-blast` / Read the producer), **CITED**. Output is the
  *symptom* and underdetermines the mechanism; reading source for a mechanism is
  **mandatory**, not a "trust the code over served" violation.

## The owner-withhold gates
- **DISPATCH / RULING (COO/CTO):** no GO on a fix/TO/ruling resting on an ungrounded
  causal claim — withheld until it cites source+graph (`file:line` or
  comp-tool+output).
- **FINDING / CERT (CVO):** verification findings (molecule / seams / verify-channel)
  carry a mandatory `grounding: SOURCE-CONFIRMED <ref> | INFERRED` field on each
  causal claim; absent or `INFERRED` = **not accepted** as a determination.
- **RESERVED WORDS (all):** "proof / provably / root cause / definitively" are legal
  **only** on a source-confirmed claim.
- **"WHY"-trigger:** for any "why is X behaving like this", the FIRST move is
  `comp-pipescript io` / `comp-blast` / Read on X's producer — ground the mechanism
  BEFORE constructing a story from its output.

## Why (incident)
Born 2026-06-22 from the **COTETL canonical-seal mis-inference**: a correctly-grounded
*symptom* (served cot AUA frozen) plus an *invented* mechanism (a "stuck canonical
seal that won't self-heal") was escalated to "proof" and nearly drove a needless
same-day fix — caught only by a code-read that falsified the mechanism. (Compare the
related, correctly-grounded work the same session: the comp-log TO_ID race [[LD-0205]],
whose "root cause" WAS source-confirmed via a producer code-read + grep + empirical
test — the contrast is the doctrine.)

## Relationship to NFRA_COMPRAIL
This is the **CONCLUSION layer** of the same connection-blindness thesis behind the
comp-rail: `comp-grep`/`comp-glob` + Rule 21 are the **lookup** layer (find the
coupled thing); Rule 22 is the **reasoning** layer (don't conclude a mechanism from a
symptom without reading the producer + graph). Same root insight — text/output-local
reasoning misses the real causal structure.
