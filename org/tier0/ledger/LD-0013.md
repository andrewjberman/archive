# LD-0013 — Amend /govern Step 1(b) population-scan rubric with project-status predicate

## Decision
Add a project-status cross-check to the `/govern` Step 1(b) population scan's
"unaddressed work surface" detection. Before a TO-queue-with-no-LIVE-owner
qualifies as a spawn candidate, the scan must read the owning project's
`status` field in `org/projects/{CODE}.json`:

| Project status | Spawn-candidate eligibility |
|---|---|
| `active` | YES -- spawn is in scope (existing rubric) |
| `dormant` / `on-hold` / `needs-sovereign-call` | NO -- surface to Andrew as a proposal; pending TOs in a dormant project are parked-pending-sovereign-judgment, not unaddressed work |
| `complete` / `cancelled` / `superseded` | NO -- skip; stale TOs from a closed project are queue-cleanup work for COO, not a population gap |

The rubric refinement was empirically demanded by the ADVI_INFGRO surgical
rollback 2026-05-23:
- CY-0005 spawn of `0.4.0_DE_ClientPlatform` for `ADVI_INFGRO.005 + .006` was
  rubric-correct under the existing scan (pending TOs + no LIVE owner = spawn).
- ADVI_INFGRO was on CTO's 2026-05-22 13:03Z dormant-cluster list awaiting an
  Andrew resume/cancel call. Andrew never resumed; the TOs lingered in the
  queue from before dormancy was flagged.
- Andrew then cancelled ADVI_INFGRO 2026-05-23 (the targeted UI surface was
  49F-only, not visible on Inflection); LD-0009's work product was reverted;
  DE_ClientPlatform killed by CTO.
- The spawn correctly executed the rubric. The RUBRIC missed the dormant-
  project signal that would have caught the case before the spawn.

The amendment closes the gap structurally rather than relying on after-the-
fact catches. The scan reads project status; the project status itself
carries Andrew's wisdom-of-restraint.

## Why now
The gap is freshly empirically demonstrated. ADVI_INFGRO rollback is the
worked example; codifying the amendment in the same /mstr_review window as
the rollback ensures future cycles inherit the corrected rubric immediately.
The /spot-check substance-meta-check landed structurally on CVO's side
(LD-0012 Part 2 ratified); landing the population-scan refinement on my side
keeps the meta-supervision architecture coherent across both verifiers.

## Assumptions
- Every active project in `org/projects/REGISTRY.md` is represented by a
  `{CODE}.json` with a `status` field. (Confirmed via the REGISTRY format.)
- Dormant / on-hold projects can be detected by JSON `status` field; CTO's
  CTO-to-COO dormant-cluster work surfaces these.
- The rubric amendment does not eliminate human judgment -- it surfaces
  the dormant-project case to Andrew as a proposal (S4-leaning) rather
  than auto-spawning. Andrew decides whether to resume the project or
  let the TOs age.

## Alternatives considered
- **Spawn AND then quickly evaluate; kill if dormant** -- rejected: wasted
  spawn slot + audit noise; the project-status read is cheap and precedes
  the spawn cleanly.
- **Always surface unaddressed-surface candidates to Andrew before
  spawning** -- rejected: defeats the autonomous-supply purpose; Andrew
  said spawn for legitimate unaddressed surfaces, just respect project
  status.
- **Add a more general dormancy detector** (e.g., commit-activity-since
  threshold) -- rejected: project status IS the canonical signal; COO
  maintains it; reading it is the correct path. A general dormancy
  detector would parallel the formal status and risk diverging.

predicate produces false-positives. The lived experience of the next
