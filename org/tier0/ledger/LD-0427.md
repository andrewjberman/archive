the role). Standing duties, scoped loop authorizations, and their stop conditions live solely in
`org/XXX.md` Step 1b-F; the launcher defers to that row and never carries a cadence, a dated
incident, a verbatim, or supersession history. This is STANDING launcher-authorship law.
