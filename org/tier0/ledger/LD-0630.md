# LD-0630 -- a quiet live seat keeps its liveness row (server.py owner fix)

## Decision
Site Z measured the cause (bus 4cef201a, 3b4cbbce, f0b81176). 0.0.3_BG_BBGTerminal went quiet, and after 10 minutes with no
heartbeat any other seat's `_clean_stale` (`DEAD_THRESHOLD` 600 s) deleted its row. After that, `heartbeat` answered "not
registered" and `get_agent_status` answered NOT REGISTERED, with the PID unchanged. This happened 3 times in 2 hours.

broker.py already drops an agent only when its PID dies. server.py now does the same: past 600 s it prunes only when
`_pid_alive(pid)` is false, and past `DEAD_HARD_CAP` (86400 s) it prunes regardless.

## Verification
Five arms run against the real module:
- quiet seat with a live PID: kept;
- dead PID: removed;
- fresh row: kept;
- row older than 24 h: removed;
- no PID: removed.

The 18:37Z parent watchdog (e6f1274d17) was ruled out: site Z found no replaced server process.

## Rollback
`git revert` of the commit.
