# LD-0204 — COO /drive auto-arm carve-out to LD-0175 (Andrew-direct, formalized)

## Decision
LD-0175 (Andrew-direct 2026-06-10: every serial arms ZERO crons at launch; loops
are operator-initiated) is **amended by a single targeted carve-out**: the **COO
(`2.x.0_SA_COO`) AUTO-ARMS `/drive` at launch** (20m recurring cron + one cycle
now). Rationale: the COO is the fabric's PRIMARY OPERATIONAL DRIVER (LD-0056), and
a COO that only answers its inbox reactively — without proactively spawning needed
agents, dispatching staged TOs, scanning idle capacity, and policing
TO/RAVC2/registry — is the failure Andrew rebuked 2026-06-18 ("you have been
categorically failing in your responsibilities to drive the fabric").

**Scope of the carve-out:** COO `/drive` ONLY. **ZERO-loops-at-launch (LD-0175)
remains in full force for every other serial** — MSTR (zero crons), CTO, CVO,
all workers, and the CL fabric. No other loop auto-arms.
