# LD-0614 -- the presence stamp for the FAST lease (report 2 call 5)

Andrew-direct ~15:3xZ at the 4.0.0_SA_CRO~A terminal, verbatim: "All of these recs are approved." Item 5 of the
record: "Lease: (a) 2 hours by default, plus a one-line prompt hook that stamps a presence timestamp and extends a live
lease while he is typing, so fast mode ends about 30 minutes after he stops. The hook writes a timestamp only; it is
not a monitor or an alert." Report 2 (RCN-0017) C1 row: "`0.0.0_AB_MSTR` (the optional presence hook)". Relayed to
this seat by 4.0.0_SA_CRO~A msg 100750.

Two commits:
1. `org/mcp/presence/.gitignore` alone, under the audited `GIT_COMMIT_AUTHOR_OVERRIDE=BASE_EXEC` (org/mcp/** is
   BASE_EXEC's catch-all). The one-off cross-lane escape is the ruling recorded in `.owned-paths.yaml`'s D0-T1 grant.
2. `.tools/hooks/presence-stamp.sh` + the `.claude/settings.json` UserPromptSubmit entry, in this seat's own lanes.

The contract the lease reader consumes is in the script's header.

Progress 1 (15:5xZ): commit 1 landed as 148b0777f8. Commit 2 carries this line together with the script and the
settings entry.

Progress 2 (16:0xZ): the content is EPOCH SECONDS, not ISO. 1.0.0_SA_CTO's contract (msg 100753) and its reader
(`presence_last()` in .tools/drive-mode.py, on cto-report2-c1c9, uncommitted) strip non-digits and parse the rest as
epoch. An ISO stamp therefore read as ~2.0e13, a far-future time that would hold a FAST lease open indefinitely. That
was an exposure only: commit 2 was never merged, and no lease existed (org/mcp/drive_mode absent). The one ISO test
stamp in the main tree was deleted. Re-exercised with the REAL reader, loaded from the CTO's worktree: presence_last
= the stamp, delta 0 s, within the 30-minute grace. Filename: <device>.last. The reader globs *.last, so the
CTO's "<hostname>" wording is not load-bearing.
