# LD-0554 - The audit archive minted nothing for 533 hours, and the fix was right on one device and wrong on the other

## What was broken

`.tools/audit-archive-invoker.py` mints into `archive/<DEVICE>/<TS>/` and **refuses until that chain
carries its own `.baseline`**. Nothing ever created the chain. So every run returned
`deferred: DEVICE-CHAIN-PENDING`, and **the estate archived NO audit evidence from 2026-08-20 to
2026-09-11.**

⭐ **THE REPORTED SYMPTOM WAS NOT THE DEFECT.** `3.0.0_SA_CVO`'s startup flagged two logs with missing
baselines. Three readings died in order, each to one measurement:

| the plausible reading | what killed it |
|---|---|
| that snapshot is corrupt | the two files are absent from **all 29** snapshots |
| the archiver stopped 22 days ago | its heartbeat was written **12 minutes** before I looked |
| it is wedged on those two errors | its own dry run said `DEVICE-CHAIN-PENDING` |

The two missing baselines were **downstream**: the logs entered `MANIFEST` after the last successful
snapshot, and no snapshot could mint, so their baseline could never appear. A deadlock the instrument
had been printing since **2026-09-05** with nobody reading.

## The ruling (`1.0.0_SA_CTO`, `6560583fd5`) — verified at source, not relayed on trust

- **(b) durable:** a chain whose ONLY snapshot is the seeded shared baseline mints its first snapshot
  as a **RE-BASELINE** (all logs from that device's live bytes, nothing pinned) and writes
  `.rebaseline` into the snapshot as the record. Future devices are covered with no recipe.
- **(a) repair:** `archive/<device>/.rebaseline-once` makes the NEXT mint a re-baseline and is
  **consumed in that mint's own commit** — `marker.unlink()` at `:486`, the deletion committed via the
  pathspec at `:487`.

⭐ **I checked `:486` specifically.** Had the marker been committed but never unlinked, **every future
mint on that device would be a re-baseline and pinning would be permanently OFF** — worse than the
defect being repaired, and it would read as success every time.

⚠ **A trap the ruling does not name:** the marker **cannot bring a mint forward**. The interval check
returns `skip` at `:424`, **before** the marker is read at `:435`, so an early run skips without
consuming it. That looks like a failed repair, and the reflex — re-run, or hand-edit — is exactly what
must not happen here. Site Z was told plainly that a skip before ~22:32Z is correct.
