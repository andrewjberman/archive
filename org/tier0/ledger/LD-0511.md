# LD-0511 — the `undefined` gap closed where it actually binds, and the archive rescued

## Decision

`check_viz_params()` in `render_daily_report.py`, run beside `validate()`. Nine viz types, their
DATA-BEARING params required; `link` additionally checks `left.t/.s` and `right.t/.s`.

## Why it is not in the schema

`schema/DAILY_REPORT.schema.json` declares `"params": {"type": "object"}` — unconstrained. But the
deeper reason is `LD-0510`'s finding: **nothing loads that file.** `jsonschema` appears zero times
in `daily_reports/`. **A per-type constraint added to the schema would have been a no-op** — the
gap would have looked closed and still shipped `undefined` to a page.

## The requirements were READ, not imagined

Each generator in `_shared_viz.js` was parsed for what it dereferences off `p`. Two were read in
full to settle ambiguity, and they justify the whole check:

- `coverage` does `Math.round(n * p.pct)` -> **NaN**, and passes `p.label` straight to `TXT()` ->
  paints the literal word **"undefined"** on the page.
- `link` dereferences `p.left.t`, `p.left.s`, `p.right.t`, `p.right.s` — nested, so presence of
  `left` alone is not enough.

⚠ **THE CHECK DECLARES ITSELF A FLOOR, NOT A CEILING.** It covers data-bearing keys; optional
editorial ones (`caption`, `footer`, `break_after`) are not required — though `coverage` paints its
caption unguarded, a gap left **named rather than papered over**, because forcing a caption on
every coverage card is a content decision and this is a structural check. **A viz type absent from
the map is UNCHECKED, which is a different claim from checked-and-clean.**

## ⛔ THE POSITIVE CONTROL CAUGHT A REGRESSION I HAD ALREADY SHIPPED

Running "does every committed day still render" — the control, not the new feature — returned
**FAILS on all five** (08-27 .. 08-31): `unknown commit_kind: 'ARCHITECTURE'`.

The 09-02 enum rewrite (LD-0506/0507) had made **the entire archive unrenderable.** Those JSONs are
correct records of what was measured under the enum in force on their date.

⭐ **RETIRING A VALUE GOVERNS WHAT THE CENSUS EMITS; IT MUST NOT GOVERN WHAT A PAST RECORD MAY
SAY.** A validator that rejects its own archive has confused *"no longer produced"* with *"never
valid."* Fixed with an explicit `LEGACY_KINDS = {"ARCHITECTURE", "OPERATOR"}` — accepted on read,
never emitted — and matching legacy entries kept in the template's colour map.

**This is the second time today that a change looked complete and was caught by a control rather
than by review** (the first: the rule reorder in LD-0508). Both times the artifact under test
passed; the thing that failed was something adjacent that nobody had thought to re-run.

## Controls

```
  FALSIFIERS -- one per viz type, drop the data-bearing key, ALL SIX FIRE:
    screens/shots · coverage/pct · steps/steps · compare/rows · flow/stages · link/left
  POSITIVE CONTROL -- the five committed days 08-27..08-31: ALL RENDER (after the legacy fix)
  POSITIVE CONTROL -- the new-enum 09-01 file with streams: RENDERS
```

## Rollback

Revert this commit. The check is additive; `LEGACY_KINDS` only widens what is accepted.
