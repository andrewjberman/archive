## What was wrong, measured

The published 09-15 report's own footer said it plainly: *"The second machine's work today is
described from its reports... its commits count at the next merge."* That was accurate when
written -- the CLOUD<->SITEZ merge (LD-0579) had not yet landed on this box's local git graph, so
`git log --all` on this box literally could not see origin/SITEZ's history yet. `team_activity`
carried 347 commits / 39 workers and a `by_project` breakdown covering only 6 of 15 directives
(missing D1, D5, D6, D7, D10, D11, D12 entirely -- all the ones a fully-merged history would show
site-Z work under).

Re-ran the two census scripts (`commit_census.py`, `project_census.py`) against the now-merged
history: 790 commits, 66 distinct agent identities. `by_project` now carries D4=139 (was 1), D5=181
(was absent), D12=2 (was absent), alongside the previously-visible laptop directives. Every number
was cross-checked two ways before trusting it: (1) `commit_census.py`'s total (790) equals
`project_census.py`'s total (790) independently; (2) attributed-by-project (666) + unattributed
(124) = 790 exactly, both as a scalar and summed per 30-minute timeline bin -- the same
discrimination the skill's own rule requires before merging two censuses' timelines verbatim.
