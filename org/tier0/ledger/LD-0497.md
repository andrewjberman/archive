## ⭐ THE REGISTER WAS CARRYING OPEN FACES OVER FINISHED WORK, AND ONLY HE COULD SAY SO

This is the **stale-face class the fabric has been finding all night**, at directive scale: an
`[ACTIVE]` row and genuinely open work **render identically**, and no instrument in the estate
discriminates them — every count tonight that tried was later withdrawn. **The sovereign is the
discriminator here.** He is the authority on whether the work is done; the register is not.

⛔ **NOTHING WAS RE-EXECUTED AND NO ACCEPTANCE WAS RE-RUN.** A world-done item **closes**; it does not
re-run (NEVER-2). `ETFS_SATELLITES` was skipped rather than re-stamped, because writing `complete`
over `complete` would put a 2026-09-01 provenance line on a closure that happened earlier.

## The placement revert was PRE-AUTHORISED BY THE ROW ITSELF

The superseded row recorded, in its own text, three things that made this act mechanical rather than
a judgement call:
- its bound had **changed KIND** — from a DATE (*"today only"*) to a **COMPLETION CONDITION**;
- **a date-bounded placement expires silently and correctly; a completion-bounded one NEVER EXPIRES
  and must be reverted DELIBERATELY**, and that revert is `0.0.0_AB_MSTR`'s act as register owner;
- the trigger would be **an EVENT NOBODY HAD YET DEFINED**.

**Andrew has now defined the event.** ⭐ **The revert target was not chosen now** — the superseded row
carried it verbatim (*"LAPTOP-OWNED — RULED (Andrew-direct 2026-08-21 ~05:5xZ, CTO~A terminal)"*), so
the row returns where it came from rather than to a placement invented at revert time. Sixth
placement on this row, all his.

## ⛔ WHAT THIS DOES NOT DO

**The four open D4 task orders are now STALE FACES over finished work.** ⛔ **Closing a TO face is its
ISSUER's act, never the register owner's** — they are SURFACED to their issuers, not closed here.
Creates zero spawns, cuts no TO, seats nobody, and moves no other directive.
