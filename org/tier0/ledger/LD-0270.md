# LD-0270 -- PTNR/CCAP architecture brief from the Cottonwood dogfood, issued to CTO

**What.** The empirical half of the `PTNR_STRUCTURE` PRD, sent to CTO as a committed artifact:
`docs/briefs/ptnr-architecture-brief-from-ccap-dogfood-20260709.md`. It records what a real partner's data
(Christa Bass / Cottonwood, 5 sheets + annotations + the Commonwealth upstream thread) did to a hand-built
proto-`PTNR` structure this session -- the two-store raw/distilled architecture, the manifest bridge, the
full schema, the ingest+verify pipeline, 8 findings a design session would have missed, the confidentiality
model, the prerequisites, and a 5-item ask.

**Why it is a co-equal half, not a footnote.** LD-0268 is intent, authored before any data ran through.
This is validation -- the structure held, and every failure it hit is a real acceptance criterion (the
inception mask, cross-foot-by-construction, unreadable!=empty, scope-as-denominator, derive-don't-assert).
The dogfood also surfaced two live tool defects (M365 gateway reporting an unreadable file as empty; the
confidentiality lint that does not yet exist) routed to CTO for the PRD.

**The through-line.** Every partner-data finding is the session's own defect class in a new surface:
existence!=content, value!=denominator, rendered!=measured, reported!=derived, sent!=reached. The brief
sells our own governing discipline -- derive the reported value from the source; state your denominator;
never maintain two expressions of one truth -- as the `CCAP_FEEINT` partner deliverable.

**Stakes.** S1, reversible, a design input. The confidentiality half (raw out of repo, separate buckets,
commit-lint) is the risk-reduction: it forecloses rebuilding the `opal-data-schwab` one-bucket PII exposure
in the partner-data surface.
