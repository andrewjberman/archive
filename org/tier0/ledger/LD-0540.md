# LD-0540 - Minting 1.7.0_SA_KRN, and the two acts a spawn actually is

## Decision

Mint `1.7.0_SA_KRN`, live lane `~Y`, as a C3 boundary / deterministic-kernel producer seat
reporting to `1.0.0_SA_CTO~Y`. Grant its home a dedicated `KRN_WORKSPACE` lane.

## The serial was measured, not chosen by convention

`1.7.0` is the next free slot after `1.1.0`-`1.6.0` (KJ, A0, ADP, GATE, FI, TRN).

| check | result |
|---|---|
| `1.7.0_SA` across the estate | 0 hits / 154,521 files |
| `1.6.0_SA`, same instrument, as positive control | 5,101 hits |
| process truth for the target serial | 0 of 5 live `claude.exe` |
| spawn markers for the full `~Y` serial | 0 |
| concurrency | 1 of 20, cap read at source |

⛔ **THE FIRST CENSUS WAS BROKEN AND THE CONTROL IS WHY I KNOW.** My initial pattern was escaped,
the shell doubled the backslashes, and the `1.7.0` half tested a literal backslash. It returned a
confident zero that meant nothing. Only the `KRN` half of that alternation was ever valid. A zero
with no positive control beside it is not a measurement.

## ⭐ A SPAWN IS TWO ACTS AND ONLY ONE OF THEM IS THE SPAWN

This is the durable finding and it cost the delay.

1. **The RUNTIME.** `/mstr-spawn`'s launcher is `spawn-agent.ps1`, which runs `ajb -Headless` and
   provisions a **Claude** seat. It contains **zero** references to codex or `ajb2`. A `~Y` seat is
   a **Codex** seat and launches through `org/mcp/start-codex-seat.ps1`. Using the skill as written
   would have handed a Codex issuer a Claude peer.
   The loader also resolves a lane by finding its **base serial's** directory
   (`codex-seat-load.py:37` splits on `~`, `:46-49` walks `org/tier*/`), so the home must exist
   **before** launch or the seat starts unable to read its own context.

2. **The GRANT.** The seat came up fine without any `.owned-paths.yaml` entry, because the loader
   reads the **filesystem**. The commit gate then refused its own scaffolding with
   `UNDEFINED (no matching path rule)`. **A directory on disk and an ownership rule are different
   acts, and only the second makes the seat's work committable.** A seat that can run and cannot
   commit is a seat whose output dies with its window.

**Both belong in the same turn as the spawn.** Neither `/mstr-spawn` nor the seat READMEs say so.

## The grant follows the CRO precedent exactly

`KRN_WORKSPACE` names the seat, its issuer, its validator, and MSTR. The issuer and validator are
enumerated so they can author task orders and review records into the home, mirroring
`CRO_WORKSPACE` where CTO and COO retain write access for gate-review reports. The base serial
covers its tilde lanes because this file's agent side strips one tilde.

**Separation of duties held:** the grantee did not commit its own grant. MSTR is grantor here, the
same role it held for the CRO home on 2026-07-22 under Andrew's SoD ruling.

⛔ My first attempt laned the home `BASE_EXEC` and the gate refused it, correctly: `BASE_EXEC` is
CTO, COO and CVO, which excludes both MSTR and the seat itself. A lane that contains neither the
minter nor the occupant is not a home.

## Not a revival

`1.0.0_SA_CTO~Y` retired the six Phase 2 producer lanes the same day. Their charters, history and
worktrees are preserved untouched and this seat inherits none of their assignments. The issuer
asked for a fresh serial in those words.

## Rollback

`/mstr-kill 1.7.0_SA_KRN~Y` ends the session; the home and the lane can be reverted in one commit.
The audit trail preserves both the spawn and any later kill.
