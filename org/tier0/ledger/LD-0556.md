# LD-0556 - Stopping a box that ran 31 days, and the three controls that called it healthy

## The acts

On Andrew's explicit word, after a read-only investigation:

1. **135 `orion-gold-dispatch.sh` processes killed** on `i-0ed5afded2ebd4ed4`. All died on a plain
   `TERM`; `KILL` was never needed. ⭐ **They were not busy with anything they were unwilling to
   abandon** — after holding a 16 vCPU box for 31 days.
2. **`inf-gold-layer-runner` (`c6a.4xlarge`) stopped.** Verified: all four inflection instances read
   `stopped`.

**Full finding:** `docs/briefs/inflection-gold-runner-31-day-overrun-20260911.md`.

## Why this is a ledger entry and not just a brief

Stopping an EC2 instance is on CLAUDE.md's human-confirmation list. **Andrew gave that confirmation
directly and it is quoted in full in `andrew_instruction_ref`.** The entry exists so the act has an
owner and a rollback, not because the judgement was close.

## What it cost and what it did not

| | |
|---|---|
| uptime at stop | **31 days 13 hours** (since 2026-08-11T05:14:20Z) |
| hung processes | **135**, oldest started **37 seconds after boot** |
| derived cost | **~$450 spent, ~$440/month ongoing** — ⚠ instance-type × uptime, **Cost Explorer NOT read** |
| gold output | **CURRENT** — today's outputs landed 12:41–12:43. **A leak, not an outage.** |
| duplicate pipelines | **NONE.** 30 of 33 instances were already stopped; this box was the outlier. |

## ⛔ The finding worth keeping

**Three independent controls reported health for a month:**

1. **The watchdog** shuts the box down when no gold process is running. Its regex matches
   `orion-gold-dispatch` — **the stuck processes ARE its proof of life.** It saw 135 hung dispatchers
   and correctly skipped shutdown, every 5 minutes, for 31 days.
2. **SSM** reported `Success` on every daily dispatch. `Success` means **delivered and started**,
   never finished. A dispatcher that hangs forever still reads Success.
3. **The status writer** produced `overall: ok` while carrying `chain_exit: 1` inside the same object,
   11 seconds after the same run wrote a failure marker with `exit_code: 1`.

>> **NONE OF THEM MEASURED DURATION OR OUTCOME. ALL OF THEM MEASURED PRESENCE.** A control that asks
   *"is something there"* cannot detect *"the thing there is broken"* — and it **fails toward
   reassurance**, which is why nobody looked for 31 days. <<

⚠ **And the boot script DELETES the watchdog units from four systemd directories by design** — stated
reason, preventing shutdown before work arrives. **Yet the timer measured ACTIVE and logging.** That
contradiction is unresolved and is recorded as unresolved.

## ⛔ Still armed

`comp-trigger` shows all three sanctioned EventBridge rules **DISABLED**, while **two senders dispatch
daily via SSM outside that path** (`source=aggregator`, `assets_v2 ... AUDIT-008.PERM`). **Stopping
the box does not stop them.** The next dispatch may start it again, and the pileup would restart from
zero with the same watchdog logic.

**Routed to `0.7.0_AE_CloudOps`** (`review_required_by`) — `infra/aws/**` is that lane, not this seat's.

## Rollback

`aws ec2 start-instances` restores the instance; nothing was terminated, no volume deleted, no
configuration changed. **The killed processes are not recoverable and were not intended to be** — they
were hung, producing nothing, and the daily chains that matter completed independently of them.
