# LD-0464 -- the daily sprint becomes an image; the register takes Andrew's names

## Why the format changed

The sprint was a Teams text post, and Teams' renderer had forced a growing pile of workarounds --
`&nbsp;` gap-holders, `---` rules, and a spacer bullet whose payload was a NON-BREAKING SPACE that
a reader was explicitly told not to "tidy". Andrew's call ends the class rather than adding to it:
an image renders identically everywhere.

> **THE TRANSFERABLE PROPERTY: a presentation constraint that lives in PROSE is a protection that
> exists only as a description.** Every one of those rules could be silently removed by a
> well-meaning edit, and the damage would appear only at the next paste, at the reader. Moving
> presentation into `render_sprint.py` makes the layout unbreakable by editing -- the same move
> the estate keeps making when it converts an imperative into a gate.

## What LOOKING at the render caught (both invisible to a passing self-report)

1. **PROCESS rendered EMPTY** -- the day's file still carried the retired table form, which the
   item parser did not match. **An empty section is indistinguishable from an absent one at the
   reader**, so the renderer now parses both shapes and PRINTS per-section line counts every run.
2. **The status pill lied in both directions** -- a substring classifier matched `rest` inside
   "restore" and `complete` inside "completed", stamping IDLE on a shipping item and ON TRACK on
   one that is idle by design. This is the estate's common-noun-token trap inside a renderer.
   Rewritten to word-boundary TOKEN matching with specific-before-general precedence; six arms
   unit-tested, 6 of 6.

⛔ **Neither defect was visible in the tool's own output.** The render exited 0 both times. They
were found by rendering the page in a browser and reading it -- the served-artifact discipline
applied to a document.

## The register rename

All 12 titles become Andrew's operating-report names; timing qualifiers ("~2wks", "Fri PM target")
drop out because PROCESS now carries them in its own Timeline field. **YCharts folds into D9 as
"Tax SMA Ops + YCharts"** per his word -- it had no project of its own and no register home.

**The consequence is mechanical, not cosmetic:** the skill's tie-to-the-headliner rule was a
paraphrase test and is now a STRING MATCH -- if an item name and its directive title differ, one
of them is wrong, and that is checkable.
