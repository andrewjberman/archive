# LD-0338 — NOTHING OWNS THE DECAY OF A TRUE STATEMENT

**Surfaced by `2.0.0_SA_COO` (154943Z), framed by `1.0.0_SA_CTO`, filed by MSTR under retained record
custody.** COO explicitly declined to staff it and left the judgment to me: *"if you judge it worth a
ledger entry, it is yours; if you judge it worth nothing until someone is bitten harder, that is a
defensible read too."*

**I judge it worth the entry and NOT worth a lane today.** The record costs one commit; a sweep would
justify itself into scope on the day Andrew's close-out is holding at 22 dates plus the render, and
CTO has held that line twice today after being told he widens.

## The claim

> **We have a strong, well-exercised convention for RETRACTING a WRONG record. We have NO convention
> for EXPIRING a record that was RIGHT and has been overtaken by events.**

The asymmetry has a mechanism. A **wrong** record has an author who is embarrassed, a correction that is
loud, and an event that triggers it. An **aged** record has **no author who feels responsible and no
event that triggers review.** It sits being quietly false **with full authority** — and it is *more*
dangerous than a wrong one **precisely because its provenance is clean.** It was verified, by someone
competent, and the verification is still attached to it.

CTO's framing, carried rather than improved: ***"That is not three mistakes, that is a maintenance model
that does not exist."***

## The three instances — one hour, three carriers, all CORRECT WHEN WRITTEN

| # | record | what changed under it | near-cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **"0.2.0_DT KILLED 07-20"** (fabric record / MEMORY.md) | lane was respawned; ran the v2.1.148 promote 07-23 | **CTO nearly concluded promote authority had moved** |
| 2 | **"roster: only 8 partitions, a point-in-time roster, not a history"** (07-22 hold record) | DS~A measured **11** accumulating from the same start — *the feed's own behaviour changed under a true statement about it* | scoping built on a stale shape |
| 3 | **a `/49-schwab-conv` skill row** (OneDrive is the authoritative delivery channel) | the monolith supersedes it | **on that row alone the answer to "have the Schwab files been delivered" is ABSENT — which would have sent ANDREW to download data already sitting in bronze** |

**Instance 3 is the one that prices this entry.** The cost was not an agent's confusion; it was the
**sovereign's time**, spent on work already done, on the authority of a record that was true when written.

**Detection rate: LUCK.** All three were caught by an agent tripping over them mid-task. **None by any
process.** Against ~93 memory topics plus the ledger plus the skills plus the fabric record.

### Instance 1 verified at source before correcting (Rule 22)

I did not correct the DT line on COO's report. Ground truth: **43 commits authored
`0.2.0_DT_PortfolioAnalytics` since 2026-07-21**, including four PROD promotes on 07-23
(`v2.1.146/147/148`, portal pointer `@343e7b2a`). The record is decayed, not disputed. Corrected in
MEMORY.md this session with the correction *attached to the stale claim* rather than replacing it, so a
reader who remembers the old line finds out why it changed.

**It had also been self-contradicting for four days:** MEMORY.md line 19 read `KILLED 07-20` while line
30 read `ACTIVE`. **Two clauses in one always-loaded file, disagreeing, and nobody read them together** —
because nobody reads a reference file end-to-end; they grep it. **An internal contradiction is not
self-detecting when the carrier is consulted by lookup.**

## Why this is NOT the class the fabric chased all day

Today's governing class was *"the thing you believe happened, didn't"* — seven instances, all of them
**verification pointed at the wrong layer**. This is a different failure:

> **That class is "we verified the wrong thing." THIS class is "we verified the RIGHT thing, correctly,
> and then the world moved."** No amount of verification rigour at write-time prevents it. **Rigour is
> not the remedy — a re-read trigger is.**

They are siblings only in outcome. Conflating them would send the fix to the wrong place: more careful
verification would have produced all three of these records **exactly as they were written.**

## What I am NOT doing

- **NOT staffing a sweep.** Scope discipline holds; the close-out owns the fabric's attention today.
- **NOT designing an expiry mechanism.** That is CTO's/COO's to route when it is theirs to route.
- **NOT adjudicating which carriers need it.** I hold custody, not the design.

## What is worth carrying when someone does price it

- **The trigger is the hard part, not the edit.** Every one of these was a one-line fix once seen. The
  entire cost is *noticing*. A convention that says "review aged records" with no firing event is
  itself a record that will age.
- **Candidate shape, offered not designed:** records asserting a *state* (X is dead / there are N
  partitions / channel C is authoritative) are the decaying class; records asserting a *law* or an
  *incident* are not. **A state-claim in a durable record should carry an as-of and a re-check owner,
  or it should not be phrased as a state.** Instance 2 is the purest form: a true statement about a
  feed's behaviour, invalidated by the feed.
- **Attach corrections to the stale claim, never replace it.** A reader carrying the old belief needs
  to encounter the collision, not a clean line that silently disagrees with their memory.

## Relation to the day's other entries

**[[LD-0337]] says a diagnosis delivered as correspondence installs no enforcement point and therefore
does not reduce recurrence. THIS IS THE SAME SHAPE ONE LAYER OUT:** a *true record* with no expiry
trigger installs no enforcement point either. **In both, the content was right and nothing owned its
future.** LD-0337's remedy is a gate at the writer; this one's would be a trigger at the reader — and
neither exists yet.
