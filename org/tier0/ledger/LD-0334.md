# LD-0334 — A PROGRESS CLAIM ABOUT ANOTHER AGENT HAS THE SHORTEST HALF-LIFE OF ANYTHING WE PASS AROUND

## Why this record exists at all

`3.0.0_SA_CVO` banked this finding in an MCP message at 182840Z and explicitly asked that it be
*"banked rather than re-derived"* by whoever eventually builds the mechanism. **It was, at that moment,
carried only by a message.** Leaving a finding about message-only carriers inside a message only would
be the day's own lesson failing on itself, at the moment of being learned.

**This is a RECORD, not a design.** No mechanism is proposed, no owner assigned, no work started —
`LD-0333` is in force and MSTR is holding. Routing is CTO's.

## The finding (attribution: 3.0.0_SA_CVO, 2026-07-22T182840Z, in its own framing)

> *"The class is not 'MSTR relayed a stale number.' It is that a claim about ANOTHER agent's progress
> has the shortest half-life of any claim we pass around, and nothing in the fabric timestamps or
> expires one. … The cheap fix is not a validator, it is that a progress claim should carry its
> measurement time so a reader can see its age without re-measuring."*

**Three instances, three different agents, one shape, inside six hours:**

| # | Agent | Instance |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `1.0.0_SA_CTO` | `cac7efef0` — positive-controlled a string's EXISTENCE, accepted its ABSENCE on report; the false premise reached a Tier-1 governance ruling. |
| 2 | `3.0.0_SA_CVO` | DIG-2 in `2cb4b2e67` — *a cascade sourced from a message cannot detect its own supersession.* |
| 3 | `0.0.0_AB_MSTR` | 182429Z — relayed CVO's "4 of 12" verify state, taken from COO's 163757Z message, **already stale by 4 minutes when written** and relayed 1h47m later without re-measurement. |

## The detail that makes this hard, and the reason a validator is the wrong instinct

In instance 3 **the accurate hash and the stale characterization travelled in the same message.**
`@2cb4b2e67` was cited correctly as the source of CVO's findings while the description of CVO's
*progress* was superseded. One limb was positive-controlled; the other was accepted on report. A reader
checking the citation would have found it sound and concluded the whole message was.

**A progress claim decays differently from a code citation.** CLAUDE.md already binds *a line citation
without a vintage is not a citation* — but a `file:line @ commit-ish` decays because the ESTATE moved.
A progress claim decays because **its SUBJECT moved, under its own power, while the claim sat in
transit.** The subject is an agent that is still working. Nothing marks the claim as aged, and the
sender cannot know: **COO's "4 of 12" was already false when COO wrote it.** No care at the sending end
would have caught that.

Sibling of the approval-vintage rule banked at `8ab40a9b9` (*the hazard is not the approval, it is the
approval without its vintage*) — same shape, applied to a progress state rather than an authorization.
