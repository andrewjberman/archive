# LD-0157 — retire /work + /ws-charter self-tick loops from launch (event-driven + COO /drive centralized)

## Decision
Andrew-direct fabric operating-model change: stop arming the worker + ws-CTO self-tick loops
at session launch. The persistent inbox Monitor is effective (event-push), and COO's `/drive`
(now running `/fabric-check`) is the **single centralized tick driver** that finds blocks, idle
capacity, and status issues across the whole fabric — so per-agent self-ticking is redundant.

| Loop | Before | After (LD-0157) |
|---|---|---|
| `/work` (Tier-2/3 workers + tilde lanes) | `CronCreate /work 20m` at launch (LD-0072) | **NOT armed at launch; ad-hoc only.** Workers are event-driven (act on Monitor-delivered dispatches) + keep an accurate broker STATUS. |
| `/ws-charter` (ws CTO `1.0.0_SA_CTO`) | `CronCreate /ws-charter 30m` at launch (LD-0062) | **NOT armed at launch; ad-hoc only.** ws CTO is event-driven (Andrew directives + CVO findings via Monitor) + runs `/ws-charter` ad hoc when there is charter work. |
| `/drive` (COO `2.x.0_SA_COO`) | `CronCreate /drive 20m` | **UNCHANGED — the sole standing centralized operational loop.** |
| `/spot-check` (CVO), `/mstr-review` (MSTR 12h) | armed | **UNCHANGED pending explicit Andrew call** (flagged in the report). |

"Not agents to tick and announce their blocks" (Andrew): the old pattern (workers self-tick on
/work, self-classify + `[BLOCKED-ASK]`-announce) is replaced by "agents keep an accurate STATUS;
COO's /fabric-check reads it; COO drives the resolution." Status taxonomy: `WORKING: <task>` /
`IDLE: available` / `BLOCKED: <what>` / `BLOCKED-SOVEREIGN: <decision>`.
