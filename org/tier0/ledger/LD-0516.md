# LD-0516 — two charts, two questions, and I had them answering the same one

## ⛔ THIS SUPERSEDES MY IMPLEMENTATION OF HIS EARLIER RULING, NOT THE RULING

Asked this morning where the 158-of-293 residual belonged, Andrew said *"briefs go in a Planning
group, logs go in an Audit group."* **I read that as two new buckets on the DIRECTIVE axis** and
built `by_stream` for them (LD-0504, wired to the page in LD-0510).

**Wrong axis.** `PLANNING` and `AUDIT` were *already values on the commit_kind enum* — the
type-of-action axis. So the same commit carried the same word on both charts, and the directive
chart stopped reporting a directive for 80 of 293 commits.

⭐ **HIS TEST IS THE ONE THAT SETTLES IT:** *"a clear split of type of action and associated
directive."* A session log about D2 work is **AUDIT on chart 1 and D2 on chart 2** — one commit,
two independent facts. Intercepting it as a "stream" on chart 2 made that chart answer chart 1's
question **and threw the directive away.**

>> **THE RENAME I WAS PROUD OF WAS THE TELL.** LD-0504 renamed that axis from "directive" to
"work stream" to keep PLANNING/AUDIT Rule-24 legal on it — same grain, MECE, residual named. The
postulates passed. **They passed on a coherent axis that was answering the wrong question**, and
renaming the axis to fit the values is how a taxonomy launders a category error into a clean
audit. Rule 24 (iii) is INTENT coherence: I preserved the coherence and lost the intent. <<

## Removed, not left dark

`by_stream` and `timeline.stream` are gone from `project_census.py`, `render_daily_report.py`,
both templates, `_shared_viz.js` (the `STREAM` map) and the schema. **A JSON that still carries
one is REFUSED with a named reason**, not ignored:

```
  by_project.streams is RETIRED -- Planning/Audit belong to by_kind (type of action), not to
  the directive axis; the two charts are orthogonal by ruling
```

⭐ **A removed feature that fails silently comes back; one that fails loudly does not.** Leaving
the plumbing in place and simply not emitting would have been the "built primitive unconnected to
its surface" trap — indistinguishable, later, from a missing one.

## The palette split makes the orthogonality visible

- **Chart 1, type of action:** a COOL RAMP, deep indigo -> blue -> cyan -> teal -> sea green,
  walked in enum order so it reads as ONE family.
- **Chart 2, associated directive:** ROYGBIV, D0 red -> D12 magenta (LD-0515).
- **The residual is neutral grey on BOTH** — deliberately the only thing shared, because it is
  the same non-answer on either axis.

**Logs about D2 and D12 work now attribute to D2 and D12** — 34 commits recovered a real
directive that the stream rule had been intercepting. The residual rises to 72 (25%) because
agent-home logs naming no project and no directive genuinely have none, **and a bucket that hid
them was measuring the wrong axis.** That number is honest; the earlier 8.9% was flattering for
the same reason the seat-derived work-mix was.
