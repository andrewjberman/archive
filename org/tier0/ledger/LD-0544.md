# LD-0544 - Two items showed `undefined`; five were broken

## What Andrew saw, and what was actually wrong

He named the fund-screening and investor-reporting diagrams. Both were `compare`. An audit by
subtraction over all seven items found **five** broken, not two.

| # | item | type | defect |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | spam trap | steps | rows carried `c`, generator reads `state` |
| 1 | change report | flow | `break_after` / `break_label` absent, dereferenced anyway |
| 2 | access roles | coverage | correct |
| 3 | data map | tree | correct |
| 4 | guardrail | compare | rows were LISTS; generator reads `.label/.before/.after` |
| 5 | investor reporting | compare | same |
| 6 | research | steps | same as 0 |

⭐ **THE TWO HE SAW WERE THE TWO THAT PRINTED THE WORD.** The `steps` pair failed SILENTLY and
worse: a wrong `state` greys every node and prints a **false done-count**, which reads as a
measurement rather than as a fault. `flow` painted a NaN marker and the literal `undefined`.

## Why the existing checker passed all five

`check_viz_params` asks only whether the top-level key is present and non-empty. `rows` was
present. `steps` was present. `stages` was present.

⛔ **A CHECK SCOPED TO THE CONTAINER CANNOT SEE A DEFECT IN THE MEMBERS.** This is Rule 22's
shared-dependency blindness one level down, and the checker's own docstring already said what it
was for -- it just never descended.

## The two repairs

**Diagrams.** `compare` is a before-to-after BAR CHART. The guardrail item has no historical
before/after pair, so it became `coverage` (7 of 9 goals met) rather than acquiring an invented
"before" number. Investor Reporting became `link`, which is literally a two-party boundary map and
is the item's real story.

**Projects.** `n` is the DIRECTIVE NUMBER and `name` is its canonical register title -- yesterday's
report did this correctly and I overwrote it with a sequential index and seven invented names. Now
rebuilt from `org/missions/DIRECTIVES.yaml` for the 7 directives that carried commits, chosen by
SUBTRACTION from the register's 13 with the 6 quiet ones named.

## The control

The hardened checker was exercised BOTH WAYS before landing: the repaired report still renders,
and the four defects replayed verbatim produce **5 refusals**. An unrun falsifier is a promise.

## Rollback

Re-publish 2026-09-09 with `--presign`; the three `latest/` keys return to the objects measured at
09 Sep 21:21:50 GMT. The checker change reverts in one commit and loosens nothing else.
