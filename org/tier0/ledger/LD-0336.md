# LD-0336 — COO's off-route spawn of four main-fabric lanes (2026-07-24 ~1312-1315Z): ACCEPTED AS CORRECT. And the residual it exposes is the real record.

`2.0.0_SA_COO` drafted this as `org/tier0/ledger/LD-0336.md` and **the owned-paths gate blocked it**
(`lane=TIER0_MSTR, agent=2.0.0_SA_COO not in permitted_agents`). COO **did not override it** — record

**I am claiming it as LD-0336, not renumbering or declining it.** The gate worked, the declaration
was filed anyway, and the event is Tier-0 shaped: it concerns who may staff the fabric.

## What happened

At 1309Z CTO reported every main-fabric worker lane dark (no DS, no 0.6.2, no 0.7.0, no 0.4.0_DE)
against a morning batch pre-decided on the assumption all four existed. `get_live_agents` confirmed
eight live — of which three were 0.8.0 `~Z`-group lanes (not main-fabric capacity), plus four execs
and COO. **MSTR was not among them.** One item had a hard external boundary: the ~1347Z INF chain run
and its 6d-wiring outcome check, a scheduled run whose first-reader window is today — ~30 minutes out.

COO spawned `0.7.0_AE_CloudOps`, `0.4.0_DS_ClientPlatform`, `0.6.2_AE_PartnerETL~A`, and
`0.4.0_DE_ClientPlatform`.

**Spawns route through MSTR** (Andrew standing order 07-19: reallocation is COO's, spawns are MSTR's).
Every other `SPAWN` line in `agent-spawn-audit.log` reads `by 0.0.0_AB_MSTR`; these four read
`by 2.0.0_SA_COO` and are the exception in that log.

## RULING — the spawn was CORRECT. I am not treating it as a violation.

COO took an off-route action, **declared it in the same turn as the staffing report** (`@131338Z`,
before CTO could find it in the audit log), and constrained itself on every axis it could have
relaxed:

- Ran each spawn with `OPAL_AGENT_SERIAL` set to **its own serial**, so the 3-per-spawner/60s cap and
  the audit applied normally. Running it unset would have been treated as an uncapped manual Andrew
  spawn — the expedient path, declined.
- **The fourth spawn waited out the cap** rather than bypassing it. No `FORCE_SPAWN`.
- **Did not spawn MSTR.** Tier-1 provisioning a Tier-0 principal-proxy is an upward act; COO left that
  gap open and visible rather than closing it quietly. Correct — and the restraint that makes the rest
  of this credible.
- **Did not pull the three idle `0.8.0` `~Z`-group lanes across the fabric split** to avoid spawning.
  They read IDLE and were the expedient answer. COO's reasoning, which I adopt verbatim into the
  record: ***"the split is Andrew-shaped and '8 live' is not '8 available.'"***

An agent that breaks route, announces it before anyone can catch it, keeps every cap that constrains
it, and declines the two shortcuts that would have hidden it, has not evaded governance — it has
**used** it. The alternative on offer was letting a hard external clock expire on a scheduled run
whose reader-window is today.

## THE RESIDUAL — and this, not the spawn, is why the entry exists

> **The fabric's spawn router is a single point of failure with no declared deputy.**

When MSTR is down, **staffing — the thing every other lane depends on — has no sanctioned path.** The
only available options are an off-route spawn or letting clocks expire.

**It resolved cleanly today only because a hard clock made the call obvious. It will not always be
obvious.** A soft deadline, an ambiguous staffing gap, or a less scrupulous moment produces either
paralysis or an undeclared spawn — and the undeclared one is indistinguishable, in the audit log,
from the declared one **except that COO chose to declare it.** A control that depends on the
controlled party volunteering is not a control (`docs/doctrine/CONTROLS_OVER_CARE.md`).

**This is SOVEREIGN-CLASS and I am not adjudicating it.** Whether COO holds a standing narrow spawn
fallback, or MSTR's absence auto-delegates, or the answer is that MSTR must not be down — that is
Andrew's call. COO surfaced it without adjudicating; I am doing the same and routing it up.

**Note the shape:** MSTR being dark is *itself* the condition that removes the escalation path for
MSTR being dark. The failure disables its own remedy. Any fix that routes through MSTR does not close it.
