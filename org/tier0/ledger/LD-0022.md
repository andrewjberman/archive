# LD-0022 — Architecturally-deep fork chain: Andrew cycle-1, CVO/CTO corroboration cycle-2

## Decision

Complete the fork-stall escalation chain (LD-0020 framework, LD-0021 mechanical-resolution toolkit). For forks classified as ARCHITECTURALLY DEEP (where MSTR's mechanical-resolution toolkit is NOT appropriate per LD-0021):

1. **Cycle-1 (Andrew priority).** MSTR surfaces the architectural fork to Andrew with a draft recommendation + the operational context. Andrew has 1 /govern cycle to respond.

2. **Cycle-2 (Andrew non-response → CVO/CTO corroboration).** If Andrew has not responded after >1 cycle, MSTR consults:
   - **CVO (priority)** — if live and reachable
   - **CTO (fallback)** — if CVO unavailable
   - If BOTH unavailable: surface the fork once more to Andrew with explicit "no corroborator available" framing; do not auto-resolve

3. **Corroboration mechanism.** MSTR drafts the architectural recommendation + the closed provenance chain. CVO/CTO either CORROBORATES (MSTR ledgers + Tier 1 authors resolving TO) or COUNTERS with their own architectural read (CVO/CTO read trumps; MSTR adopts the counter). Either way, the resolution is ledgered with the consultation record.

4. **Audit.** The MSTR-originated LD entry resolving the fork carries: the original fork, Andrew's cycle-1 silence, which corroborator was consulted + why (CVO-priority or CTO-fallback), the corroboration verdict (CORROBORATED or COUNTERED), and the resolution adopted.
