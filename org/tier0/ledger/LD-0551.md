# LD-0551 - Minting D13 Design, and labelling the four fields he did not name

## Decision

D13 `Design` is in the register. His word was one sentence; the row needs five fields.

## What he said, entire

> *"I need a new directive, D13 which will be Design. Get that into directives.yaml"*

## What I supplied, and why each is marked as mine

| field | value | status |
|---|---|---|
| `title` | Design | **his** |
| `directive` | `docs/architecture/D13_DESIGN_PRD.md` | the canonical slot; **no PRD exists** |
| `placement` | LAPTOP | **my default, not his ruling** |
| `members` | empty | a state, not a gap |
| `stance` | his verbatim + what is NOT ruled | mine, labelled |

⛔ **THE RISK IN A REGISTER ROW IS PUTTING WORDS IN HIS MOUTH.** Four required fields, none of them
named by him. Each is labelled *at the point of use* — the `placement` value opens with "DEFAULTED
BY 0.0.0_AB_MSTR, NOT RULED BY ANDREW", and the `stance` says "that is the whole of his word;
everything else in this row is the register owner supplying REQUIRED FIELDS".

**LAPTOP is defensible on measurement, not taste:** the design surfaces this estate actually has are
the portal (laptop) and the IRPT frontend round (laptop). And it is cheap to change — he moved D9
whole with one sentence on 2026-09-10, and placement attaches to the **directive**, never to a
member (LD-0498, LD-0502), so a move is one row.

## Verified with the register's own instrument

```
directives-check   live=14 retired=1 malformed=0 ghosts=0
                   14. D13  Design  <NO-MEMBERS>   [PRD PATH MISSING]
missions-tree-index check   0 violations
```

**Both flags are legal and expected.** The checker requires only a mapping with a title (`:153`) and
merely *reports* `prd_exists` (`:181`); D10 has carried `NO-MEMBERS` since its own mint.

## ⛔ What D13 cannot do yet, stated so nobody discovers it later

**No project can register under D13.** `MACROS.yaml` has no `D13` entry, and
`PROJECT_TREE.contract.json` refusal **R2** bars a macro folder whose directive has no ratified
list. **That needs Andrew's word and a Rule 24 record — it is a real bar, not a formality.**

And the directive has **no scope**: what Design means here — the portal's visual system, a design
system package, brand, or the estate's own report surfaces — is unruled. **A directive with no scope
cannot be briefed**, so the next act is his or the CRO's, not this seat's.

## Rollback

Delete the `D13:` block. Nothing references it; no project, no macro, no PRD.
