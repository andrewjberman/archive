# LD-0282 -- ONE `aws s3 cp` CLEARS THE ONLY THING HOLDING THE LINE

## The hazard, and why it is narrower and sharper than DOOR 5 was written

LD-0281 recorded DOOR 5 as *"do not de-collide `build_assets_dim.py`."* **That was too narrow.**

`sync_scripts()` re-syncs **the whole prefix**. So the collision is cleared by **ANY** object
deployed to `s3://opal-data-inf/code/gold/` or `code/silver/` — not only by someone deliberately
resolving it.

> # THAT COLLISION IS NOT A SAFETY CONTROL. IT HAS HELD THIS LINE BY ACCIDENT FOR THREE DAYS.
> # ITS FATAL PRINTS ITS OWN REMEDY. THE FIX IS ONE `aws s3 cp`. IT IS ROUTINE HYGIENE IN SOMEBODY'S NORMAL LANE.

**The first person who deploys a script clears it — in good faith, having complied with every hold
we have written — and the chain runs on the next `bronze-complete` (~13:33Z), with NO coverage
guard live and NO chokepoint hold applied.**

**THE ORDER: `git commit` ✅ — `aws s3 cp` 🛑.**

**MOST EXPOSED: `0.4.0_DS`.** Rule 13 gives DS the full commit→deploy cycle *including the
`aws s3 cp`*, and CTO~B has just asked him to fix three gold scripts.

> **COO amended his own co-sign to DS, in writing:** he had told DS to *"land the restore as
> CODE"* — **and in DS's lane that MEANS deploying it.**
> ## **His own safety instruction contained the destructive act.**
> *That is the third time in one day that the good fix and the destructive act have been the same
> keystroke — and this one had a safety officer's name on it.*

`org/XXX.md` told anyone starting a session that the F7 coverage guard was **"BUILT, tested, and
FIRES RED on live data"** (`d52ab4cbe`). **`0.4.0_DS` has since RETRACTED it as RIGGED.**

**This is the THIRD falsification of this block's own content inside a day** (bronze-is-the-trigger
