# LD-0548 - A correct measurement, a wrong verdict, routed to four seats

## What I claimed, and what is true

I reported that the cross-fabric inbox ferry silently fails whenever the watcher moves a message
into `read/` before a commit, and asked for a one-line `.gitignore` widening.

**The behaviour is real and my discriminator was sound:**

| probe | result |
|---|---|
| `~Z/probe.md` | TRACKABLE |
| `~Z/read/probe.md` | IGNORED |

**The verdict was wrong. That behaviour is the design**, stated in the file itself at
`.gitignore:244-247`:

> *"read/ archives stay ignored => only the undelivered queue travels (self-cleaning: delivered
> mail drops out of git)"*

and again at `.owned-paths.yaml:1420`. `1.0.0_SA_CTO` holds that lane, declined the edit, and is
right: widening it converts a self-cleaning queue into an accumulating archive on both slices,
which is precisely what that comment refuses. **"1 file today" was my size argument and size was
never the question.**

The actual cause of the miss is simpler and was measured by that seat: the file was **local mail**
written to `0.0.0_AB_MSTR~Z` **on site Z**, consumed by the watcher there. A delivered message is
by design not a thing that travels.

## ⛔ How I got it wrong, which is the part worth keeping

I ran `sed -n '250,258p' .gitignore`. **The statement of intent is at 244-247 — six lines above
where my window started.**

>> **MEASURING A MECHANISM TELLS YOU WHAT IT DOES, NEVER WHETHER THAT IS WHAT IT IS FOR. Intent
   lives somewhere other than behaviour, and a DEFECT verdict is a claim about intent, not about
   behaviour.** A correct measurement under a wrong frame produces a confident wrong finding —
   and it is *more* persuasive than a sloppy one, because the measurement really does hold. <<

⭐ **AND THE RAIL EXISTS EXACTLY FOR THIS AND I STEPPED AROUND IT.** CLAUDE.md Rule 21: a
single-file read routes to `comp-read`, which returns *"every QUALIFIER that limits/negates/
corrects a nearby claim"*. That is this failure, by name. A windowed `sed` returns what I asked for
and cannot tell me what I did not ask for. **The cost of the shortcut was a wrong finding sent to
four seats; the cost of the rail was one command.**

COST of the rule: use `comp-read` before calling anything a defect. NON-APPLICATION: reading a
region whose intent you are not about to judge.

## What stands, and what does not

- **WITHDRAWN:** the ferry defect, and the `.gitignore` edit request.
- **STANDS, and is unrelated:** the `.git/config` token-persistence finding in the sprint Lambda
  (msg 95684). That one is measured on git's own behaviour with a canary, is not about intent, and
  `1.0.0_SA_CTO`'s 95687 does not address it.
- **STANDS:** the seating routing (item 1), agreed on every point by that seat and going into
  Andrew's report as a COO seating.

## Rollback

Nothing to roll back — the edit was never made, because it was never mine to make. The routing that
carried the wrong claim is corrected by the withdrawal message this entry accompanies.
