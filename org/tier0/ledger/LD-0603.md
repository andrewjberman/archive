design (`d12-sms` SP-1 behind carrier provisioning; `freshguard` L4 a declared FAIL-BY-DESIGN whose
close path needs Andrew's word on the alarm moratorium), and one is mine and in-window. The other

authored by a `0.7.0_AE_CloudOps` lane that no longer exists** -- `~R`, the base, and `~T`. An entry
whose author is gone cannot close itself, and the COO-facing RED alarm on it fires forever.

⛔ The governing rule is CLAUDE.md NEVER-2: *an entry a device left OPEN proves only that THIS device
saw no completion.* Measure the ARTIFACT; world-done closes SUPERSEDED; **never re-execute**, because
a re-execute of an overtaken promote is a REWIND. Nothing here was re-run.

that first result would have been reported as "the log population is largely absent" -- a confident,
well-formed, completely wrong negative, produced by the platform and not by the world. Every count
