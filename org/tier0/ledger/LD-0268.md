# LD-0268 — Charter `PTNR` (partner-brain structure) + `CCAP` (Cottonwood); PRD request issued to CTO

**What.** Two new macros. `PTNR` builds the pipe; `CCAP` is the first water through it. `PTNR_STRUCTURE`
is the first PRD, issued to CTO for decomposition. `CCAP_FEEINT` is explicitly **not** scoped here — a
structure that has never carried a real partner's contradictions has never been tested.

**The corpus, assessed before proposing structure.** Christa Bass (CFP®, CDFA®) and Jay Rubottom (Founder,
Dir. of BD) at Cottonwood Capital Advisors; Stephen Shipley cc'd; Alexandra Romanello (Ellevate FT,
outside platform consultant). Opal: Andrew, Yang Liu, Manav Shah. Five live threads and a 58-minute call
on 2026-07-07 (993 cues) covering net-revenue provenance — Fidelity vs. internal financials — and
Cottonwood's own A/B/C client tiering already assigned in their CRM.

**Four wants are legible today, with no tooling at all**, which is precisely the argument for the tooling:
portal access (`ASK_ACCESS`, blocked on compliance/beta, **asked at least three times**); model-level
performance over horizons (`ASK_CAPABILITY`); advisor intelligence superficial because BD has no history
and clients aren't advisor-tagged (`BLOCKER_INTERNAL`); fee/revenue intelligence (`ASK_DATA`) → `CCAP_FEEINT`.

**The two rules that make this different from a folder of notes.**

1. **The register is derived, never authored.** A hand-maintained wants board beside an event log is two
   expressions of one truth. This session catalogued sixteen instances of exactly that — culminating in
   CTO's formulation, ratified as Tier-0 doctrine: **comments are unexercised code.** Nobody executes a
   board. `wants.md` is generated, carries the event-tree hash, and declares itself STALE when the hash
   diverges.

2. **Unreadable is not empty.** `Bass Revenue Tracker` is Purview-encrypted. Its event records
   `body_available: false` with a reason. An ingestion that silently files an unreadable message as empty
   is the CloudWatch alarm resting at `OK` through a seven-day freeze, rebuilt in a new system. **"Could
   not read" is a finding.**

**Confidentiality is the load-bearing constraint.** `opal-data-schwab` holds raw PII, its pseudonymized
derivative, and the key that undoes the pseudonymization, in one bucket — and bucket-level IAM
structurally cannot express *"may read the anonymized data, may not re-identify it."* Raw and distilled
partner data go in **separate buckets**, not separate prefixes. **A prefix is a policy statement; a bucket
is an enforceable boundary.**

**Prerequisites, as TOs and not discoveries:** `.owned-paths.yaml` must declare `partners/**` (block mode
refuses undeclared paths — verified empirically today); `.gitignore` must precede the directories; the
crosswalk (`CCAP` ↔ `cot` ↔ `ADVI_COTETL` ↔ `CCA`) lands first, as one source.

**Stakes.** S2 — by confidentiality blast radius, not delivery risk. Nothing is built; nothing is ingested;
no directory exists. Charter only.
