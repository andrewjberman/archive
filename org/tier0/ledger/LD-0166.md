# LD-0166 — /watcher is session-start-once, NOT a recurring cron (correction to LD-0082)

## Decision
Andrew direct (2026-06-08): "watcher should not be a loop just initiated at start of the
session." The MSTR deterministic-startup directive (LD-0082) instructs `CronCreate /watcher
10m, then /watcher` — that recurring-cron step is **stale** against the persistent-Monitor
model and is removed.

- `/watcher` runs **ONCE at session start** (arm-at-load): registers, arms the persistent
  inbox Monitor, pulls the inbox. That is the whole mechanism.
- The **persistent Monitor** (`inbox-watch.sh <serial> stream`) is the standing reachability
  mechanism for the session — it streams every inbox message as a notification and never
  exits. A `/watcher` cron just re-runs an idempotent no-op every 10m.
- The `/watcher` skill already documents this ("The recurring `/watcher` loop is retired";
  "Do not add a `/watcher` ScheduleWakeup/CronCreate loop — it's obsolete"). LD-0082's
  CronCreate step predates / contradicts it; this LD resolves the contradiction in favor of
  the skill.

## Actions
- **This session:** deleted the `/watcher` 10m cron (`2e9aa7dd`). With `/mstr-review`
  retired to ad-hoc (LD-0164), **MSTR now arms zero crons** — reachability via the
  persistent Monitor, governance review ad-hoc.
- **Routed to CTO (durable doc-impl, exec lane per LD-0165):** strike the "CronCreate
  /watcher 10m" step from the MSTR deterministic-loop-arming directive (the launch prompt /
  org/XXX.md startup section / LD-0082 reference) so a future MSTR session arms `/watcher`
  once and does not re-create the cron. Folds into the LD-0164 org/XXX.md doc-impl already
  in flight with CTO.

## Rollback
Re-arm via `CronCreate /watcher 10m` — but there is no reason to; coverage is complete
without it.
