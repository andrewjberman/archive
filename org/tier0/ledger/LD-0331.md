# LD-0331 — 4.0.0_SA_CRO established as a Tier-1 exec (Chief Research Officer)

## 1. What Andrew established

A **fourth Tier-1 exec**, peer to CTO / COO / CVO, reporting to Andrew. Verbatim chain in the frontmatter.
**Mandate:** competitive recon + external research, and the **translation of both into buildable specs.**
**Home:** `org/tier1_exec/4.0.0_SA_CRO/`.

**The gap it closes:** recon products were accumulating with **no durable owner and no index.** RCN-0001
(the Black Diamond teardown, 07-21) named an author in a header, but nothing tied a **serial** to a
**purpose**, and nothing answered *"what has research produced"* across sessions. It read as one-off
research rather than a standing capability. The CRO now owns an **append-only `RECON_REGISTER.md`** as the
authoritative index, independent of where any engagement's artifacts sit.

## 2. THE FINDING — a title is not a grant (fourth declared-vs-applied instance today)

The CRO flagged this itself, and it is the governance-relevant part:

> **MAKING AN AGENT AN EXEC AUTO-GRANTS NOTHING.** Andrew's stated intent was *"that way you can work freely
> as an exec"* — **and the rename alone does not deliver it.** `.owned-paths.yaml` is **ENUMERATION-BASED,
> NOT TIER-BASED**: there is no `org/tier1_exec/*/**` wildcard. Every exec home is listed **individually**,
> and the strings `4.0.0` and `CRO` appeared **nowhere** in the file. `safe-commit` therefore blocked the
> CRO's entire home with `UNDEFINED (no matching path rule; agent=4.0.0_SA_CRO)`.

**This is the same shape as the other three instances recorded in LD-0330 today** — the pin-void co-sign
(§8.2), the retracted sovereign hold surviving in 5 of 6 enforcing copies (§8.20), and the declared-but-
unapplied `ReadTaxsmaRoster` IAM grant (§8.19 RESERVED). **In every case a decision was recorded and the
ENFORCING copy did not know.** A sovereign declaration is not self-executing; **the marker has to say so.**
Grounding supplied by the CRO: `.owned-paths.yaml:808-831` (home-folder rules), `:1617-1619` (the file's own
`ANY_EXEC_OR_AE` lane), `:543+` (`ANY_EXEC_OR_AE` membership — `4.0.0` absent).

and it turns on an **open authority question Andrew has not ruled**: whether the CRO registers its own
research projects in `org/projects/` directly, or routes through CTO/COO.

default**, because *"I did not want to widen my own scope by writing a favorable default."*

the behaviour to expect from a grantee.** Grant the minimum that unblocks; let the sovereign rule the rest.
