# LD-0007 — Adopt spawn/kill capability + stimulus-scan population extension per Andrew architectural ruling

## Decision
Record that MSTR adopts the spawn/kill agent-management capability (CTO commit
`b2698da47` -- `.tools/spawn-agent.ps1` + `.tools/kill-agent.ps1` + 8-process
concurrency cap + audit log) and the stimulus-scan population extension folded
into `/govern` Step 1(b) + Step 4 (skill commit `30e5a58e4`), under Andrew's
architectural ruling: **"MSTR = creator and destroyer; COO = coordinator."**
Supply side (which agents EXIST in the fabric) is MSTR's authority; demand
side (writing TOs against the existing population) is COO's.

Operating consequences this cycle and forward:
- `/govern` Step 1 now runs a two-dimensional scan (work-per-Directive AND
  population-per-LIVE-agent), producing a menu of CHARTER candidates AND
  POPULATION-ACTION candidates.
- Step 4 originate-and-delegate is extended: spawn for unaddressed work
  surfaces; kill for STANDING_BY-with-no-next-step or unrecoverable-BLOCKED;
  kill+spawn for wrong-mix swaps. Each population action is S1 with a
  ledger entry BEFORE the script call.
- 8 live spawned PowerShell windows is the hard cap. Overflow requires kill
  first; the `$env:FORCE_SPAWN` bypass is audited and exceptional.
- Spawn != dispatch. MSTR provisions the agent; TOs + direction still flow
  through Tier 1 (Constitution Art. VI).
- The capability applies across both fabrics (workspace + feature_clb) for
  D-001/D-002 and D-003 respectively; the 8-cap is combined.

## Provenance chain (Art. XIII)
Andrew architectural ruling 2026-05-22 ("MSTR = creator and destroyer") +
verbatim test directive ("test it out right now while i am still at the
office") -> Constitution Art. I (Andrew instruction overrides + sets new
operating rules) + Art. VI (govern-through-the-org — but supply-side
provisioning is consistent with this; spawn !=  dispatch) -> D-001 success
depends on the right population running on the right TOs (PHASE 1 lane
coverage); D-003 success depends on the same on the CL fabric; the
capability serves both -> adopt + ledger.

## Why now
Andrew enacted DIRECTIVES.md v1.4 (commit `5e306b09c`) AND the supporting
infrastructure (capability + skill body) AND set up a live test of all of
it in one window. Recording the adoption in the ledger now is the audit
trail of MSTR formally operating under the new ruling, before the population
actions in LD-0008 / LD-0009 propagate.

## Assumptions
- The 8-process cap is host-resource-defended (the fork-exhaustion crash
  was the root motivation per CTO's brief). Respecting it is non-negotiable.
- The spawn script's `-Headless` default (dropping the
  `--dangerously-load-development-channels` flag) means new spawns do not
  stall on Y/N prompts; spawn is genuinely hands-free.
- Kill is reversible by re-spawning; spawn is reversible by killing. Both
  are S1.

## Alternatives considered
- **Don't adopt; refer every population question back to Andrew** -- rejected:
  Andrew's ruling explicitly delegates supply-side to MSTR, with /mstr_review
  as the audit. Deferring is not honoring the delegation.
- **Adopt but cap usage at 1-2 spawns until /mstr_review** -- rejected: the
  cap is already 8; self-imposing a lower one is artificial. The bounds in
  Stakes Matrix (5 new ledger / 3 delegations / 2 S2 per cycle) already
  shape the pace.

## Rollback plan
A capability-adoption ledger entry is informational. If the spawn/kill
capability itself proves problematic, MSTR refrains from using it until
Andrew adjusts; the entry is updated to status: superseded. Population
actions taken under this entry (LD-0008, LD-0009, future) have their own
rollback plans -- kill the spawned agent.
