# LD-0545 - Closing the `undefined` class at its cause, and carding every directive

## Why `undefined` happened "almost every single day"

**The diagrams are built by JavaScript in the browser**, from `templates/_shared_viz.js`, at VIEW
time. Python validated long before any of that, against a **hand-maintained table** of required
params.

⛔ **TWO CARRIERS OF ONE FACT, AND ONLY ONE WAS EVER UPDATED.** The table drifted; the generator
did not. Measured against the source, **four of nine generators** had a required param the table
did not know about:

| generator | table said | source also dereferences |
|---|---|---|
| `flow` | `stages` | `footer`, `break_label` |
| `coverage` | `pct`, `label` | `caption` |
| `link` | `left`, `right` | `caption` |
| `orbit` | `core`, `count` | `caption` |

This is Rule 24(i) exactly: **a hand-written forward list of what a consumer needs omits silently.**
The same set *derived from the consumer* cannot lose a member without the parse disagreeing.

## The fix

`daily_reports/scripts/viz_contract.py` reads the generator and reports what each one actually
touches — required params, row keys, nested keys. `render_daily_report.py` consumes it and refuses,
fail-closed. Add a generator, add a param, rename a field: the contract follows on the next run
with nobody remembering anything.

## ⛔ The control nearly shipped over-firing, and that is the finding worth keeping

The first version refused **23 findings across 8 of 11 already-served reports**. Every one was a
false positive:

- `compare.rows[].unit` — the source reads `r.unit||""`, a **default**. The guard rule was applied
  to top-level params and not to row fields.
- `screens.shots[].src` — **injected by the renderer** at `render_daily_report.py:144`, never
  authored.

⭐ **A POSITIVE CONTROL BOUNDS UNDER-FIRING ONLY.** I had proved it caught the defect. It took the
historical corpus — reports known-good because they were served — to bound the other side. Both
corrections are now pinned as **over-firing assertions** in the self-test, so a future "improvement"
that re-broke them fails.

Final: **0 findings on 11 served reports; 8 refusals on the 5 defects replayed verbatim.**

## Every directive gets a card

`n` is the directive number and `name` its canonical register title. Quiet directives carry
`quiet: true` — the templates already styled `.card.quiet` dashed and greyed and nobody was
setting the flag. The set is built **by subtraction** from the register, so a new directive appears
the day it lands. `check_directive_coverage()` refuses a missing one, and refuses an `n` that is
not a directive number — the exact regression I introduced hours earlier with a sequential index.

## Rollback

Revert the commit: the renderer returns to its hand table and the page to seven cards. Nothing else
depends on `viz_contract.py`.
