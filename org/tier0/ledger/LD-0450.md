# LD-0450 — The promote-hold register exists because its reader does not

Register: `org/tier0_exec/0.0.0_AB_MSTR/deliverables/PROMOTE_HOLD_REGISTER_20260825.md`

**The reason it is a FILE.** `0.7.0_AE_CloudOps~G` correctly addressed hold (3) to
`0.4.0_DT_ClientPlatform`. **That message landed in an unseated inbox** — my population read
showed 17 live seats and not one DT instance. Meanwhile Andrew has said he will instantiate
`0.2.0_DT_PortfolioAnalytics` for the AGG lint-check. **So the seat that owns the promote act for
all three holds is about to exist and would arrive knowing none of them.**

This is the two-delivery-surfaces law one step further out: a commit fixes the record and a
broadcast fixes the *live* fabric — **but neither reaches a seat that has not spawned yet.** For
that reader the only live surface is the spawn brief, and a brief can only carry what is written
down somewhere liftable.

**Standing commitment:** if this seat spawns any DT instance, the register rides the brief
verbatim. If Andrew instantiates DT directly — his stated intent and entirely his to do — **the
brief is not mine to write**, which is precisely why the content had to become a file rather than
a promise.

**CVO's contribution is the load-bearing half of hold (3)** and is why this is a document rather
than a one-line note: the condition *"verified end to end"* is **not merely pending — it has two
measured counter-examples already filed** (`ADVI_PERFRP-S3`, `-S7`). *A hold whose condition reads
"not yet done" is priced differently from one whose condition has known failing measurements.*
Neither of us claims the condition is unmeetable; both items are graduated, open, and owned.

**What this entry does NOT do.** It does not interpret any hold, appoint a verifier, or decide
whether holds (2) and (3) are one or two. Each silence is recorded as a silence. **CVO explicitly
declined to appoint itself verifier**, and that refusal is preserved in the register rather than
quietly resolved — an unnamed verifier is a fact about his word, not a gap for the fabric to fill.

**Why it was ledgered at all:** `mstr-ledger-check` refused the commit, and correctly — the
register carries a *standing commitment* about future spawn briefs, which is a commitment that
propagates, exactly what Art. V names. **Fourth time tonight this gate has been right against my
own proportionality read.** The pattern is now the finding: proportionality is the argument that
is always available, which is why the requirement is mechanical and not mine to weigh.
