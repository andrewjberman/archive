# LD-0026 — Generalize LD-0022 corroboration backstop to all Andrew-pending items

## Decision

Extend LD-0022's architectural-fork-specific CVO/CTO corroboration backstop mechanism to **all Andrew-pending sovereign items**, not just architectural forks.

**The generalized rule:**

1. **MSTR surfaces any sovereign-pending question to Andrew** with draft recommendation + provenance chain (existing pattern).

2. **Andrew has 1 /govern cycle SLA** on the surfaced item (existing per LD-0022).

3. **If Andrew non-responsive >1 cycle**, MSTR consults:
   - **CVO (priority)** — if LIVE and reachable
   - **CTO (fallback)** — if CVO unavailable
   - Both unavailable → re-surface to Andrew with "no corroborator available" framing

4. **CVO/CTO returns CORROBORATES (MSTR draft recommendation stands as working ratification → Tier 1 authors resolving TO/action) OR COUNTERS (MSTR adopts the counter — corroborator's read trumps MSTR's working interpretation).**

5. **Additive considerations** (CVO M-1-class, CTO architectural-context-class) from a CORROBORATE verdict fold into the materialization design as constraints/checks, NOT as gates that re-open the question (CY-0055 banked discipline).

6. **Andrew override authority preserved on re-surface** — MSTR ledgers reversal LD + Tier 1 swaps via `<issued_to>` field-edit pattern (banked operational discipline).

7. **Audit trail** — each corroboration-resolved item gets its own LD entry referencing the original sovereign-surface + the silence-window + the corroborator consulted + verdict + adopted resolution.

## Why now

Today exposed the asymmetry: LD-0022 generalized architectural-fork handling cleanly but left STALE-ladder cycle-3 (and other sovereign-pending classes) without a corresponding backstop. DS Base cycle-3 disposition has been Andrew-pending across ~7+ cycles since CY-0046 surface; status quo (effective REDIRECT via re-route absorption) was operationally fine but lacked explicit ratification. CY-0055's discussion surfaced the question; Andrew confirmed the generalization.

The mechanism IS Andrew's principle stated more broadly: he's not the sole resolver of sovereign items by intent — he's the preferred resolver, with CVO/CTO as the chartered backstop. LD-0026 makes that pattern fabric-wide.

## Assumptions

- CVO is the right priority corroborator across operational + architectural classes (their verify-channel discipline applies to both; F-005 magnitude-sanity, F-011 META yellow-flag, RV-0010 acceptance all demonstrated empirically today).
- CTO fallback applies when CVO unavailable (rare in current fabric topology).
- Andrew override authority preserved on re-surface preserves sovereign primacy even with backstop mechanism active.
- 1-cycle SLA is the right threshold — long enough for Andrew to respond when present, short enough to prevent throughput drag.
- The mechanism handles all sovereign-pending classes: population-management (DS Base), project-scoping (Q1/Q2), audit-trail (RV-batch), cross-domain (coordination calls), product-strategy (deferrals).

## Alternatives considered

- **Keep LD-0022 architectural-fork-only.** Rejected: Andrew explicitly authorized the generalization; the architectural-fork-only scope was the empirical starting point not the principled stopping point.
- **Auto-fire CVO consultation immediately on surface (no 1-cycle SLA).** Rejected: preserves sovereign primacy via 1-cycle Andrew SLA; immediate consultation would undermine Andrew-first principle.
- **CTO priority instead of CVO.** Rejected: Andrew explicitly stated CVO priority for architectural forks; the same authority preserved here; CVO's verify-channel discipline applies broadly.
- **Different mechanism per item class.** Rejected: unified mechanism preserves simplicity + audit clarity.
