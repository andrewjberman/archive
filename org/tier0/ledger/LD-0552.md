# LD-0552 - DSGN ratified for D13, and an enum over a population that does not exist yet

## What he said, in sequence

> *"First macro will be DSGN, obviously"*
> *"It is design system, branding, aesthetic frontend"*

The first ratifies the enum. The second is the **scope** LD-0551 recorded as missing, and it is now
quoted verbatim into D13's `stance` rather than paraphrased.

## The Rule 24 record

| postulate | answer |
|---|---|
| **(i) MECE over a declared domain**, hunted by subtraction | D13 has **no members**. The population is empty, so there is no uncovered member to find — and the **first registrant is therefore the first real test** of whether one macro is enough. |
| **(ii) same grain** | `DSGN` is a macro, sitting beside NFRA / ETFS / MDPF / AUTH / IRPT / AMGT. Same level, same grammar. |
| **(iii) intent coherence** | One axis: design as a discipline. His three words name one field, not three unrelated ones. |
| **(iv) state coherence** | This flip **moves D13 only**. It does not touch placement (still my default), mints no project, writes no PRD, grants nothing. |

**Collision census — unscoped, denominator printed, floors disclosed:**

```
comp-find '\bDSGN\b' -i        0 hits / 167,325 files scanned [corpus=all]
NOT covered by that zero:      104,647 ephemeral-floor · 4,444 binary · 11 unreadable
grammar (contract at HEAD)     ^[A-Z]{4}$  ->  DSGN conforms
```

⭐ **The census is UNSCOPED deliberately.** Rule 24's amended clause is that a subtraction over the
wrong domain is *blinder* than recall, because it arrives with a printed denominator and so reads as
measured. The cheapest defence is to not narrow the domain at all, and here that cost one command.

## The alternative I named and rejected

**`[DSGN, BRND]`** — he named "branding" separately from "design system". Rejected: he said **FIRST**
macro, which is a statement about *order* and leaves room for a second; and **an enum is a
measurement of what is entering, not a wish list.** Nothing is entering under `BRND` today, and
minting a macro no project uses is the self-granting shape this file already refuses at D8. It costs
one word and one record when a brand project actually appears.
