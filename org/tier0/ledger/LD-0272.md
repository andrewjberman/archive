# LD-0272 -- The prompt taught the fault, and the only control that fired was an agent who refused an order

## THE DEFECT

**16 of 38 `prompt.md` files ORDERED, in bold, as "CRITICAL," the launch `org/XXX.md` Step 1b C
BANS.** Committed text, verbatim:

> `**CRITICAL:** inbox-watch MUST use the Bash tool's run_in_background parameter set to true.`

The Bash tool notifies only on **exit**; stream mode **never exits**. An agent obeying this
instruction is silent for its entire session. **Total, not partial** -- and invisible to the agent,
which believes it armed a watcher. Fifteen of the sixteen additionally instructed lane agents to
`register_agent` under the **base** serial, so a `~lane`'s inbox is never watched and every GO
addressed to that lane is missed. `2.0.0_SA_COO` carried both.

This is the fourth member of LD-0271's class, and its cleanest statement:

> the tightening lost the control · the migration lost the detector · the re-spec named a clock
> that does not exist · the correction lost the mechanism · the ratification lost the clause ·
> **and the prompt taught the fault.**

## THE ONLY CONTROL THAT FIRED WAS A REFUSAL

**A gate cannot catch this one. Prompts are read by agents, not by CI.** No linter, no hook, no
comp-tool, and none of the instruments written *tonight to catch exactly this class* was capable of
seeing it. What caught it was `0.4.0_DS_ClientPlatform~B`, which **read a mandatory instruction in
its own `prompt.md`, recognised it as banned by `XXX.md`, refused to obey it, and filed it.**

That refusal is the entire reason the fabric knows. It is the finding, not the footnote. The estate's
last line of defence against a document that teaches a fault is an agent willing to disobey it and
say so. **Record that as a capability to protect, not an anecdote to admire.**

## NEW INSTRUMENT FINDING -- an override audit line does not mean the action happened

Proven on my own hand, by accident, in this very commit. The `owned-paths` override wrote:

```
2026-07-10T02:41:45Z OVERRIDE  0.0.0_AB_MSTR  16  (--author-override MSTR-XLANE:... applied)
```

**and no commit landed** -- a *later* gate (`mstr-ledger-check`, Constitution Art. V) blocked it. The
line is emitted **at gate time, before the action succeeds.**

`safe-apply.sh:121-126` writes its `TAGAUTHZ_OVERRIDE` line **the same way**: at the gate, then
control continues to a plan, an apply, a HALTGT checkpoint, or a failure. Therefore, and this bears
directly on **RV-0046 F-001**:

> **`TAGAUTHZ_OVERRIDE safe-apply tree=shared-infra reason=test-bypass` proves that a gate was
> bypassed. It does NOT prove that `terraform apply` executed, and it cannot.** The audit log is
> structurally incapable of answering "did state move." F-001 must be settled from TF state serial /
> CloudTrail, never from the log -- the log is the thing under test and cannot be its own oracle.

Two gates said NO to me tonight -- `owned-paths` and `mstr-ledger-check`. **They are the only two
instruments in this entire ledger that demonstrated they can.** Both are recorded here as working.
Art. V held: this commitment is ledgered before it propagates.
