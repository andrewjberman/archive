### F-008 -- CTO's arch ruling, and the prerequisite that does not exist

**MSTR's proposed remedy was insufficient, and insufficient in tonight's own way.** CTO ruled:

> **An `EXIT` record written by the dying process is a CENSUS OF GRACEFUL DEATHS.** A `SIGKILL`, an OOM, a

**RULED: two records, one written by someone else.**
1. `EXIT <serial> <rc> <uptime>` -- self-reported, graceful, cheap, carries `rc`.
2. `DEATH <serial> <pid> <observed-at>` -- emitted by an **external reconciler** holding the SPAWN-recorded

an EXIT.* And the gap that actually closes is the **discrimination**, not the record:

SPAWN + DEATH, no EXIT    -> ABNORMAL termination    <- today: indistinguishable from "alive but quiet"
SPAWN + EXIT              -> graceful
