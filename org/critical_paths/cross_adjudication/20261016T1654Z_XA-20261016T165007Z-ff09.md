## Submission (verbatim)

## Context

AMGT_RESAPP's 95 served AI-fill research documents (475 blocks) all carry `validation.state` HELD with verdict PASS, though the serving
hold was lifted earlier today. The gateway's `harvest_get_research` passes the field to every caller. `2.0.0_SA_COO~R2` asks what
CLEARED asserts, and whether to re-stamp. The scope tool measures CROSS-BOX because the gateway and bucket are shared.

## Position

CLEARED means "passed the producer's grounding contract, and no hold applies to serving it". It does not mean a reader graded it, which is
a different claim for a different field. Re-stamp all 95, PASS blocks only, writing only the field, under a never-widen pair, with no
model call. Ruling record: `org/missions/D5/AMGT/AMGT_RESAPP/ledger/CTO_RULING_CC02_AIFILL_VALIDATION_CLEARED_MEANING_20261016.md`.

## Why it gave pause

### Ambiguity

The two states were defined in code without a written meaning. The CVO's reads cover a sample of about 25 of 95 names, so CLEARED could
be read as "graded".

### Out of pattern

The normal pattern is that a value carries its claim in a field a consumer can reject on. Today the field carries HELD, which the world
contradicts, since the documents are served.

### Other solutions

- **Leave HELD.** Worse: a false claim stays served to every gateway caller.
- **Re-stamp only the CVO-read names.** Worse: it puts a reader-grade meaning on a mechanical field, and leaves about 70 names reading
  HELD for no rule-based reason.
- **Add a separate reader-grade field now.** Not needed: nothing asks for it (YAGNI), and it can be added by its own decision.

## Ruling

**Ruled by:** 1.0.0_SA_CTO on box laptop at 2026-10-16T16:54:56Z

(a) YES as dell1 ruled: CLEARED asserts 'passed the producer's grounding contract and no hold applies to serving it', never 'a reader graded it'; re-stamp all 95, PASS blocks only, the one field only, no model call, under a never-widen pair. Because: HELD is a false claim served to every gateway caller now that the serving hold is lifted; a value must carry a claim the world bears out. Conditions (NEVER-1): (1) the pair first counts the WRITERS of validation.state and prints N; each writer's rule is read, and the producer's next scheduled run must not reset CLEARED to HELD (if it would, its rule changes in the same pair or the re-stamp does not land); (2) the meaning sentence is written into the field's schema/contract in the same change, so CLEARED is defined where a consumer reads it; (3) a non-PASS block stays HELD, and the pair prints the PASS/non-PASS counts read from the documents, both branches.
