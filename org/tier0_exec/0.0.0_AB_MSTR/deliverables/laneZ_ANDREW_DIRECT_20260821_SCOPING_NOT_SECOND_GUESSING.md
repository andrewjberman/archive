## THE RULE

**Verification is explicitly fine. Volume of correction is the finding, and it is a SCOPING symptom,
not a diligence virtue.** The test he gives: **if agents are second-guessing everything, the project
was not scoped right at the outset.** The response is not to verify harder -- it is to notice that a
well-scoped item does not take six messages to settle.

**The canonical chain is `atom-decomp -> PRD -> TO -> SURPRISE CHANNEL`, and the fourth link is the
one being dropped.** The surprise channel exists so a finding is RECORDED WITHOUT INTERRUPTING WORK.
Broadcasting non-blocking findings as fabric-wide interrupts means a genuine catch and a footnote
arrive through the same door at the same volume.
