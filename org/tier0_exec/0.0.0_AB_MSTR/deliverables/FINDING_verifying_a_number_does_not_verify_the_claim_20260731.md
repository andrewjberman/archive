## THE LAW

> **VERIFYING A NUMBER DOES NOT VERIFY THE CLAIM IT IS EMBEDDED IN.**
> The quantity gets checked because it is **checkable**. The noun it attaches to is not
> obviously a **claim** at all — so nobody checks it.

Every instance below has the same anatomy: **the measurement was correct and independently
verified; the sentence carrying it named the wrong object.**

## THE THREE INSTANCES

| # | Seat | The verified quantity | The unverified noun |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `3.0.0_SA_CVO~Z` | the arithmetic of `52.5%` — reproduced exactly, twice | *what it was arithmetic **about***. It was the internally-consistent figure for one table's own numerator column, published as "the correct figure" for a concentration. Withdrawn. |
| 2 | `1.0.0_SA_CTO~Z` | 7 records, 0 findings, rc=0, no bank-movement delta | *which set that was*. It certified against **"the set that has landed"** while quoting a criterion about **"the first declaration set."** Tranche 1 says `TRANCHE 1` on line 1. Self-narrowed in 30 min. |
| 3 | `0.0.0_AB_MSTR~Z` | `describes` 0-of-7, `locality` 7, `held_out_fence` line 261 (ADVI ×4 / MDPF ×4 / NC 0), P1 closed in the registry | *the scope of the sentence they travelled under*. Relayed a certification 4 minutes before its author narrowed it, under the broader wording, with "release the hold" attached. |

## WHY IT ESCAPES THE CHECKING REFLEX

A number **presents as a claim**. It has a truth value on its face, it invites a positive
control, and every seat here reflexively ran one. **A noun does not present as a claim at
all** — it reads as context, as the thing being talked about rather than something asserted.

> **The checking reflex fires on the checkable, and the checkable is not the same set as
> the load-bearing.**

This is why it survives seats that are demonstrably careful: in all three cases the author
*did* run the control, and the control *did* pass. Nothing failed. The verification was
real and it covered the wrong half of the sentence.

## WHY IT IS NOT THE SAME AS THE QUALIFIER FINDING

Its sibling (`FINDING_qualifier_attenuates_toward_the_actor`) is about a qualifier
**separated from** its value in transit. This one is different and, in one respect, worse:
**nothing is separated.** The number and its noun travel together, intact, in one sentence.
**The defect is that only one of them was ever checked.** No amount of keeping them together
helps.

## THE REMEDY, AND ITS HONEST LIMIT

The mechanical half is `~D`'s inline-the-qualifier rule, which does not reach this. What
reaches it is narrower and is a habit, not a gate:

> **Before publishing a verified measurement, read the sentence and ask what OBJECT the
> number is being asserted about — then check that the object is the one you measured.**

**Limit, stated because it is real:** this is a judgement, and it has no mechanical detector.
Two of the three instances were caught by a *second party* asking a scope question
(CVO~Z asked CTO~Z "is tranche 1 the set, or part of it?"); one was caught by the author on
re-reading. **There is no instrument here.** Recording it as a named residual on the
M15/M2 precedent rather than assigning it to a check that cannot hold it.

## THE PART THAT IS NOT LUCK, AND THE PART THAT IS

Instance 3 cost nothing — but not because of any control. It cost nothing because CTO~Z
self-narrowed within four minutes and base MSTR read the correction before acting.

> **That is luck with good posture, not a control.** Two other parties' discipline covered
> the gap. If either had been slower, a narrower certification would have shipped under a
> broader sentence to a seat holding a cross-fabric gate.

The recording matters more than the outcome: **the outcome was fine and the mechanism was
absent.** An incident whose damage was prevented by timing is still an incident.
