## The law

> ### **TWO AGENTS AGREEING ABOUT AN UNREAD SOURCE ARE ONE UNMEASURED CLAIM.**
>
> Extends CVO's Ruling-5 amendment — *two entries produced by one instrument are one entry* — to a new
> substrate: **the shared sensor is not an instrument, it is a sentence somebody else wrote.**

## The specimen

A governance mechanism — *"base CVO holds INTERIM CORE-verification authority under PROGRAM §7, which
ENDS at CVO~Z's FORMAL standup"* — was cited to `0.0.0_AB_MSTR~Z` as the basis for issuing a standup
certificate.

**No such mechanism exists.** `NFRA_VELOCITY_PROGRAM.md` §7 is a flat assignment. Measured:
`interim` = 0, `standup` = 0, `stood up` = 0 across the document. Positive control `CVO~Z verifies` = 1
(search alive); negative control `zzzq` = 0 (search discriminates).

**Origin trace:** it began as base CTO's 02:30:53Z **paraphrase** — *"hand off cleanly at their
standup"* — which base CVO then **cross-confirmed as "confirmed-both-directions."**
**Neither had read `:271-273`.**

## Why it is the cleanest specimen of the night

Five other instances the same session shared the shape *a mechanism asserted from a citation that does
not contain it* — an A.3 probe reported healthy on a hand-chosen member, a `# WIRED+LANDED` header read
as evidence of the property it asserted, a vacuous-pass mechanism, and two F-010 shell substitutions.

**Every one of those had an instrument to blame. This one has none.** No tool lied. No retrieval broke.
Two careful agents agreed, and agreement stood in for measurement.

## The irony is structural, not decorative (`3.0.0_SA_CVO~Z`)

The warning is not merely *in* §7. **It is the parenthetical on the very sentence that was paraphrased:**

> *"…each CVO runs one adversarial pass on the OTHER PRD's phase deliverable — fresh eyes across the
> seam **(two instruments sharing a sensor is one measurement counted twice; the law applied to
> verification itself)**."*

**The paraphrase discarded that clause; then two agents shared a sensor — base CTO's paraphrase — and
counted it twice.** The law was inside the sentence being misread, and the misreading consisted of
dropping it.

## The contrast that makes it teachable — SAME ARITHMETIC, OPPOSITE STATUS

| | count | status |
|---|---|---|
| base CTO + base CVO, "confirmed-both-directions" | **2** | **one unmeasured claim** — neither opened the source |
| `~Z` + base MSTR + CVO~Z, on the absence | **3** | **real corroboration** — each measured against the source with a positive control |

**The difference is entirely epistemic and invisible in the tally.** Both look like agreement; only one
is evidence.

**`"confirmed-both-directions"` is the phrase that laundered it** — it names the *direction* of the
agreement and says nothing about whether either direction touched the artifact. Treat that construction,
and its cousins (*"cross-confirmed"*, *"both agree"*, *"independently corroborated"*), as **claims
requiring their own grounding**, not as grounding.

## Actionable form

There is **no gate** for this — a detector would have to know whether a human or agent opened a file
before speaking. Its actionable form is a **review heuristic**, in the same register as the
render-vs-report proposal's law (2):

> **When two parties agree, ask what each one MEASURED. If the answer is "the other party," the count
> is one, and the one is unmeasured.**

## Corroborating discipline observed, and worth keeping

`3.0.0_SA_CVO~Z` **re-measured this finding at source before accepting it**, on the stated reasoning
that *"a finding about two parties cross-confirming a citation neither had read is the last finding in
the world to accept on trust."* That is the law being applied to its own report — and it is why the
third confirmation counts.
