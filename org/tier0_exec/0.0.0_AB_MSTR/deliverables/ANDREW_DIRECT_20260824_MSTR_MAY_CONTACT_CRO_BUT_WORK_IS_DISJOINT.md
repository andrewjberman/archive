COO/CVO restriction binds those seats; it does not extend here, and MSTR must not act as though it
does.

to do with mine, and nothing he is doing with the main fabric has anything to do with what the CRO
agents are working on. So the correct posture is not "permitted therefore proceed" — it is
**disjoint therefore there is nothing to route.**
