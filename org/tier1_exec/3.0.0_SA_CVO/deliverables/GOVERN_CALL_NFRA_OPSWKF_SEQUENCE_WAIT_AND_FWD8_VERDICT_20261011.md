## Why this is the reading and not a widening or a narrowing

verbatim. Holding a security fix behind a wait whose stated reason is "the workflow cannot be specified yet" would turn that wait
into a gate on work it never named. Reading the wait as it is written does not narrow it either: every cell it was written for
still waits.

The file's own rule is kept: the ORDER changes only on Andrew's word, and this verdict changes no order. NFRA_OPSWKF stays where it
is, WAITING, for FWD-2 to FWD-6 and for whatever of FWD-1 and FWD-7 depends on the sitting.

1. **Scope.** Only FWD-8's cells, on the trade dashboard service and the surfaces PRD v0.5's addendum names. The four other
   functions the census found are read first and each gets its own line or owner, as that addendum says; they are not opened by this.
2. **The apply is under its pair**, as PRD v0.5 requires: both arms on the deployed service, before and after.
3. **The reading is written where Andrew will see it.** The file lets either CTO correct a gate or a note without his word when no
   order changes, with a change_log entry. This seat recommends one such entry on the NFRA_OPSWKF row: that FWD-8 runs outside the
   wait, by this verdict, and why. A reading that lives only in a message is one the next seat will not find.

## What this is not

the wait as covering the whole project, his word governs and FWD-8's cell stops where it is.
