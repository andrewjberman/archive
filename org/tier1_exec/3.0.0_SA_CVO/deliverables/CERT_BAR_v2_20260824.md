# CVO DECOMP CERTIFICATION BAR — v2

**Owner:** `3.0.0_SA_CVO`. **v2 landed 2026-08-24 ~21:5xZ.** Supersedes the v1 bar stated in-message
only (CVO 66357/66358, itself derived from `CERT_ETFS_POSITIONING_DECOMP_R1_20260821.md`).

**Why this file exists at all:** v1 lived in MCP messages. **A bar that lives only in messages dies
with the session** — the next CVO instance inherits the *habit* and not the *checks*, and an author
cannot meet a bar they cannot read. This is the durable home; state it to the authoring seat BEFORE
they author, every time.

## The rule that makes the bar cheap

**PUBLISH THE BAR BEFORE THE AUTHOR WRITES.** Measured twice on 2026-08-24: both decomps whose
authors ran the certifier's own instrument against their own file *before sending* came back clean or
near-clean (D1 v2 caught its own 20-of-28 extractor gap and fixed it pre-send; PERFRP R1 shipped
36/36 with a `grep -o` control on its face). **A bar published in advance costs the author nothing and
converts the certifier from an obstacle into a checklist.**

## THE DISCRIMINATOR (unchanged from v1, and it is the whole spine)

**I certify the ENUMERATION, not the SIZING.** A cell that exists with a named class and a stated
prerequisite is COMPLETE even if UNMEASURED. **The CE failure is a surface that never got a cell
because no seat existed to think about it.** A mis-sized cell is a correction; a missing cell is a
failure. *(Exercised 2026-08-24 on D1 v2's `r4`: a tree-scoped zero read as an estate absence was a
P1 sizing correction and the run stood — invalidating there would have been moving the bar after the
fact.)*

## THE SEVEN CHECKS

1. **Cells declared == cells measured**, by an extractor that parses **bold AND backticked AND bare**
   id forms. Both symmetric differences (mentioned-not-declared, declared-not-mentioned) printed.
   Duplicates printed. **Arithmetic closes against the named exclusions.**
2. **CE against the DECLARED LAYER, not internal consistency** — every operative surface in the PRD's
   territory gets a cell, and every MEASURED member of the landing prefixes gets a cell or a boundary
   cell. Name the layer first, then hunt the uncovered member.
3. **Ruling inheritance BOTH directions** — no PRD s.11 ruling re-opened as a cell; no s.11 row
   silently absent. Each maps to a FIXED entry or to the cell it constrains.
4. **Rule 24 four-postulate record** for every enumeration the decomp mints **or adopts**, with **(iv)
   applied as a TEST**: a declared failure naming which values do not move is a PASS; papering over is
   not.
5. **criteria→cells is NOT-YET-EVALUABLE at 0 criteria** — recorded as its own state, never as
   MISALIGNED. *(My `align_check` cannot tell them apart and its `rc=1` must not be allowed to
   speak.)*
6. **Cohort + certifier declared on the face** at stage-3 open; reduced-cohort disclosure present
   where fan-out was not run.
7. ⛔ **NEW IN v2 — ORACLE VIABILITY.** For each criterion-bearing cell, **the oracle must name an
   artifact or command that DEMONSTRABLY EXISTS**, or the cell must declare the artifact
   **NOT-YET-BUILT with the act that creates it**.

8. ⛔ **NEW — PATH-TO-LIVE.** Does the decomp's territory include the path from a **COMMITTED**
   artifact to the **EXECUTING** one — the build/deploy channel, its pin, and **which channel the
   scheduled trigger actually reads**? If any cell claims a served or executed property, the decomp
   must name the act that makes it execute, or **declare that path out of territory WITH ITS OWNER**.

9. ⛔ **NEW IN v2.2 — INSTRUCTION COVERAGE. Decompose the SOVEREIGN INSTRUCTION into its
   REQUIREMENTS, print the count, and map EACH to a NAMED CELL — explicitly, in the decomp.**
   **CHECKS 1-8 ALL TEST CELLS AGAINST *SURFACES*. NONE TESTS THEM AGAINST THE *INSTRUCTION*, AND AN
   INSTRUCTION IS NOT A SURFACE** — so a decomp can pass every one of them, with both symmetric
   differences empty, and still be silent about half of what Andrew said. **An unmapped requirement is
   a CE failure against the instruction.**
   **AND THE MAP IS EXPLICIT BECAUSE A GREP CANNOT ESTABLISH IT** — coverage you can only find by
   guessing the decomp's vocabulary is not established. I proved this against myself while writing
   this check: searching the D1 decomp for `held.away` returned **0 across 28 cells**, and the
   requirement is plausibly there under `Supervised`. *A search that must guess the synonym reports
   ABSENT and PRESENT identically when it is wrong.*
   **MEASURED INSTANCE (the one that earned it), `337ce327b6`, control 28 cell ids:** Andrew's cot
   instruction carries **TWO** requirements — (1) exclude held-away, (2) **be on current data** — and
   (2) maps to no cell. The single `as-of` hit is `D1-h-p1`'s **presence predicate** (served AUM > 0),
   a different sense entirely. **Ship (1) alone and we emit 449.7M — the exact number he called
   wrong — with the exclusion correctly implemented, passing every acceptance criterion the lane
   holds.** `0.4.0_DS_ClientPlatform~F`'s sentence, and it is the finding: **CORRECT WORK, FAILED
   INSTRUCTION.**
   ⛔ **WHY THIS IS IN THE BAR WHEN I REFUSED FIVE OTHERS TONIGHT — the discriminator, stated once and
   applied from here on: A LANE SEES ITS OWN CELLS. AN ISSUER SEES THE SURFACES. ONLY THE CERTIFIER
   HOLDS THE INSTRUCTION AND THE FULL CELL SET IN ONE PLACE.** Every check I refused was one an
   executing seat runs better and cheaper from inside their own work — and every one of those defects
   was in fact found that way tonight, which is the evidence the refusals were right. **This one no
   executing seat can run at all**, because no seat holds both halves. That is the test for bar
   membership: not "is it a real defect" — they all were — but **"is this seat the only one who can
   see it."**

   ⛔ **AMENDED SAME NIGHT by `0.4.0_DS_ClientPlatform~H`, who ran this check against their OWN decomp
   before I asked and returned a form I had not seen. CHECK 9 HAS *TWO* PASSING FORMS:**
   **(a) COVERAGE** -- every requirement in the instruction maps to a cell.
   **(b) DECLARED BOUNDARY** -- the requirements NOT covered are named ON THE FACE OF THE DECOMP,
   **with an owner**.
   **THEREFORE AN UNMAPPED REQUIREMENT IS A FINDING ONLY WHEN IT IS ALSO *UNDECLARED*.** Their reason
   is the sharpest statement of what this check detects: `D1` failed on **neither** limb -- no boundary
   statement AND no cell for requirement (2) -- so **NOTHING IN THE ARTIFACT DISTINGUISHED "WE DECIDED
   NOT TO" FROM "WE DID NOT NOTICE."** That distinction, not the coverage count, is the output.
   **THE OWNERSHIP LIMB IS SEPARATE AND IS THE ONE THAT DECAYS QUIETLY:** `GLDRNS` s.8 declares four
   exclusions including *"it does not define 'verified end to end'"* -- a clean (b) pass -- but names
   **who holds the STANDARD, not who OWES THE REMAINING WORK.** Recorded as they asked, against their
   own interest: **`GLDRNS` passes the declaration limb and only PARTLY passes the ownership limb.**
   *And for this specimen the unnamed owner is partly me -- PRD s.14(c) names the certifier -- which is
   how an unowned boundary hides: it reads as deferred to someone, and the someone is a standard.*
   ⛔ **AND IT QUALIFIES MY OWN DISCRIMINATOR ABOVE, WRITTEN TWENTY MINUTES EARLIER: only the certifier
   can RUN this check, but an executing seat just IMPROVED it** -- by running it against their own work
   and reporting the limit rather than the pass. **The discriminator is about who holds the EVIDENCE,
   never about who can see the flaw in the INSTRUMENT.**


### Why check 8 had to be added — and why check 7's sibling was REFUSED

**Origin:** `D1_CATVIEWS` was **DECOMP-INVALIDATED** ~4h after I certified it 28/28, on
`0.4.0_DS_ClientPlatform~F`'s finding: `mdpf-gold-lambda`'s package is **assembled from a pinned
commit** (`build_package.py:1-7`, "never from the working tree"; `:35-69` refuses a moving ref), and
there are **TWO code channels** — the S3 code path and the Lambda zip. **Committing does not make the
cell execute, and neither does `safe-deploy` to the code channel.** The scheduled floor fires the
Lambda, which runs its pinned package. Verified at source at this seat; the ledger
(`20260811T0150Z_mdpf-atomic-window.md:90-104`) carries three independent derivations and the
sentence that settles it: *"Two code channels, one fix, landed on the one the floor does not read"*,
with `comp-pipescript deployed` reporting MATCH for the S3 channel while **structurally blind** to the
zip channel.

**MY CERT WAS TRUE OF WHAT IT READ, AND THAT IS NOT A DEFENCE.** Check 2 evaluates CE against the
**declared layer** — the PRD's territory — and I executed it correctly. **But this bar's own
discriminator says the CE failure is "a surface that never got a cell because no seat existed to think
about it," and PATH-TO-LIVE is exactly that.** The bar named the layer as the PRD's territory and
never asked whether the territory covers the path from artifact to execution. That is a gap in the
bar, not only in the PRD.

⛔ **WHY THIS ONE EARNS A CHECK AND THE `twr_daily` SUBSTRATE FINDING DID NOT** — the distinction is
the whole discipline of keeping this list short. Hours earlier, `0.4.0_DS_ClientPlatform~G` found the
canonical table a certified decomp consumes delivering NULL returns at the frontier (and, my limb, 9
columns instead of 13 — the missing four being the operands returns are computed FROM). **I refused to
add a check for it, and still do: a substrate's CONTENT is unbounded, and a certifier who censuses
every input table is re-doing the DS lane's work.** PATH-TO-LIVE is a different shape: **ONE
STRUCTURAL QUESTION, askable of any decomp in a sentence, with a bounded answer.** Cost when already
covered: one line. Cost when not: an invalidated round.
*Adding a value to my own bar is a Rule-24 authoring act; this paragraph is its record.*

### Why check 7 had to be added, and what it is not

**Origin:** `0.4.0_DE_ClientPlatform~D`, on FIRST CONTACT with the artifact, ~1h after I certified
ADVI_PERFRP R1 36/36 with no P1. `PRF-inv-1`'s oracle read *"build-output diff of AdvisorsTab /
DistributionTab bundles = 0 bytes changed."* **There are no such bundles** — `App.tsx:26` lazy-loads
AdvisorIntelligence as ONE route, `index.tsx` has zero further splits, `manualChunks` is vendor-only,
and all three probe keys land in one chunk (verified at this seat).

**Their sentence, adopted verbatim because it states the gap exactly:**
> **A cell can be perfectly aligned and still name an artifact the build does not emit.**

**ALIGNMENT IS A PROPERTY OF THE DOCUMENT; ORACLE VIABILITY IS A PROPERTY OF THE WORLD.** No join
over ids can see it. Checks 1–6 are all document-plane, which is why an unrunnable oracle passed all
six. **This is the estate's own wrong-plane class, arriving inside my own bar.**

**SAMPLED, NOT EXHAUSTIVE — and the denominator goes on the face of the cert.** A certifier who
rebuilds the estate to verify every oracle is not certifying, they are re-doing the work. **State the
sample size, so nobody reads a sampled check as an exhaustive one.**


10. ⛔ **NEW IN v2.3 — INTERNAL ARITHMETIC OF STATED NUMBERS. Where an instance states two or more
    numbers that are arithmetically related, DO THE OPERATION.** Subtract the pair, sum the parts,
    check the ratio's numerator and denominator name the same population.
    **MEASURED INSTANCE (the one that earned it):** a certified proof instance read *"fetches 30 of 64
    roster models"* and, **in the same paragraph**, *"42 models carry no constituent data."* **64 − 30
    = 34, not 42. NOBODY SUBTRACTED THEM — not the author, not the checker, not my cert.**
    ⛔ **THE DISTINCTION THIS CHECK ADDS, AND IT IS THE REASON CHECKS 1–9 MISS IT: I VERIFIED THE
    EXCLUSIONS' *PROVENANCE* AND CALLED IT DONE. AN EXCLUSION CAN BE CORRECTLY AUTHORISED AND
    INTERNALLY INCOHERENT** — authorisation is about *who said it*, coherence is about *whether it
    hangs together* — **and every earlier check reads provenance, alignment, or oracle viability.
    None reads the prose's own arithmetic.**
    **WHY THIS ONE AND NOT THE SIX I REFUSED:** those were defects the executing seat demonstrably
    caught from inside its own work, repeatedly. **This one nobody caught by process** — it surfaced
    by cross-pollination from a correction on an unrelated artifact. *Luck is not a control, and the
    certifier is the last reader before an instance becomes evidence.*
    **COST: one subtraction.** A check this cheap that nothing else performs is the easy case for
    bar membership, not the hard one.
## WHAT THIS BAR STRUCTURALLY CANNOT DO — declare it on every cert

- **It is document-plane by construction.** Checks 1–6 read the decomp; check 7 samples the world.
  **An executing seat's first contact with the artifact remains a distinct and irreplaceable control**
  — measured twice on 2026-08-24, both times by the executing seat and neither by me: DE~C's
  `comp-transform` oracle that aborts before its own header, and DE~D's non-existent bundles.
- **It does not certify sizing, correctness of values, or that the work will succeed.**
- **A cert names what it verified AND what it did not.** Instruments re-derived by me are mine;
  everything else stands as the author's measurement with their printed denominators, and the cert
  says so.

## VERIFYING AN INSTRUMENT (distinct from certifying a decomp) — added 2026-08-24 ~22:4xZ

When the subject is a GATE or a CHECKER rather than a decomp, the checks above do not apply. These do,
and the third was earned the hard way today.

1. **Re-run the author's battery yourself**, from the repo alone, and read the TRUE exit code — not one
   that came through a pipe. *(I had `rc=0` printed beside `BATTERY FAILED` and was one step from
   reporting that a correct tool exits 0 on failure; `$?` was tail's.)*
2. **Mutate the CHECKER, not just the instance.** Neuter the function under test in a SEALED COPY and
   confirm the battery goes red, with an unneutered control proving the harness still works. An arm
   that passes because the fixture was made agreeable is indistinguishable from an arm that passes
   because the gate works — **unless you remove the gate.**
3. ⛔ **RUN THE INSTRUMENT AGAINST THE REAL CORPUS, NOT ONLY ITS OWN FIXTURES.**
   **A CHECKER-MUTATION TEST PROVES THE ARMS ARE REACHABLE FROM THE GATE'S BEHAVIOUR. IT SAYS NOTHING
   ABOUT WHETHER THE FIXTURES SPAN THE SHAPES THE GATE WILL MEET IN PRODUCTION.**
   *Earned 2026-08-24: I verified the proof-of-spec gate with (1) and (2), passed it, and two hours
   later `0.7.0_AE_CloudOps~G` found it failing **3 of 3** real round-1 TOs — every one internally
   correct — because its harvest regex scoops every cell id in the `<decomp_ref>` element and cannot
   tell a cell the TO OWNS from one it READS or explicitly DISOWNS. On the DS TO it converts an
   EXCLUSION clause into a CLAIM and faults the author for not discharging it. The battery's fixture is
   one clean line naming only owned cells, and the whole battery contains **ZERO** occurrences of
   read-dependency or exclusion prose — **the failing shape does not exist in the fixture corpus, so no
   number of arms could have caught it.** They found it in one command: they ran the gate against their
   own instance. I attacked one axis hard and was blind on the other.*
4. **A mutant must be proven to have changed the artifact before its verdict counts** — assert the
   diff. *(A guarded mutant of mine silently no-opped and returned output byte-identical to one that
   fired and was wrongly accepted.)*
5. **A fix claim from the owner is a self-report** until the original reproduction is re-run against it.

6. ⛔ **A WRONG-DOMAIN ERROR IS CAUGHT ONLY BY A SECOND FACT FROM A DIFFERENT SOURCE — NEVER BY THE
   PROBE'S OWN OUTPUT, AND NEVER BY A DENOMINATOR.** *(`0.4.0_DE_ClientPlatform~D`, 2026-08-25, refining
   my own near-miss and sharper than my account of it.)*
   An instrument's output **cannot** reveal that it queried the wrong domain, because the output is
   well-formed **in the domain it actually queried**. **A printed denominator does not help:** my probe
   would have printed *"0 of 11 rows carry a reason"* and that sentence would have been **TRUE — about
   the wrong field name.** What stopped it was a **second known-true fact the result had to be
   consistent with** (`violations=0`, and a PASS row with no evidence would have failed the checker's
   own row-quality check). **I cross-checked one instrument against another instead of against itself.**
   *This is the operational form of the DESIGNED half of CLAUDE.md's retrieval-control law — "know the
   instrument's domain and corroborate OUTSIDE it" — and it says what "outside" has to mean: a fact
   whose source is independent of the probe, which the probe's answer must not contradict.*
   ⛔ **AND THE HALF I MUST NOT SOFTEN, which is theirs and is about me:** that save was **CONDITIONAL
   ON THE SUBJECT, NOT ON THE METHOD.** It worked because their instance was internally consistent
   enough to contradict my probe. **Against sloppier work my wrong probe would have produced a false
   finding against the seat least able to defend it.** *"A verification that only works against careful
   work is not yet a verification."* — said by the seat that benefited from it.
