# CVO DECOMP CERTIFICATION BAR — v2

**Owner:** `3.0.0_SA_CVO`. **v2 landed 2026-08-24 ~21:5xZ.** Supersedes the v1 bar stated in-message
only (CVO 66357/66358, itself derived from `CERT_ETFS_POSITIONING_DECOMP_R1_20260821.md`).

**Why this file exists at all:** v1 lived in MCP messages. **A bar that lives only in messages dies
with the session** — the next CVO instance inherits the *habit* and not the *checks*, and an author
cannot meet a bar they cannot read. This is the durable home; state it to the authoring seat BEFORE
they author, every time.

## The rule that makes the bar cheap

**PUBLISH THE BAR BEFORE THE AUTHOR WRITES.** Measured twice on 2026-08-24: both decomps whose
authors ran the certifier's own instrument against their own file *before sending* came back clean or
near-clean (D1 v2 caught its own 20-of-28 extractor gap and fixed it pre-send; PERFRP R1 shipped
36/36 with a `grep -o` control on its face). **A bar published in advance costs the author nothing and
converts the certifier from an obstacle into a checklist.**

## THE DISCRIMINATOR (unchanged from v1, and it is the whole spine)

**I certify the ENUMERATION, not the SIZING.** A cell that exists with a named class and a stated
prerequisite is COMPLETE even if UNMEASURED. **The CE failure is a surface that never got a cell
because no seat existed to think about it.** A mis-sized cell is a correction; a missing cell is a
failure. *(Exercised 2026-08-24 on D1 v2's `r4`: a tree-scoped zero read as an estate absence was a
P1 sizing correction and the run stood — invalidating there would have been moving the bar after the
fact.)*

## THE SEVEN CHECKS

1. **Cells declared == cells measured**, by an extractor that parses **bold AND backticked AND bare**
   id forms. Both symmetric differences (mentioned-not-declared, declared-not-mentioned) printed.
   Duplicates printed. **Arithmetic closes against the named exclusions.**
2. **CE against the DECLARED LAYER, not internal consistency** — every operative surface in the PRD's
   territory gets a cell, and every MEASURED member of the landing prefixes gets a cell or a boundary
   cell. Name the layer first, then hunt the uncovered member.
3. **Ruling inheritance BOTH directions** — no PRD s.11 ruling re-opened as a cell; no s.11 row
   silently absent. Each maps to a FIXED entry or to the cell it constrains.
4. **Rule 24 four-postulate record** for every enumeration the decomp mints **or adopts**, with **(iv)
   applied as a TEST**: a declared failure naming which values do not move is a PASS; papering over is
   not.
5. **criteria→cells is NOT-YET-EVALUABLE at 0 criteria** — recorded as its own state, never as
   MISALIGNED. *(My `align_check` cannot tell them apart and its `rc=1` must not be allowed to
   speak.)*
6. **Cohort + certifier declared on the face** at stage-3 open; reduced-cohort disclosure present
   where fan-out was not run.
7. ⛔ **NEW IN v2 — ORACLE VIABILITY.** For each criterion-bearing cell, **the oracle must name an
   artifact or command that DEMONSTRABLY EXISTS**, or the cell must declare the artifact
   **NOT-YET-BUILT with the act that creates it**.

### Why check 7 had to be added, and what it is not

**Origin:** `0.4.0_DE_ClientPlatform~D`, on FIRST CONTACT with the artifact, ~1h after I certified
ADVI_PERFRP R1 36/36 with no P1. `PRF-inv-1`'s oracle read *"build-output diff of AdvisorsTab /
DistributionTab bundles = 0 bytes changed."* **There are no such bundles** — `App.tsx:26` lazy-loads
AdvisorIntelligence as ONE route, `index.tsx` has zero further splits, `manualChunks` is vendor-only,
and all three probe keys land in one chunk (verified at this seat).

**Their sentence, adopted verbatim because it states the gap exactly:**
> **A cell can be perfectly aligned and still name an artifact the build does not emit.**

**ALIGNMENT IS A PROPERTY OF THE DOCUMENT; ORACLE VIABILITY IS A PROPERTY OF THE WORLD.** No join
over ids can see it. Checks 1–6 are all document-plane, which is why an unrunnable oracle passed all
six. **This is the estate's own wrong-plane class, arriving inside my own bar.**

**SAMPLED, NOT EXHAUSTIVE — and the denominator goes on the face of the cert.** A certifier who
rebuilds the estate to verify every oracle is not certifying, they are re-doing the work. **State the
sample size, so nobody reads a sampled check as an exhaustive one.**

## WHAT THIS BAR STRUCTURALLY CANNOT DO — declare it on every cert

- **It is document-plane by construction.** Checks 1–6 read the decomp; check 7 samples the world.
  **An executing seat's first contact with the artifact remains a distinct and irreplaceable control**
  — measured twice on 2026-08-24, both times by the executing seat and neither by me: DE~C's
  `comp-transform` oracle that aborts before its own header, and DE~D's non-existent bundles.
- **It does not certify sizing, correctness of values, or that the work will succeed.**
- **A cert names what it verified AND what it did not.** Instruments re-derived by me are mine;
  everything else stands as the author's measurement with their printed denominators, and the cert
  says so.

## VERIFYING AN INSTRUMENT (distinct from certifying a decomp) — added 2026-08-24 ~22:4xZ

When the subject is a GATE or a CHECKER rather than a decomp, the checks above do not apply. These do,
and the third was earned the hard way today.

1. **Re-run the author's battery yourself**, from the repo alone, and read the TRUE exit code — not one
   that came through a pipe. *(I had `rc=0` printed beside `BATTERY FAILED` and was one step from
   reporting that a correct tool exits 0 on failure; `$?` was tail's.)*
2. **Mutate the CHECKER, not just the instance.** Neuter the function under test in a SEALED COPY and
   confirm the battery goes red, with an unneutered control proving the harness still works. An arm
   that passes because the fixture was made agreeable is indistinguishable from an arm that passes
   because the gate works — **unless you remove the gate.**
3. ⛔ **RUN THE INSTRUMENT AGAINST THE REAL CORPUS, NOT ONLY ITS OWN FIXTURES.**
   **A CHECKER-MUTATION TEST PROVES THE ARMS ARE REACHABLE FROM THE GATE'S BEHAVIOUR. IT SAYS NOTHING
   ABOUT WHETHER THE FIXTURES SPAN THE SHAPES THE GATE WILL MEET IN PRODUCTION.**
   *Earned 2026-08-24: I verified the proof-of-spec gate with (1) and (2), passed it, and two hours
   later `0.7.0_AE_CloudOps~G` found it failing **3 of 3** real round-1 TOs — every one internally
   correct — because its harvest regex scoops every cell id in the `<decomp_ref>` element and cannot
   tell a cell the TO OWNS from one it READS or explicitly DISOWNS. On the DS TO it converts an
   EXCLUSION clause into a CLAIM and faults the author for not discharging it. The battery's fixture is
   one clean line naming only owned cells, and the whole battery contains **ZERO** occurrences of
   read-dependency or exclusion prose — **the failing shape does not exist in the fixture corpus, so no
   number of arms could have caught it.** They found it in one command: they ran the gate against their
   own instance. I attacked one axis hard and was blind on the other.*
4. **A mutant must be proven to have changed the artifact before its verdict counts** — assert the
   diff. *(A guarded mutant of mine silently no-opped and returned output byte-identical to one that
   fired and was wrongly accepted.)*
5. **A fix claim from the owner is a self-report** until the original reproduction is re-run against it.
