## The two points the CTO asked to have attacked

**The 126 runs with no anchor that day.** They prove nothing either way, as the addendum says. They are runs on days the first
witness did not carry the security. Nothing in the record ties the anchor's absence to the reader's behaviour, and nothing rules
it out. They do not weaken the 416; they bound them.

**Limb (b) was written after the two types were seen to pass it.** That is so, and the floor of 100 sits just under the smaller
count. For these two types limb (b) is therefore not a test they passed: it is a statement of the grounds on which they were
admitted. That is acceptable for a judgment, and the addendum says the floor is chosen. It becomes a test only for a type measured
after today. The text would be more exact if it said so in one sentence.

## Note 1: Table B is not evidence about the six's dates

The addendum reports, for the six institutional funds v0.40 named, that on every moved day every priced held row equals the
reader's bar for that day and none the prior day's (181 of 181).

That compares the custodian's mark with the reader's bar. It is what the second witness does when it grades. It is not a check of
the witness. If the reader were a day late on a moved day and the mark were a prior close, the two would be equal, and Table B
would read exactly as it does. The record says the same under "Not established": no grade of the six other than against the
reader's own bar.

Five of the six are not carried by the anchor at all. For them v0.40's sentence still stands: nothing measured says what the bar
is on the days it moves. They are admitted on the type's evidence from other securities, as every outside security of the seven
earlier types was. That is the design's standing limit, not a new one, but Table B should not be read as having lifted it.

## Note 2: why the six repeat is not told

By name they are floating-rate, short-obligation and limited-term funds; two move under 0.1 percent on every moved day. A price
that is truly flat to four digits would repeat without the reader being stale, and the guard would then blank days that are
right. That is cost, not risk. The record does not establish it.
