### 2.3 Rule-24 test, each postulate answered

- **(i) MECE** -- one and only one value per vertical at a vintage: `EMERGING` -> `ACTIVE` -> {`WINDING_DOWN` | `DISSOLVING`} -> `DISSOLVED`, plus the sentinel. - **(iii) INTENT COHERENCE** -- one question only: POSITION IN LIFE. ONE remedy moves every non-sentinel value: a walk assertion from the user-input rung (re-walk moves them together). **WHY TWO VALUES AND NOT ONE -- the Rule-24(iv) reason, stated more sharply than sec.2.3 did:** the ASSERTION remedy is uniform (a walk assertion moves every value), but the TRANSITION remedies are OPPOSITE -- winding down is RETIRE-THE-MEMBERS (SHED, the axis-1 intent decision, irreversible), dissolving is RE-HOME-THE-MEMBERS (a naming act, reversible).
