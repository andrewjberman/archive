It records the measurement, rules that no winner is written, keeps the served convention at END with its reason, and changes what

## The point the CTO asked to have attacked

"I keep serving a convention the measurement shows the oracle does not use."

That is the right thing to do on this evidence, for the reason given: agreeing with another system is not being right, and the
other system's convention is known only by inference from 51 segments. A change that moves about 98,700 account-days, and turns
between 1,260 and 2,590 served returns into nulls because the guard moves with it, should not be made on a count of 50 against 48.
Saying in the record that END does not close, instead of calling the 51 reconciled, is the honest half of keeping it.

## Note 1: "not shown to be less accurate" is true of all three

The reason written beside the served value says END "is not shown to be less accurate than the alternatives". That is so. It is
equally not shown to be MORE accurate. The sentence as written leans toward END; the evidence is silent on accuracy for all
three. What the text can truthfully say is that END is kept because it is the default and no evidence yet prefers another, and
that the one thing measured, agreement with Black Diamond, goes against it. A reader of the method contract should not come away
thinking END was weighed and held.

## Note 2: accuracy is partly a seat's act

The addendum says what would settle the question is the other system's written statement, which is not a seat's act. That settles
what Black Diamond does. The text's own ground is different: which timing is TRUE "depends on when the cash is put to work, which
nothing read here shows".

That can be read from data already held. On the 234 flow-bearing days, the custodian's transactions say whether the deposited cash
bought anything that day, the next, or sat. A count of flow days by when the cash was first invested, on a rule written first,
would not pick a convention; it would say whether the day's return on a deposit is closer to none, to half, or to all, on these
accounts. It is read-only and on a copy. It would also give Andrew something beside the vendor's answer.
