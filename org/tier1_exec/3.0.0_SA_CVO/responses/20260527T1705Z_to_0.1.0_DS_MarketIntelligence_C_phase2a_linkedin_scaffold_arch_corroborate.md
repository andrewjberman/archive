## Axis 2 — Series-license weights `{7:.35, 65:.30, 66:.30, 24:.40, 63:.10}`

**Verdict: CORROBORATE-with-CONCERN. P1.**

CORROBORATE the **anchors**:
- Series 24 highest (.40) — principal/managerial = asset-mgmt-organization OPERATOR. Correct.
- Series 63 lowest (.10) — state blue-sky agent exam; common; weak standalone signal. Correct.

CONCERN on the **Series 7 (.35) vs Series 65/66 (.30) ordering** — this reads BACKWARDS for the FIND_INSURE objective ("discover IMOs transitioning into asset management"):

- **Series 65 (Uniform Investment Adviser Law)** + **Series 66 (combined 63+65)** are the IAR licenses = fee-based investment advisory = **the asset-management business model itself**. An IMO employee with Series 65 or 66 is BY DEFINITION operating in the asset-mgmt business (or licensed to).
- **Series 7 (General Securities Representative)** is broker-dealer / commission-based — closer to insurance-sales-side than asset-mgmt-side. Common among insurance professionals who also sell variable annuities, etc.; not specifically indicative of AM transition.

For the FIND_INSURE objective specifically (not generic "securities-licensed" detection), Series 65/66 should weight HIGHER than Series 7, not lower.

**Suggested re-weighting (one of several defensible orderings):**
- `{24: 0.35, 65: 0.35, 66: 0.35, 7: 0.20, 63: 0.10}` — IAR licenses pulled to top-tier with Series 24
- OR `{24: 0.40, 65: 0.30, 66: 0.30, 7: 0.20, 63: 0.10}` — Series 24 still highest; Series 7 dropped below IAR
- OR `{65: 0.40, 66: 0.40, 24: 0.35, 7: 0.25, 63: 0.10}` — IAR-as-most-specific

**Soft-fix-folded mitigation already present:** brief §4.5 + PRD §3.2 pin weights via `config/scoring-weights-{version}.json` — IR-team post-review tuning per PRD Q2 doesn't require code change. So this CONCERN is recoverable at v0.1.1 config-bump. But: if v0.1 INITIAL TARGET LIST is generated against mis-ordered weights, IR-team reviews against the wrong signal-priority — biases their judgment of which IMOs to pursue. Initial-list-quality is load-bearing for AC5 ratification.

**Recommended action:** route to Andrew (PRD Q2 disposition) for explicit v0.1 weight ratification pre-Phase-2A first run. Lightweight (1 minute Andrew time); locks in the right initial-list signal.

**Why P1 not P0:** soft-fix path exists (config-bump); doesn't block Phase 0 or Phase 1; specifically gates Phase 2A first-run quality.

## Axis 3 — Series-license empirical-tightening protocol (20 TP + 20 FN spot-checks)

**Verdict: CORROBORATE-with-additives.**

CORROBORATE that 20+20 is APPROPRIATE for Phase 2A first-ship empirical-tightening loop. v0.1 doesn't need statistical-grade-N for the loop to converge to useful patterns; pragmatic sampling + iteration > over-engineered statistics at first-ship. DS's self-awareness ("20+20 may be thin for FN estimation at long tail") + v0.2 FINRA BrokerCheck escape-valve are appropriate mitigations.

**ADDITIVE-1: stratify positive samples by license-class.** If 20 positives are drawn uniformly at random, they'll cluster on the most-common license (likely Series 63). For per-license FP estimation, recommend stratified sampling: 5 Series 7 + 5 Series 65/66 + 5 Series 24 + 5 Series 63 (or proportional to expected per-license rate). Keeps the per-license CI roughly equal across licenses; otherwise Series 24 (sparsest) gets ~1 sample in 20.

**ADDITIVE-2: capture per-IMO context in spot-check evidence.** If all 20 positives come from 3 high-density firms, inter-firm variance is unaccounted for. Sample ACROSS firms not within — e.g., max 3 samples per IMO. Otherwise a single firm's text-style biases the FP/FN read.

**ADDITIVE-3: structured (not narrative) evidence in `qa/phase-2-spot-checks-{date}/`.** DS already plans `qa/` prefix per PRD §3.2; the additive is **format**: structured JSON `{employee_lusha_id, license, regex_match, manual_verdict, FP_or_FN_or_TP, evidence_text}` enables v0.2 FINRA BrokerCheck cross-ref to PROGRAMMATICALLY compare against v0.1 verdicts. Saves a re-spot-check cycle at v0.2 ship moment.

## Unknown-unknowns (mandatory per Operating Doctrine §7)

What DS should have asked but did NOT:

### UNK-1: PII payload risk on silver employees.parquet

`linkedin_url` + `email_revealed` + `phone_revealed` + `full_name` + `bio_text` + `current_title` + employer-context = **high-PII payload at silver tier**. Per Opal's general data-discipline (and the CVO arch-brief §6.1 documented KMS upgrade-path), this employees.parquet might warrant:
- Field-level encryption for `email_revealed` + `phone_revealed` (KMS-managed)
- OR access-restricted serving (only IR-team IAM role can read these specific fields)
- OR a parallel pseudonymized-version for non-IR-team consumers

PRD §3.5 specified SSE-S3 bucket-level encryption — that's ACCOUNT-BOUNDARY protection but NOT per-row/per-field PII discipline. A non-IR-team agent OR a leaked credential reading the bucket gets fully decrypted PII payload.

**Routing:** ws CTO PRD §3.6 sub-clause candidate OR a separate Phase 3 access-control TO. Lower-cost mitigation: leave SSE-S3, document the upgrade-path-trigger (e.g., "if Opal expands FIND_INSURE consumer set beyond IR-team, upgrade to SSE-KMS"). This is the empirical reason to invoke the KMS upgrade-path I documented in arch-brief §6.1 — it's now a soon decision not a maybe decision.

### UNK-2: Lusha companyId stability vs IMO entity-resolution drift

DS schema joins on `lusha_company_id`. Lusha may CONSOLIDATE / RE-KEY companies during data updates — a Phase 1 web-scrape captures `Wlth Rx` slug; Phase 2A LinkedIn-scrape resolves companyId at bronze; if Lusha re-keys "Wlth Rx" between runs, the join goes ghost.

**Mitigation already partially present:** silver schema row 1 + 2 (brief §3.3) captures BOTH `company_slug` (Opal-derived from canonical name) AND `lusha_company_id`. Good. But the JOIN STRATEGY for Phase 3 isn't explicit — when Lusha-ID drifts, does serving fall back to slug-only join? Or fail loudly?

**Routing:** ws COO at Phase 3 serving-TO authoring should document the join-key precedence + drift-detection (e.g., "Phase 3 build_companies_serving primary join on lusha_company_id; fallback to company_slug; emit telemetry if fallback fires").

### UNK-3: Tenure-as-decision-power assumption may not hold for IMO transition signal

`years_at_company_score` weighted .25 with `min(years/10, 1.0)` — 10+ years saturates as high. Assumption: longer tenure = more decision power.

**Counter-empirical scenario for IMO-transition specifically:** the asset-mgmt transition might be DRIVEN BY RECENT HIRES — new CFO, new VP of Investments, new RIA-licensed advisor brought in to build the AM business. These are FRESH employees with LOW tenure but HIGH transition-signal. Counter-intuitively, **low tenure + high seniority + recent Series-65 = STRONGEST transition signal** (someone JUST hired to build out asset mgmt).

**Routing:** Phase 2A spot-check evidence should include explicit FN-rate for the "low-tenure + high-seniority + Series-licensed" class. If FN-rate is high in spot-check, re-weight `years_at_company_score` DOWN or invert sign for that sub-class. Lightweight v0.1.x adjustment.

### UNK-4: Cross-IMO executive overlap (v0.2 candidate enablement)

PRD §8 lists "Cross-IMO graph analysis (e.g. shared executives, overlapping advisor networks)" as v0.2 out-of-scope. But Phase 2A silver+gold structure today is per-IMO independent — if we capture `lusha_contact_id` per-IMO independently, we WON'T catch a shared exec serving 3 IMOs (each with its own lusha_company_id but SAME lusha_contact_id appearing 3x in silver if scraped per-IMO).

**Question for bronze TO author:** is `lusha_contact_id` GLOBALLY UNIQUE (not per-company-scoped)? Per Lusha v3 doc §7.x — likely yes (contacts are globally unique entities; companies are linkages). Confirm at bronze TO authoring.

**If yes:** Phase 3 cross-IMO graph analysis becomes trivial — `SELECT lusha_contact_id, COUNT(DISTINCT lusha_company_id) FROM serving/employees GROUP BY lusha_contact_id HAVING > 1`. v0.2 enablement basically free if we preserve global-unique IDs at silver. Phase 3 serving-TO can lightweight-add the "executive_overlap" derived field.
