# Decomposition grammar v1

Read this reference during S1 and S2.

## Declared layer

The grammar classifies the composition operator by which independently meaningful
children reconstruct one parent concept at one declared grain. It does not classify
discovery methods, evidence sources, or reasons a candidate was generated.

This grammar is target-blind. Its version and commit must predate subject selection.
One layer declares one binding grammar version beside its Rule 24 grain. A rival grammar
is a challenge; disagreement routes to the layer owner and cannot be resolved by the
last runner. Any grammar edit is a knowledge-basis change that requires affected current
uses to re-run rather than grandfathering older certificates.

## Operators

### CONJUNCTIVE_DECOMPOSITION

The parent holds only when all children compose. Removing one child changes the parent
at the declared grain. Examples of reconstruction forms include a product of independent
axes or a contract whose separately actionable obligations all bind.

### DISJUNCTIVE_DECOMPOSITION

The parent is a union or alternative whose children can occur separately. Each child is
independently instantiable, and the parent is reconstructed by their governed union.

## Admissibility tests

For a candidate split, answer all five:

1. Grain: are every parent and child at the declared grain?
2. Independence: can each child be instantiated or falsified without the others?
3. Reconstruction: does AND or OR reconstruct the parent without residue?
4. Consequence: does the split change decision, failure direction, remedy, authority, or
   resolution obligation?
5. Non-triviality: are the children more than synonyms, examples, attributes, evidence
   fields, or causal antecedents?

Any NO excludes the candidate from the decomposition frontier with the failed test and
evidence recorded. Exclusion is not proof that no other candidate exists.

## Open generator protocol

Generators are declared per run and preserve their derivation. At minimum, attempt:

- definition analysis;
- usage census across the declared canon and consumers;
- cross-derivation from an independent framing;
- incident variance when incidents exist.

Additional generators are allowed. Do not enumerate generators as if they were mutually
exclusive categories: one candidate may be produced by several independent derivations.

Normalize candidates to the run's grain before union. Preserve raw-to-normalized lineage
so generator agreement can be distinguished from duplicated derivation.

## Frontier closure

The grammar frontier is the Cartesian set:

`declared generator derivations x grammar operators x declared scope partitions`

Every cell must be assessed, correctly empty, or explicitly unreachable. An unreachable
cell prevents `DECLARED_FRONTIER_EXHAUSTED`. A budget closes work, never truth.
