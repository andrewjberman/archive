## §4 — Disagreement rate epistemological observation

`disagreement_rate=0.875` on griffe is significantly higher than sphinx (where SC documented "8 of 10 INVESTIGATE = grader_artifact" pattern, ~80% in the affected subset, before retroactive relabel).

This is exactly the V1.2 §11.1 governance split surfacing what it was designed to surface: 14 of 16 griffe questions show exact-match-fail + semantic-aware-correct divergence. The judge knows the answer; the exact-match grader can't extract it.

This brings the CVO §4 unknown-unknown from `20260427T200500Z` review back into focus directly:

> What you should be asking next is whether response-format sensitivity belongs inside the program claim boundary or outside it. Right now the project implicitly treats format effects as contamination when they inflate retrieval-looking gains. That is defensible. But if a future target shows the opposite pattern — correct localization plus semantically correct answer plus chronic unparseable formatting under all conditions — you will need a preregistered position on whether the program is testing "knowledge delivery to the judge" or "gradeable answer production by the judge."

Griffe is exactly that future target. 87.5% of questions show "judge correct in prose, exact-match fails" — chronic unparseable formatting at near-pool scale.

The implication for primary-case 4-condition scoring on griffe:

- C1 (unaided) and C4 (full two-step) ratchet metrics under exact-match will both be artificially deflated by the format-failure rate
- The ab_lift metric (C4 − C1) might still be meaningful IF both conditions are equally affected by format-failure
- BUT: if the compiler's effect is partly to elicit different response shapes (more formatted, more parseable, or more verbose), then ab_lift on griffe is partly measuring compiler-driven format elicitation, not compiler-driven knowledge delivery
- The semantic-aware grader doesn't drive ratchet per V1.2 §11.1 — it only drives triage and is emitted as a runtime characterization metric

Net: the iter-by-iter ratchet on griffe may be epistemically ambiguous in a way that requires CVO to settle the §4 question before we accumulate iter results that are hard to interpret.
