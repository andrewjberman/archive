## 3. What the evidence says about the two items most likely to be mis-handled

**Item 1 is genuinely unsigned, and the existing pair does not stretch.** The co-sign on file
(`ledger/NEVER_WIDEN_ROUND13_DRAFTING_CACHE_REBUILD_{CTO,COO}_HALF.md`) covers ONE key,
`drafting/v1/BA_US_Equity.json`, and its NOT-COVERED list says so in terms: *"Any second key. No other name's drafting
object, no enumeration, no loop."* Reading it as authority for eleven more keys would be the exact widening the half
was written to prevent.

**And the eleven are not one population.** Before a pair is written, the names split by a measured question: does the
name's STORED analysis object already carry verified points with `span` and `passage_id`? Where it does, the rebuild is
the same pure transform as the signed one (`build_drafting_document` makes no model call; `write_pool` skips an
identical digest), so the blast radius is N keys of one writer and the old versions are the rollback. Where it does
not, the only way to a verified shape is re-analysis, which reaches the vendor -- and the vendor cadence is RESERVED to
Andrew. **The co-sign must therefore name the two arms and halt on the second, or it is not signable.**

**Item 4 is two different defects wearing one sentence.** Commentary at 5 of 33 and posture at 2 of 33 are PRODUCTION
coverage: the objects were never written for the other names. The scores field is a CONTRACT gap: 66 score objects
exist and the served bundle has no field to carry them. The first is a pull/producer cell; the second is an adapter
and bundle-shape change that needs no new data at all. Cutting them as one cell would leave whichever half is cheaper
to satisfy standing in for both.

- **Do not cut round 14 before round 13's slug.** It deadlocks round 13's L10 until round 14 fully drains, and the cost
  lands on the CVO, invisibly to whoever cut it.
- **Count the writers before signing anything in item 1.** A property with N writers needs N rules read, and the half
  prints N.
- **Print evidence must be built from the page's own call.** A direct invoke with a hand-built key is evidence about
  the key, not about the route; this project has already recorded one wrong-object 404 that read as a product defect.

  build-correctly-and-port question for Andrew, not a parallel pipeline.
- **A served-plane claim carries its denominator and its vintage** -- 33 names, at a named commit and a named read
  time -- or it is not a claim this seat will carry into a report.
