Prepared for Andrew's exec summary, at his request. Fifteen outcomes, every one of them named.

## The one-paragraph shape, before the list

**Nothing is realized. Fifteen of fifteen.** That is not a coverage gap and never has been --
every outcome has a cut order behind it, the coverage read came back empty, and forty-three
orders have run to completion across seven rounds with three more in flight tonight. A second
seat checked that same claim tonight from the other end and by a different method -- reading the
requirements half rather than counting the orders, and subtracting rather than reading down the
list -- and got fifteen outcomes declared, fifteen covered, none uncovered and none invented. Two
instruments, not one seat agreeing with itself.

**It is one gate, and it is his.** Fourteen of the fifteen outcomes are written so that they are
only done when *a person has actually used the thing* -- not when it is built, not when it passes
its checks. The page those people would use cannot be put in front of them: the standing word on
this project is *commit yes, promote no*, and it is his own. Until he lifts it, the fourteen
cannot move however good the work behind them is, and the fifteenth (the only one that is done
when the marking is merely in place) still needs the page to exist to be marked on.

**What the rounds bought, then, is the thing that is true on the day he lifts it.** Round 7's
finding is the honest summary of where that stands: the back end was repaired and the page still
could not read it -- thirteen of thirteen header figures joined and zero of one hundred and
eighty-three fiscal figures did, because the page asks for a figure under a different name than
the producer writes it. That join is being fixed tonight. It is arithmetic, not flakiness.

## Three findings that bear on what a grade means -- or on what a reader would believe

**The first changes no grade, and one of the numbers in circulation is wrong -- including one I
put there.** Three different counts of "carried obligations" have been quoted on this project
tonight. *Nine* is correct for what it names: nine open items on the most recent round's remedy
list, a complete and bounded block, and tonight's three cells were subtracted from exactly that
list with nothing left over. *Thirty* is **wrong** -- it was my correction of an earlier count and
it read two documents of a fifteen-document record. The true standing figure over the whole record
is **fifty-three**. None of the three touches an outcome's grade: they count obligations this seat
carries, not outcomes he is owed. I am correcting it here rather than letting it reach him.

**The second is genuinely a fact about what a grade means.** The panel work is verified and
*unpublishable*: the evidence sits on one local branch and is reachable from none of the shared
ones, while the pointer the project records is the older one. The rule that follows -- that a
portal-dependent outcome can at best be graded *exercised*, never *realized*, while that is true --
had been written down in a round analysis and in a tick log. Both are durable, and **neither is read
by the step that takes a grade.** It has been moved onto the record the grader actually reads. This
matters because the sibling project on this box graded eight outcomes realized against something
that had never run once; without this, a round could have graded a page outcome realized off its
own artifact, which is precisely that failure.

**The third is somebody else's catch and changes no grade either, but it would have misled a
reader.** The requirements document lists the things that had to be true before this work could
stand. One of them says the research data store exists, and names it. **That store no longer
exists** -- it was renamed during the harvest consolidation on his own word and the old one was
deliberately deleted. The precondition is still genuinely satisfied, by the store that exists
today; the sentence naming the old one was simply left behind when the rename was applied one
field over. Nothing downstream reads it, because that section says in its own words that these are
preconditions and that no outcome is graded on them. And the larger version of it, which is not
this project's to fix: **the estate's own picture of what is running in the cloud still lists that
deleted store as present**, on a build stamped after the deletion -- checked against the live
service with both a name that should exist and a name that never did, so the check does
discriminate. Three seats cited that picture as live tonight.
