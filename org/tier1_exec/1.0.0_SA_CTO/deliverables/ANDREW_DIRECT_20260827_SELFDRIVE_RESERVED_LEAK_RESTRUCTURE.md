## The diagnosed leak (measured on the skill's own runs)

The old RESERVED definition included *"anything a ruling explicitly carried 'to Andrew'"* — a hook
that catches **every card ever surfaced to him**. Surfacing a call once made it RESERVED forever;
the class devoured the queue; ticks classified zero adjudicable while he approved every rec
outright (his own stated base rate). The classifier confused **a call that was SURFACED to him**
with **a decision class the LAW reserves to him** — two different things, and only the second is a
reservation.

## The restructure (landed in `.claude/skills/self-drive/SKILL.md` §1 + §2)

1. **ADJUDICABLE is the DEFAULT.** RESERVED is the cited exception: only a NEVER-class law line or
   a verbatim standing Andrew hold that names the DECISION CLASS, quoted on the card. No quote =
   ADJUDICABLE by definition.
2. **CVO validates the reservations** — his "CVO come in to help" mechanism: every tick's pitch
   carries BOTH lists; CVO confirms each RESERVED citation or re-classes and adjudicates in the
   same reply. Over-reservation cannot survive a tick.
3. **The all-RESERVED tell:** a uniformly-RESERVED tick is presumptively misclassified and says so
   in its own output.

Nothing lifts a NEVER — the restructure stops ordinary judgment calls from being COSTUMED as
NEVERs. Genuine NEVERs (served-output never-widen, vendor egress, spend/contracts/entitlements,
hold-lifts by name) remain his, and there are few of them — which is the point.
