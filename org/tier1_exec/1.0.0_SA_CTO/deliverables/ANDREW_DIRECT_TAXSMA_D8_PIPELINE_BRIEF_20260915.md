## I. Outcome territory

### O1. Traded accounts and prospects have distinct, defensible status

The pipeline identifies the complete expected population of Tax SMA accounts already traded, separately from prospects, accounts awaiting opening, opened accounts and funded accounts that have not entered the strategy. The workbook's explicit trading status is the starting business evidence. Readiness, an account number, funding, an open custodian status or a strategy label alone must not activate membership. An operator can explain the evidence for each included or excluded account and any unresolved disagreement. Accounts do not need to trade every day to remain live.

### O2. Workbook identities reconcile to the correct custodian population

Every expected traded account is traced by its complete account identity to the intended 0833 feed and the daily value source. The comparison also discovers Tax SMA candidates at the custodian that the workbook does not list, without automatically treating them as live. Missing identities, duplicates, masked or malformed numbers, master changes and conflicting strategy labels are visible, actionable exceptions. Unknown or conflicting identities cannot quietly become trusted membership. The business meaning of the onboarding money-manager master and the reporting feed master remains distinct.

### O3. A newly traded account reaches the entire data path

An authorized business-status change leads to a versioned expected account population, verified source coverage and a daily category contribution without a hardcoded account list or a silent manual enrollment gap. Changes, corrections, deactivations and transfers preserve their effective dates and history. The expected population drives the completeness check; merely processing the files that happened to arrive is insufficient. A technical identifier-resolution failure remains a pipeline exception for a traded account rather than reclassifying it as a prospect.

### O4. Daily Tax SMA value has one explicit, traceable basis

For the validated traded population, daily assets come from the established 0833 account valuation plane, with its business date, source lineage and stated treatment of cash, shorts, margin and accrual limitations. The value is not the workbook's rounded sales estimate, gross leveraged exposure, a multiplier of strategy leverage, a sum of tax lots or a stale quarterly report. Reuse the existing canonical valuation engine and carry its basis rather than inventing a competing value. Conflicting business/custodian evidence is resolved explicitly; it is not repaired by changing the definition of assets.

### O5. Missing or stale coverage cannot masquerade as a complete total

The consumer can distinguish a complete current cohort, an incomplete cohort, a true zero, an unavailable value and a value outside its valid reporting date. Missing active accounts, unavailable workbook reads and source failures cannot silently shrink the denominator. A historical value may remain available as clearly dated history; it cannot become today's value by changing its label. Freshness is judged against the actual trading calendar and expected delivery time. Partial observations must not be presented as the full Tax SMA total or a complete overall total.

### O6. Tax SMA adds to the intended views exactly once

Validated Tax SMA assets populate the existing Tax-Managed SMA category beneath SMA / ALT Strategies, its parents and the 49 Financial total, and reach Global exactly once. The Client residual is unchanged by this additive contribution. Other firms are unchanged. The source declares its additive basis independently of the category registry, and a disagreement is refused. The account population is checked for overlap with the existing base population so the historical additive premise cannot drift silently. The existing category vocabulary and display composition are reused.

### O7. The result is explainable without exposing account information

An authorized operator can follow membership, exclusions, exceptions, business dates and source versions from workbook to account value to the category result. Consumer outputs carry the necessary dates, coverage and limitations while retaining the current boundary against client names, complete account numbers and other private onboarding information. Operational evidence remains available in the authorized data plane rather than being copied into consumer views or ordinary repository artifacts.

### O8. The pipeline can be operated, challenged and recovered deliberately

The run mechanism has a supported owner, an observable completion/failure record and a repeatable recovery path. A controlled run exercises a new activation and correction; deliberately missing, duplicated, conflicting, delayed and unavailable inputs demonstrate the corresponding refusal or visible incomplete result. Repeating or replaying a run does not duplicate contribution, and recovery restores a correctly dated result. Validation can be driven on demand with controlled records or existing history; no outcome waits for a naturally occurring outage or a calendar event. Existing producers and consumers continue to work across rollout and reversal.
