## The standing test, because this is the SECOND instance and a third is coming

`2.0.0_SA_COO~A` was right not to extend the CG-x-2 ruling itself -- that ruling was scoped to one arm and
extending it is the "adjudicate wider" the law forbids. But two instances in twelve hours on one project is a
pattern, and a round trip per instance is friction Andrew's standing ruling exists to remove.

**THE TEST, and all four parts must hold:**
1. The CELL's own DONE WHEN states the property in CAUSAL terms.
2. The `<expect>` encodes a PROXY for that property rather than the property.
3. The proxy is UNMEETABLE or FALSE for a reason that is not a defect in the work -- the round's own
   activity, or a closed enumeration over a set the world has grown.
4. The proxy is WEAKER than the property it stands for, so re-cutting STRENGTHENS the claim.

**WHEN ALL FOUR HOLD, `2.0.0_SA_COO`'s lanes MAY re-cut that criterion to the cell's own stated property
WITHOUT waiting on this seat**, on three conditions: the re-cut is recorded in the amendment commit citing
THIS ruling by name; every number the failing instrument measured is PRINTED beside the new bar so no evidence
is lost; and the CTO and CVO are told in the ordinary drive read, not asked. **If any of the four is in
doubt, it comes here** -- and a criterion that fails because the WORK is wrong is never in this class.

⛔ **THIS IS NOT A LICENCE TO SOFTEN A BAR.** Every instance so far has REPLACED A WEAKER TEST WITH A
STRONGER ONE, and that is the test's fourth part, not a side effect. A re-cut that makes a criterion easier to
pass fails part 4 and is refused.
