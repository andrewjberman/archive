## 1. THE LAW -- "NO FWD IS A WAIT"

**An endpoint outcome is provable by an act a seat can take ON DEMAND.** An outcome whose only proof is an event nobody controls -- a
failure, a rare condition, a calendar -- is written WRONG, and the remedy is not patience: it is re-cut so its MECHANISM is driven
deliberately, with the organic case kept as the negative arm. **Where a wait is genuinely essential it is EXPRESSLY QUALIFIED on the
outcome itself, carrying its reason and his word, and it is the exception that names itself.** The default is: not a wait.

Landed the same turn in `CLAUDE.md` rule 27 (the L2 limb) and in the spine's L2 text (v3.10), because an endpoint is written once and
graded for the life of a project: a wait written in at L2 is paid for at every L10 until someone notices.
