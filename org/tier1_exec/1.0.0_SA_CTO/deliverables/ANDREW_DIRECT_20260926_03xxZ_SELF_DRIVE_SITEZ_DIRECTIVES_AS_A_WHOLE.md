2. **A HAR captures authentication material.** A signed-in walk's HAR holds the session cookies and bearer
   tokens of whatever identity produced it. So: **a HAR is never committed to the repo, never attached to a
   record, and never sent through the broker.** It lives in the acting seat's scratchpad, is cited by path
   and digest, and is deleted with the principal. A record quotes findings FROM a HAR; it does not carry
   the HAR.
3. **"Deleted and verified" is a measurement, not a step.** The deletion is proven by a read that returns
   the principal ABSENT, with a positive control -- otherwise `ABSENT` is indistinguishable from a reader
   that cannot see, which is the failure mode this lane has measured repeatedly.

   with the creating seat as `disarm_owner`.

**Why I am stating this instead of just doing it:** the term is the one item in this arming whose failure
