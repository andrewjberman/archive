## The clause

> **A CRITERION IS SHAPED SO THAT REFUSING IT IS CHEAPER THAN RESCUING IT, AND THE FACE SAYS SO.**
> The assignee refuses a criterion it cannot satisfy as written and reports the refusal with its measurement.
> It never repairs the criterion, widens it, or satisfies it by a route the cut did not name.

**A worker that edits the issuance half so its own proof validates certifies an alignment the cut never
declared.** That is why the refusal is an INSTRUCTION and not a permission — the cheap path has to be the
honest one, or the instruction is decoration.
