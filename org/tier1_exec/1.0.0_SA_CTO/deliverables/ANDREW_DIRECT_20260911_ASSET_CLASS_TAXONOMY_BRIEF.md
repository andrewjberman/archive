## THE SUBSTANCE THE TRANSCRIPT ESTABLISHES

Cottonwood asked to break **volatility products and gold** out of the equity bucket -- to stop hedge and
volatility positions inflating the equity figure. Andrew and Young generalised that into **FIVE asset
classes** that REPLACE the old scheme, applied consistently across every surface that shows asset classes,
and originating from ONE central place rather than being re-implemented per page.

### The five classes -- canonical order, colors, and what each holds

| # | class | color | what it holds |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Fixed Income / Cash | green | fixed income and cash |
| 2 | Equity | blue | equities only -- **volatility and hedge positions REMOVED to Structured** |
| 3 | Structured | pink | options, VIX futures, volatility overlays, VXX and the like |
| 4 | Commodities | gold | physical (gold, nickel, silver), crypto, and commodity indices (e.g. GSCI) |
| 5 | Alternatives | orange | remaining alts -- real assets (not commodities), private equity, private credit, real estate |

### Where it must appear, and how it must be sourced

- **Three surfaces**, all agreeing: the **Positioning** page, the **Philosophy** page, and the
  **Capital Market Assumptions**.
- **One central source.** The classification originates in one place (the ETF classifier function, or
  wherever it truly lives) and flows to every surface -- never re-implemented per page. Andrew stressed a
  generalisable, consolidated approach.
