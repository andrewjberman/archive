## ⛔ TWO THINGS THE CUT SURFACED, BOTH FOR ANDREW BEFORE THIS IS RATIFIED

### 1. FWD-6's BAR REACHES INTO A SIBLING PROJECT, SO I CUT THE BAR LOWER THAN THE BRIEF WROTE IT

The brief's Part III bar for O6 is *"a migration project starts from the ledger's rows rather than
from a fresh census."* But Part II says **migrations are SEPARATE PROJECTS, one per pipeline
family.** So that bar cannot be graded inside D14 — it is met, or not, by a project D14 does not own
and may not exist for months.

**I cut FWD-6 at `APPLIED`** — every part carries its decision, and the exceptions are auditable —
rather than at the `EXERCISED` the brief's bar implies. ⚠ **This is me lowering a bar the brief set,
and it is the kind of move that should never pass silently**, so it is stated here rather than
absorbed. If Andrew wants the higher bar, FWD-6 becomes ungradeable until a migration project runs,
and D14 cannot close on its own.
