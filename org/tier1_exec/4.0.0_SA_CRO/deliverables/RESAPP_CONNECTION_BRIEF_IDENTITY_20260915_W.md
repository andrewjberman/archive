## Required connection

Choose one canonical security identity for RESAPP and persist a versioned,
explicit mapping from that identity to its Bloomberg identifier, display
ticker, existing model/pack key, and existing publication-association keys.
Carry the original universe row and identifier provenance. Resolve the
identity once at the route boundary and pass the same resolved identity
through the overlay, model, coverage, ranking and later Posture paths.

The mapping must preserve exchange/share-class distinctions and tolerate an
unresolved vendor identifier without inventing one. Do not solve this by
blindly appending `US Equity`, stripping exchange suffixes, or treating a
folder rename as a new security. The five pilots and the other 84 names
must participate in the same declared identity system.

Existing article bodies need no migration: they are globally keyed by
`publisher:article_id`. Either teach the read adapter the recorded legacy
association aliases or perform a separately verified link migration. In
either case, an old and a new link for the same article must count once.
Preserve the old links until the chosen transition is verified; no deletes
or broad backfill are requested here.
