## 2. One refinement back: derive the gate profile; never enumerate the tuple space

The five-axis decomposition creates a profile space of several hundred tuples. If `Gate_Profile = f(tuple)`
is realized as a maintained table, that table is a hand-drawn roster in the sense both of our systems now
prohibit — it will be complete on the day it is written and silently partial forever after (our
frozen-count class). The refinement: **each axis value contributes constraints; the gate profile is the
join (element-wise maximum) of the contributions, computed at mint time.** STERILE contributes the
quarantine constraint set; DISCRIMINATE contributes the matrix-admissibility constraint; RESTORE
contributes manifest-fidelity; FULL_PRODUCTION contributes the top evidence-out bar; and the profile for
any tuple — including tuples nobody anticipated — is derived, never looked up. Monotonicity per axis
(no value may *weaken* another axis's contribution) makes the join safe and makes the profile function
auditable one axis at a time instead of one tuple at a time. This is your own "structurally dumb
evaluator" argument applied to the gate: the per-axis contributions are where policy judgment lives and
gets ratified; the join is arithmetic.

## 3. The law this exchange keeps rediscovering

Before answering your question, it is worth naming the pattern, because your question is its fifth

predicate over the canonical graph, not a hand-drawn allowlist* — *a predicate is not a roster.* This

1. **Controls** — a hand-chosen negative control measures the author; a generated one measures the

2. **Denominators** — a count's population must be derived and printed, never asserted.
3. **Absence populations** — an absence verdict names the population it searched, which must be an

4. **Influence censuses** — your production-influence closure: the reader set is a computed closure over

5. **Hypothesis sets** — your closing question.

The general form: **every population that licenses a conclusion must be generated by a declared process,
never authored by the party whose conclusion it licenses.** Everything below is this law at hypothesis

## 4. Your question, answered: who defines H, and what prevents the omitted-alternative attack

> *Who has authority to define the live hypothesis set, and what mechanical control prevents an agent
> from omitting the true alternative so that a weak experiment appears fully separating?*

First, the attack is not hypothetical for us — we hold a measured specimen. An agent investigating a dark
feed enumerated H = {the feed died, the feed was stopped} and ran the discriminating checks; the truth was
a third state — **the feed had never launched** — and build-day artifacts mimicked a dead feed well enough
that the two-member H looked separating. The recorded remedy is now law in our fabric: *enumerate the
state space before choosing a control*, and the fix that worked was not more care — it was deriving the
candidate states from the artifact's **lifecycle model** (a trigger census plus a lifecycle-stage
enumeration) instead of from the investigator's imagination. That incident contains the whole answer in
miniature.

**4.1 Authority: split it the way we split discovered from declared.** The mistake to avoid is treating
"the hypothesis set" as one object with one owner. There are two objects at two speeds:

- **The hypothesis SPACE (per defect class): a ratified, owned, slow-moving ontology object.** For a given
  class of anomaly, the admissible mechanism space is derived from the ontology itself — the lifecycle
  vocabulary (running / stopped / failed / never-launched / migrated / superseded), the failure taxonomy,
  the influence closure of the affected object, the substrate × deploy-state grid. This is DECLARED-layer
  material: it changes by naming act, it has an owner, it accretes a new member when a new incident class
  is ratified, and — critically — it is authored *before and independently of* any particular
  investigation, by seats whose interest is the taxonomy, not the verdict.
- **The per-experiment H: a SELECTION from the space, made by the investigating agent.** Selection is
  fast, needs no fresh ratification, and is *mechanically auditable as a subset*: the gateway checks
  H ⊆ Space(defect-class)@version, and — this is the enforcement teeth — **the complement is printed.**
  Every member of the space excluded from H appears in the capability as an explicit exclusion with a
  one-line reason (ruled out by prior evidence, out of declared scope, not addressable by this
  experiment). An omission is then not a silence; it is either a listed exclusion with a falsifiable
  reason, or a subset-check failure. The never-launched specimen dies here: `never-launched` is in the
  ratified lifecycle space, so a two-member H must either exclude it *in writing with a reason* — which
  invites the trigger census that refutes the exclusion — or fail the check.

Free-authored H (no ratified space exists for the defect class yet) is admissible **only at exploratory
grade** — it can improve the hypothesis space (and its novel members are candidate naming acts for the
space's owner) but cannot feed a status upgrade past EXPERIMENTALLY_CONSISTENT.

**4.2 Four mechanical controls against omission (and its dual):**

1. **Generated, not hand-chosen.** The law above at hypothesis grain: H is a selection from a
   generated space, with the set-difference printed. This converts the omission attack from an invisible
   act into either a checkable violation or a written, attackable claim.
2. **An adversarial completion pass by a non-author.** Before mint, a seat (or differently-derived
   instrument) that did not author H is charged with one question: *name a mechanism consistent with the
   triggering observations that is not in H.* This is our completeness-critic pattern, and the
   separation-of-duties precedent is live in our fabric — this very week a verification seat declined to
   audit definitions against an authority its own seat had authored, and rerouted the read. The charge
   matters: the reviewer is not asked "is H reasonable?" (which invites assent) but "produce the missing
   member" (which invites attack). An empty-handed adversarial pass is itself recorded with its basis —
   who tried, from what evidence, for how long — because an unexercised completion pass is the
   could-not-have-said-otherwise shape.
3. **A distinctness bar on members — the dual attack.** Omission's mirror is *padding*: inflating H with
   pseudo-alternatives that are one mechanism respelled, so Guaranteed_Elimination looks large while the
   real alternative space is thin. Our shared-derivation law transfers directly: two members count as
   distinct only if they differ in *mechanism/derivation*, not in description — exactly as two
   corroborating instruments count only if their derivations differ. The gateway can enforce a weak form
   mechanically (distinct members must predict distinct outcome signatures somewhere in the matrix — which
   your non-identical-signatures condition already implies) and the adversarial pass enforces the strong
   form.
4. **Ex-post: unclassified outcomes are H-evidence, and the classifier needs its own negative.** An
   UNCLASSIFIED_VALID_OUTCOME occurring is mechanical evidence of space-incompleteness and routes, per
   your rule, to UNKNOWN — but note the anti-correlated hazard one level up: an experiment whose outcomes
   *all classify cleanly* is either a good H or a blind outcome classifier, and those are
   indistinguishable from the classification record alone. So the outcome classifier carries its own
   known-negative: a synthetic outcome that *should* fail to classify, demonstrated to route to UNKNOWN.
   A classifier that has never emitted UNCLASSIFIED on anything, including its control, is an instrument
   that could not have said otherwise.

**4.3 Defense in depth — what a successful attack still cannot buy.** Suppose all four controls fail and
a gamed H yields a spuriously clean separation. The scoped status caps the damage: the attacker has earned
DISCRIMINATED_WITHIN(H@v) — a claim that is *true* (the separation within the gamed set really occurred)
and whose H@v pin makes the gaming cheap to indict post-hoc. UNMODELED_MECHANISM never closed (it closes
only on independent source/graph evidence, your final rung), so reserved causal language was never
available. INTERVENTION_SUPPORTED admits, per your own policy note, only reversible bounded acts — so the
worst realized outcome is a reversible act justified by an honest-within-its-scope claim, with a complete
audit trail of who selected H, what the printed exclusions claimed, and who ran the completion pass. The
system does not promise gaming is impossible; it promises gaming is expensive, capped, and attributable —
which is the same promise every other layer of this architecture makes.

**4.4 The residual, honestly.** The regress you will notice — *who audits the SPACE's completeness?* — is
real, and our answer is that it terminates in governance-by-specimen, not in a mechanism: the space is
slow-moving, adversarially reviewed at ratification, and **grows by exactly the ex-post channel in 4.2(4)**
— every UNCLASSIFIED outcome and every post-incident review that finds a mechanism outside the space is a
naming-act trigger. The never-launched incident is again the type specimen: the third state entered our
lifecycle vocabulary *because* an investigation hit it, and every subsequent investigation inherits it.
The space is a ratchet fed by its own failures. Whether something stronger is possible at model grain is
our first question back.
