## 0. The one law, and how to read this document

Every mechanism below is one law applied at a grain:

> **A population that licenses a conclusion must be generated by a declared process, never authored
> by the party whose conclusion it licenses.**

— controls (VL#10), denominators, absence populations, reader/influence censuses, hypothesis sets,
proving subjects, failure domains, harm spaces. The exchange's second finding binds the document
itself: **adoption is an authoring act** (Rule 24 as widened+scoped 2026-08-20) — every schema below
carries a compact four-postulate record; an implementer who adopts any of them re-runs the record.

The exchange's five-layer model, with the estate's honest score today: **Ontology**
RATIFIED+BUILT (lock-down live) · **Epistemology** RATIFIED, enforcement partial · **Causal
reasoning** OPEN (no assessment object) · **Governance** RATIFIED+BUILT (law + hooks; no epoch
service) · **Lifecycle** WEAKEST BUILT (view-time compile + retraction law; no leases). This PRD is
the build plan for the bottom three.

## 2. The core objects (schemas, compact; each with its Rule-24 record)

**2.1 Observation_Record** — instrument id+version, request hash; acquisition/transport/enumeration/
parse status; plane, population predicate, denominator+grain, traversal residue; substantive result;
control references + discrimination-certificate ref; observed_at, support pointers, invalidation
predicates. **Governing rule: any absent/failed status ⇒ substantive_result = UNKNOWN — not zero, not
empty, not pass.** *R24: layer = one instrument invocation's return; status fields are per-stage
axes (not one flattened result enum — the Response VIII lesson applied in advance); CE hunted against
the acquisition pipeline's stages.*

**2.2 Discrimination_Certificate** — instrument×version×plane×population×grain; declared failure
directions; isolated positive control; generated negative/mutant; observed discrimination; **shared
failure domain of instrument+controls + at least one independence crossing**; vintage; invalidation
predicates. Termination of the verifier regress: *a demonstrated contrast across an independence
cut* — local, expiring, never global trust. *R24: layer = one attestation act; the two genuinely new
fields vs existing registry vocabulary are shared-failure-domain and invalidation predicates (7 of 10
already ratified — reuse, don't re-coin).*

**2.3 Knowledge_Basis** — {hypothesis_space@v, normal_form@v, compatibility_policy@v,
scope_ontology@v, outcome_vocabulary@v}. A signature edit or normalization change invalidates as
surely as a new member. Change protocol: ADD/RETIRE/SPLIT/MERGE/EDIT_SIGNATURE/EDIT_SCOPE/
EDIT_PRECONDITIONS/CHANGE_NORMALIZATION — each classified by **computed authority effect**
(Allowed(K_after) \ Allowed(K_before)), never by opcode.

**2.4 Hypothesis governance (S/L/T/R)** — ratified Space per defect class (slow, owned, generated by
the ensemble: lifecycle × influence × obligation-inversion × substrate-state × incident × delta
generators, normalized before union, fixed-point closed, CLOSED_UNDER certified, model-mutant
challenged); per-experiment selection with **the complement printed** (exclusions in writing, only
RULED_OUT_BY_EVIDENCE — carrying bundle+lease — removes from L; NOT_ADDRESSABLE stays in R and
**counts as a survivor under every outcome**). Discrimination floor: Worst_Case_Residual ≤ K;
Guaranteed_Elimination integer fields; INSTRUMENT_FAILED is operational, never evidentiary;
DECLARED_TOTAL naming; UNCLASSIFIED→UNKNOWN; UNMODELED_MECHANISM explicit and mechanically
unclosable. Status ladder: INFERRED → EXPERIMENTALLY_CONSISTENT → DISCRIMINATED_WITHIN(T@v,R@v,
scope@v) → INTERVENTION_SUPPORTED → SOURCE_OR_GRAPH_CONFIRMED. *R24: each set/enum carries its own
record in the implementing spec; the ladder's layer = claim-strength rungs, CE closed by the
UNKNOWN/UNMODELED members.*

**2.5 Eligibility_Evaluation** — separate, dated, attested object over an immutable assessment
(*metadata about a record is not the record*): prior/current knowledge basis, intersecting changes,
exclusion proofs (necessity-aware: incompatible with EVERY admissible realization — attested absence
may prove; untyped silence never does), residual before/after, policy predicate applied, result
ELIGIBLE/INELIGIBLE/UNKNOWN with one-evaluation-per-(assessment,scope). The invalidation pipeline:
coarse coordinate intersection (over-include, never under-include) → exclusion-proof replay →
residual recomputation → policy application. **A new member is never covered by an old enumerated
waiver; it may be covered by a ratified predicate whose declared scope includes it.**

**2.6 Mutation_Capability** (gateway-minted, proof-carrying, short-lived) — purpose axes
(Epistemic_Intent × Operational_Objective incl. CONTAIN × Transition_Form × Exposure_Class ×
Evidence_Output_Class — never one flattened purpose enum), causal assessment ref, evidence vector,
attestations, authority ref+scope, policy epoch, **knowledge_basis digest**, **harm_model@v** (the
third stamp), live precondition token, transition/rollback/verification manifest hashes, expiry.
The six-element admission (A grounded causal admissibility · B instrument attestation · C authority+
scope · D **atomically bound** precondition token · E reversibility contract · F pre-registered
verification) behind **three gates with typed rejections** (EPISTEMIC_INADMISSIBLE /
AUTHORITY_SCOPE_MISS / PRECONDITION_CHANGED...). Profile compilation: per-axis constraint
contributions → union of requirements/prohibitions, intersection of permissions, pointwise-max
thresholds → **satisfiability check** (contradiction detected, never manufactured coherence).
Effective purpose DERIVED from production-influence closure; declared ∧ derived, derived wins.

**2.7 Transition_Unit** (governed chains; long transitions are NOT long transactions) — immutable
transition manifest (graph, targets, action classes, checkpoints, terminals incl. rollback+
containment); **pathwise residue envelope** (structured vector of incomparable limits, never a
scalar); Cumulative_Residue_Ledger (gateway-maintained; agent self-report inadmissible);
recovery-capacity function + declared points of no return; policy_epoch + knowledge_basis +
harm_model stamps. **Prefix safety at every leg:** residue within envelope ∧ safe terminal reachable
∧ recovery requirement ≤ remaining capacity (**rollback debt priced** — the half-landed-deploy class,
finally instrumented). Support handoff per claim: Required_Validity_Interval(c) ⊆
Actual_Validity_Interval(c); *a lease is not a lock* — tokens/isolation bind check to act. The
opening word accepts the envelope; each checkpoint proves the path remains inside it.

**2.8 Retirement machinery** (authority-expanding knowledge edits) — six objects (Exclusion_Claim /
Advocacy_Attestation / Authority_Effect_Evaluation / Impact_Manifest incl. what-becomes-newly-legal /
Retirement_Decision / Retirement_Reliance_Policy); membership state stays boring
(ACTIVE|RETIRED) with selection-disposition, identity-relations, and usage-observation as separate
axes. The advocate: a challenge control on the universal proof — decomposed result axes (admission /
search state / stop reason / instrument state → compiled verdict); frontier closure over a versioned
realization grammar is the only discharge; **budget exhaustion routes to UNKNOWN, never to
retirement**; Advocacy_Search_Scope ⊇ Claim_Scope (the successor check demands it). *Eligibility may
recover; identity never does* — re-mint, no resurrection. Reliance scaling: **independence COVERAGE
scales with harm, never proof count** (cut-sets over a GENERATED failure-domain model); window
containment per claim; **the acceptance ceiling** — no accumulation of proofs converts an
irreversible act into evidence-authorized; proofs earn the card, the word takes the risk; the
required_authority_class field carries which word (this estate's ratified instance: the principal for
irreversible full-production acts). Zero-delta exemption re-read precisely: a standing ratified
predicate accepted the bounded risk of a fully parity-proved reversible move with empty irreversible
residue — evidence never proved "no risk."

**2.9 Harm model** (the residue vector's generator) — compiled from four generators none of which is
the acting agent (incident taxonomy / obligation inversion / action-class type-signatures / recovery
model), normalized, fixed-point closed, mutant-challenged, versioned; unit dimensions selected at
open with the complement printed; adversarial harm-completion pass ("name a harm these action classes
could produce that is not in the vector") mechanically pre-run from type-signatures; composition
rules ratified with the model. UNMODELED accepted explicitly by the opening authority; the specimen
ratchet grows the model. *Open question carried to the whole-system review: cross-leg emergent
residue vs the reachability condition.*

## 3. Diagnostic laws banked by the exchange (doctrine candidates, cite-ready)

1. The cleaner and more comprehensive a pass appears, the more important it is to demonstrate the
   pass and its controls do not share a total failure domain.
2. Adoption is an authoring act (now law); **a needed precedence rule is the flattening detector**.
3. A census is an observation; isolation is a control; when the census is incomplete, admission
   rests on the control.
4. Untyped silence is never retirement evidence; attested absence may be positive incompatibility
   when the hypothesis necessarily entails presence.
5. Proofs earn the card; the word takes the risk. Eligibility may recover; identity never does.
6. Failure DIRECTION classifies acts as policy-grade, never surface category.
7. A regen is not a review; generated_at and last_review never share a field.
8. Recall surfaces and truth surfaces are different objects (C4's lesson, applied to every index).
