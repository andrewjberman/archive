=== BS_YEAR_1_PRINCIPAL | Year 1 Principal Debt Payments | ftype=Real datatype=Double
    Returns the principal debt payments due during the next fiscal year (designated as Year 1) as reported in Municipal Annual Report Notes relative to the disclosure period.   For General Obligation issuers this field return the amount of general obligation principal outstanding.   For Revenue issuers this field returns the amount of revenue bond principal outstanding. 
=== SUPPLY_CHAIN_COUNT_OVERRIDE | Supply Chain Count Override | ftype=String datatype=Int32
    Specifies the number of suppliers or customers that should be included in either Supply Chain Sum of Top Revenues (DT068, SUPPLY_CHAIN_SUM_TOP_REVENUES) or Supply Chain Sum of Top Costs (DT069, SUPPLY_CHAIN_SUM_TOP_COSTS). The value in this override can only be set to a whole number less than or equal to 20.
=== BS_YEAR_2_PRINCIPAL | Year 2 Principal Debt Payments | ftype=Real datatype=Double
    Returns the principal debt payments due during the second fiscal year (designated as Year 2) as reported in Municipal Annual Report Notes, relative to the disclosure period.   For General Obligation issuers this field return the amount of general obligation principal outstanding.   For Revenue issuers this field returns the amount of revenue bond principal outstanding. 
=== FUND_PER | Fundamental Period | ftype=Character datatype=String
    Period in which the fundamental data will be viewed. The default period is last fiscal year end. This field will only work to override fields for which fundamental data is available. Please refer to Primary Periodicity (DZ373, PRIMARY_PERIODICITY) for a company's primary report periods.  When used with product and geographic bulk data fields, this field sets the starting period from which to obtain the bulk data. === EQY_FUND_RELATIVE_PERIOD | Equity Fundamental Relative Period | ftype=Character datatype=String
    Override field for specifying a single historical fundamental period.  The input should be a combination of a negative number and a periodicity such as CQ, CY, FQ, FY, etc.  Some examples of valid inputs are -3FQ, -1CQ, -4FY, etc. -0 is the most recent period available, -1 the one before that, etc.  This field can be used by itself to go back from today or can be used in conjunction with Equity Fundamental Date (DX242, EQY_FUND_DT) to go back from a specific date.   The complete set of codes are: AQ: Actual Quarterly AS: Actual Semi-Annual AY: Actual Yearly CQ: Calendar Quarterly CS: Calendar Semi-Annual CY: Calendar Yearly FQ: Fiscal Quarterly FS: Fiscal Semi-Annual FY: Fiscal Yearly FC: Cumulative Quarter
=== COMPANY_EXEC_OFFICERS | Company Executive Officers | ftype=BulkFormat datatype=String
    Provides the executive officers of the issuing company, using Bloomberg's latest available executive coverage for the company. Field reflects latest available value based on corporate filings, company websites and news sources.
=== BS_CONTRACTUAL_OBLIG_YEAR_2_3 | Contractual Obligations - Years 2 & 3 | ftype=Real datatype=Double
    Payments due in more than one year but less than three years under an existing, unconditionally binding definitive agreement or contract (or a contract or agreement subject only to customary closing conditions).
