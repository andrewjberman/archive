=== BEST_BROKER_NAME | BEst Broker Name | ftype=Character datatype=String
    Name of the Firm/Broker providing Bloomberg Estimates.
=== BEST_CPS_STDDEV | BEst CPS Std Dev | ftype=Real datatype=Double
    A statistical measure of the dispersion of the contributed CPS estimates around their mean value expressed in the same units as the data. The standard deviation of a consensus expresses the level of agreement amongst the contributed data. A consensus with a high standard deviation would represent more dissent while a lower standard deviation would represent concurrence. Available for Consensus only: Standard, Re-Set and 4 week 
=== BST_BRK_PD_OVR | BEst Broker And Period Override | ftype=Character datatype=String
    The BEst (Bloomberg Estimates) estimates source override. If this value is set, it determines the source of broker estimates and the fiscal periods returned for fields: BE001-BE042 and BE048-BE049.  Format of override is :XFY:ABC, where X=number of time periods, FY=fiscal year, ABC is the broker code. Broker codes can be found on FPDF under Earnings Data: Default Data Source. Available time periods are FY (Fiscal Year), FQ (Fiscal Quarter) and FS (Fiscal Semi-annual).    For high and low guidance values, use ::H for high value, ::L for low value.  To retrieve data for the parent company or consolidated please add 'P' or 'C' to BEst Consolidated Override (BE999, BEST_CONSOLIDATED_OVERRIDE).
=== BEST_ESTIMATE_FCF | BEst Estimate Free Cash Flow | ftype=Real datatype=Double
    BEst (Bloomberg Estimates) estimation of cash from operations minus capital expenditures. This is the consensus of estimated FCF (free cash flow) as provided by the broker.
=== BEST_EPS_STDDEV | BEst EPS Std Dev | ftype=Real datatype=Double
    A statistical measure of the dispersion of the contributed Earnings per share (EPS) estimates around their mean value expressed in the same units as the data. The standard deviation of a consensus expresses the level of agreement amongst the contributed data. A consensus with a high standard deviation would represent more dissent while a lower standard deviation would represent concurrence. Available for Consensus only: Standard, Re-Set and 4 week 
=== BEST_CALCULATED_FCF | BEst Calculated Free Cash Flow | ftype=Real datatype=Double
    Free cash flow estimate computed by multiplying BEst CPS (BE013,BEST_CPS) by the Average Number of Shares (IS060,IS_AVG_NUM_SH_FOR_EPS), then adding Best CapEx (BE879, BEST_CAPEX) to the product. CAPEX always returns negative values.
=== BEST_CPS_BRK_GRID | BEst Cashflow Per Share Broker Estimates | ftype=BulkFormat datatype=String
    Matrix of the latest broker estimates for the cash flow per share (CPS) for the security.  The CPS estimate is calculated as:  (Net Income + Depreciation and Amortization + Other Non-Cash Adjustments + Change in No-Cash Working Capital)/ Basic Weighted Average Number of Shares Outstanding for the period.  The field returns the Broker, Analyst, Date of the Estimate, Current Period Estimate, Current Period Change, Next Period Estimate, Next Period Change.  The criteria each estimate is based on may vary by analyst.  This field may be overridden by the following fields:    BEst Consolidated Override (BE999, BEST_CONSOLIDATED_OVERRIDE),    BEst Fiscal Period Override (BE997, BEST_FPERIOD_OVERRIDE), and    Equity Fundamental Currency (DS215, EQY_FUND_CRNCY).
=== BEST_CAPEX_BROKER_GRID | BEst CAPEX Broker Estimates | ftype=BulkFormat datatype=String
    Matrix of the latest broker estimates for Amount of money a company spends to buy capital assets or upgrade its existing capital assets. The field returns the Broker, Analyst, Date of the Estimate, Current Period Estimate, Current Period Change, Next Period Estimate, and Next Period Change. The criteria each estimate is based on may vary by analyst. This field may be overridden by the following fields:  BEst Consolidated Override (BE999, BEST_CONSOLIDATED_OVERRIDE), BEst Fiscal Period Override (BE997, BEST_FPERIOD_OVERRIDE), and Equity Fundamental Currency (DS215, EQY_FUND_CRNCY).
=== BEST_CPS | BEst CPS | ftype=Real datatype=Double
    BEst (Bloomberg Estimates) CPS reflects the consensus estimate for Cash Flow Per Share (CPS). The consensus estimate is the mean of sell-side analyst estimates.   Note: Available for Broker estimates and Consensus: Standard, 28 Days, Post Event, and Custom. Definition may differ based on certain industry or region specifications. The period can be shifted forward or backward by providing an offset to BEst Fiscal Period Override (BE997, BEST_FPERIOD_OVERRIDE) (3FQ, -2FY, etc.).
=== BEST_PERIOD_END_DATE | BEst Period End Date | ftype=Date datatype=Datetime
    Fiscal period end date of BEst (Bloomberg's estimates) data. This field reacts to the override entered on Best Fiscal Period Override, (BE997,BEST_FPERIOD_OVERRIDE).
