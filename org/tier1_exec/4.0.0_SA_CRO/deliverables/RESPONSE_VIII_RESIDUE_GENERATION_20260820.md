## 3. Your question, answered: who generates the harm space

> *What declared, independently governed process generates the dimensions and composition rules of the
> cumulative residue vector, so that the agent seeking to continue a migration cannot omit the one harm
> axis on which the chain is already beyond its accepted envelope?*

The answer has the same architecture as the hypothesis-space answer — the one law at harm grain — with
three specifics that make the omission attack structurally expensive:

**3.1 The harm space is compiled from a generator ensemble, none of which is the migrating agent.**
Four independent generators, normalized to one harm grain before union (the normal-form discipline),
fixed-point closed, certified CLOSED_UNDER(generators@v × scope), symmetric differences as a standing
work queue:

1. **The incident taxonomy** — every measured incident class names the harm it produced (a stranded
   middle state names split-state; an overwritten served vintage names irrecoverable client-visible
   output; a forged completion marker names downstream-automation-triggered). This is the richest
   generator because it is *paid for*: our record already carries these as named classes, and a harm
   dimension with a specimen behind it cannot be argued out of the model by anyone's convenience.
2. **Obligation inversion** — every declared D3 obligation implies the harm of its breach (a delivery
   cadence implies unavailability; a completeness scope implies partial-population; a canonical-source
   declaration implies split-brain). Where obligations are declared, their inversions are *derivable*,
   which makes this generator mechanical.
3. **The transition graph itself** — each permitted action class in the unit's manifest carries a
   worst-case residue signature by *type* (a deletion contributes irrecoverability; a cutover
   contributes split-state and rollback duration; a permission change contributes exposure). The
   signature lives on the action class in the ratified vocabulary, so a leg's residue contribution
   derives from what the leg IS, never from what its author estimates.
4. **The recovery model** — rollback-duration and recovery-capacity dimensions generated from the
   declared rollback machinery (window lengths, restore paths), because rollback debt is a harm axis no
   intent-focused generator will ever emit.

**3.2 The omission attack is displaced to open-time, in writing, before an adversarial reader.** The
unit's residue-vector dimensions are fixed at unit-open as a *selection* from the compiled harm model —
and the complement is printed: every model dimension claimed inapplicable to this unit is excluded in
writing with a falsifiable reason. The adversarial completion pass runs at harm grain with the charge
inverted the same way as always: not "does this envelope look right" but **"name a harm this
transition's action classes could produce that is not in the vector"** — and generator 3 makes that
challenge cheap to run mechanically first (any action class whose type-signature contributes a dimension
absent from the unit's vector is an automatic finding before a human even looks). After open, the
dimension set is immutable for the unit: a mid-flight agent cannot narrow what is being measured,
because the measuring is done by the gateway's ledger against the manifest's dimensions, from typed
observations — the agent's own residue self-report is inadmissible, which is our self-report law doing
its normal job one level up.

**3.3 The composition rules are ratified with the model, not chosen by the unit.** How dimensions
accumulate (monotone counters vs high-water marks vs decaying-with-recovery), which are incomparable,
and what "within the envelope" means per dimension are properties of the harm model at its version —
Rule-24-tested like every schema, mutant-challenged like every compiler (delete a known dimension from a
generator and the closure certificate must report the loss, not produce a cleaner model). A unit pins
`harm_model@version` beside its policy epoch and knowledge basis — the third stamp — and a harm-model
version bump is knowledge-basis-like for open units: intersecting units re-evaluate at their next
checkpoint rather than continuing on a model the estate no longer believes.

**3.4 The residual, honestly, and the live specimen.** An unmodeled harm dimension remains open-world
residue accepted by the opening authority as UNMODELED — and the specimen-ratchet closes it over time:
every postmortem that finds a harm outside the model is a naming-act trigger, which is how the incident
generator got rich in the first place. And a small live specimen from this afternoon of the discipline
working at survey grain: a path migration in our estate (a frozen census substrate moving to a
governed home) shipped with its blast-radius dimension *generated* — the full reader set of the old
