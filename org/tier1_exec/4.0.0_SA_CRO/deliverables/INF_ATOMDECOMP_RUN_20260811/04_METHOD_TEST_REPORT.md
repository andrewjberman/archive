## VERDICT IN ONE PARAGRAPH

boundaries while every stage reads as designed in isolation — was borne out FOUR separate times in
one night (guard-vs-architecture, Sunday-rebuild-vs-universe, pull-mode-vs-merge-base,
description-vs-implementation on the two-flow rule). The evidence-class discipline (SOURCE vs
DEPLOYED vs RUNTIME vs DATA) caught the run's would-have-been-wrong turn: the inherited "rules
disabled under the hold" mechanism died the moment DEPLOYED (trigger env) was read instead of the
stale record — the exact map-vs-territory failure the method's Rule 3 exists to prevent. The
scheduling-axes pack (coined seven hours earlier) paid for itself: A1 (write cardinality) asked
"how many times is one date-key written and by what," which is the question that found the Sunday
full-pull replace. Caveat 1: the method did NOT find the root by itself — three successive
hypotheses (allowlist, roster flags, vendor closure processing) were REFUTED by measurement before
the fourth held; what the method contributed was the discipline that each refutation was cheap,
clean, and non-poisoning (memberships-not-totals, Rule 10). Caveat 2: the full Rule-14 SSOT
rectangle + fan-out was NOT exercised (adapted to sequential single-seat passes; fabric bans the
subagent path) — so this run validates the method's INVESTIGATIVE spine, not its multi-agent
certification machinery.
