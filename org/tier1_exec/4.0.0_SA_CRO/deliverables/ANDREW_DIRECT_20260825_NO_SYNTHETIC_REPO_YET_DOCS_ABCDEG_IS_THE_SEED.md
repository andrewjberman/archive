## THE REMEDY, AND ITS VERIFICATION CONTRACT

**DERIVE THE SEED; DO NOT FILTER THE SOURCE.** Generate a clean specification artifact *from* these
files rather than excluding parts *of* them — the estate's own no-shadow ruling in its constructive
form. A filter asks *what do I exclude* and must find a grain; **a derivation asks *what must the
seed assert*, emits exactly that, and never meets the boundary.**

**A rewrite cannot be diffed — and is still verifiable.** `~E` proved it on a real restore hours
earlier: 7,535 deleted bytes including a schema block, restored from two partial sources, **no
comparison source byte-identical**, verified anyway because the surviving prose stated what the
block had to *do* (three admit/reject verdicts, all three reproduced, never a diffed byte).

> **The evidence stays in the working record, the constraint goes to the seed, and the check is a
> ROUND TRIP — does the retained evidence still support the emitted constraint?**

**And the reframe that dissolves the problem: the constraint and its evidence were never two halves
of one object to be divided. They are a CLAIM and its WARRANT.** Sending the claim to the seed and
keeping the warrant in the record is the ordinary relationship between a specification and the work
that produced it. An hour was spent hunting a grain at which to cut one object in half; it was two
objects.

### Where the mechanism chain bottomed out

| Refinement | Residual found, by whom |
|---|---|
| `MENTION` the warrant | **Catches INVENTION, misses INVERSION** (`~C` mutant, run on its own register): an emitted constraint stating the *inverse* of its evidence PASSES, because the words match. | `CITE` the warrant | **MIS-AIM** (`~C`): a row cited `Q-C4` where the ruling closed `Q-C5`. One digit, every target existed, nothing dangled, no check failed. |

Both remedies are **formatting rules, not mechanisms** — deliberately. Three fixes introduced
defects that night; a validator would have been the fourth and would need its own warrant.
