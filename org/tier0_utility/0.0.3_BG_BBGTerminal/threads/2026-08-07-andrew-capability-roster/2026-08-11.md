### A false alarm I caught before sending it
My first content script printed `max|ret| = 68.16%` and I nearly reported an anomaly. The
`return` column is in **PERCENT** units and my `:.4%` format double-scaled it. Grounded it by
**re-deriving the return from `price` alone** -- median(column / pct_change*100) = **0.999983**,
and the price-derived max move of 0.6816% depends on the column's units not at all.
**The lesson is the one that generalises: the reading that settles a units question is the one
that does not use the suspect column.** Had I sent the first number, Andrew would have chased a
market anomaly that did not exist, on data that is in fact correct -- the false-alarm harm
CLAUDE.md names, manufactured by my own instrument rather than by the pipeline.
